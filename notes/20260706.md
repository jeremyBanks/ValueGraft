## Research framing and terminology

Prior-art review established that hosted providers already expose compaction or
opaque-state interfaces, while recent literature demonstrates editable,
composable, and compressible KV state. The defensible novelty claim is therefore
the specific question of whether preserving or reconstructing write-time
attention state at a summary-plus-tail compaction boundary reduces behavioral
damage.

The public taxonomy was reset around independent key and value policies:

```text
K(αK) = (1 − αK) Kfresh + αK Kwrite-time, rerotated
V(αV) = (1 − αV) Vfresh + αV Vwrite-time
```

Plain compaction is `(0,0)`; current `E` variants are V-only grafts with `αK=0`;
K-only and coupled KV-Graft comparisons remain future work. Historical packed
arms must remain auxiliary because they changed layout and tail retention as
well as state source. Existing internal identifiers and result directories
remain frozen for provenance.

The primary method remains one global, holdout-validated α per model:
approximately 0.25 at 4B and 0.75 at 30B. Per-layer tuning is an exploratory
calibration interface; the 57-slot mask is demoted after failing its
wrong-conversation contamination guard. Signed coefficients are geometrically
plausible but must remain bounded and regularized exploratory fits: the 4B
holdout favored clamped factored fitting over signed fitting, without resolving
the question at 30B.

## Evidence and methodological boundaries

The evidence hierarchy was made explicit. Small 4-bit or local results can
generate hypotheses and demonstrate possibility, but null results do not rule
out effects at 30B bf16 or on other architectures. Standard claims require
clean, appropriately powered data, with cross-precision comparisons labeled.

The strongest unaffected results remain:

- Text compaction produces severe damage on context-dependent continuation and
  benchmark probes, including the 30B Stage-1 aggregate falling from 52.5%
  full-context performance to roughly 4.1% after compaction. The QA framing
  showed little separation among mitigation arms, so this is primarily a damage
  measurement.
- On 75 clean SWE-Gym/OpenHands traces, tuned V-only grafting improved true
  next-action likelihood by `+0.0156` nats/token, winning 45/75 paired cases and
  closing roughly 10% of the full-context/compacted gap. This is an offline
  trajectory result, not end-to-end task success.
- Historical packed write-time-KV experiments reduced fabrication on unknowable
  questions at 30B bf16 from 83% for production compaction to 17% for the
  write-time-KV arm, while improving evicted-fact accuracy. This supports an
  honesty/behavioral effect, but the packed layout and summary-only context
  prevent treating it as a clean current V-only or K-versus-KV result.
- Controlled micro-experiments showed content-dependent sense shifts and
  negative controls, supporting a mechanism-level effect without establishing
  that cached values recover arbitrary facts.

Illustrations and one-off demonstrations remain quarantined from results and
evidence lists. The repository’s synthesis workspace is derivative analysis
only, not an experimental source.

## Coding-agent program and data hygiene

The original live OpenHands matrix was demoted to exploratory evidence after
cross-arm session leakage, duplicate/shared run directories, broken probes,
stale endpoint/configuration behavior, cache-growth deaths, and false score
files were identified. Contaminated and invalid rows were quarantined; trusted
rows require completion evidence, score provenance, configuration metadata, and
the absence of infrastructure-invalid markers. The pipeline now emphasizes
source-level invalidation, per-task cache generations, health gating, atomic
completion writes, and explicit separation of synthetic, standard, humane, and
legacy strata.

The production-shaped humane SWE-bench track failed its capability gate: plain
SWE-bench reached 0/17 trusted passes and oracle-retrieval validation reached
0/7. These are boundary and solvability evidence, not intervention comparisons.
The task program therefore pivoted to capability-gated chained exercises, while
retaining synthetic tasks as mechanism-oriented proxies. The latest small chain
slice showed A `4/4`, B `1/1`, and E:a1.0 `1/2`, with additional rows still
incomplete; this is far too small for an arm conclusion. Earlier synthetic
positives remain suggestive but used a brief, non-production summary and must
not headline the paper.

A forensic Flask run illustrated the failure mode: repeated loss of file-path
and verification state after compaction, followed by premature completion
without running tests. The wrong patch also reflected a capability/thoroughness
gap, since the hidden host-matching behavior was never inspected. This supports
treating task success, verification behavior, and compaction sensitivity as
distinct outcomes.

## Operational and infrastructure clarifications

The audit found that no-cache mode gives the full-context arm an asymmetric
quadratic-prefill cost under a common wall-clock timeout, potentially truncating
A more often for computational rather than scientific reasons. Configuration
provenance was also insufficient: summary variant, tail length, and
incremental-cache state must be recorded in per-response debug data and startup
banners. SWE-bench test-ID cleaning can silently drop valid test IDs, especially
bare SymPy names, and requires correction before standard-task claims resume.

Incremental KV caching must be validated with realistic VRAM arithmetic, not
only tiny CPU equivalence tests. It should be bounded, admission-controlled, and
allowed to fall back to fresh serving rather than kill a process. Mature serving
improvements—flash/compiled attention, asynchronous serving, and a validated
cache hot path—are future infrastructure priorities, not evidence-producing
changes during an active comparison.

## Open questions and next actions

- Complete the chain-task arm table under the corrected scaffold and determine
  whether the model can produce a measurable, compaction-sensitive signal.
- Keep synthetic, chain, historical packed, humane SWE-bench, and standard
  benchmark results stratified; do not pool them into a headline pass rate.
- Resolve whether native tool calling and sampling configuration artificially
  depressed Qwen performance before interpreting capability-floor results; use a
  small controlled scaffold A/B first.
- Pilot `tau2-bench` `banking_knowledge` only after the chain gate: verify token
  lengths exceed the compaction threshold, reward determinism, and
  user-simulator cost. Core tau domains are planned negative controls because
  their policy remains in the unevictable system prompt.
- Preserve the sealed final evaluation until a champion is stable and all
  configuration/provenance audits pass.
- Complete the matched `(αK, αV)` experiments if the project proceeds beyond the
  current V-only evidence, with identical summary text, tail, layout, positions,
  prompt, and decoding across arms.
- Treat the layer-tuned candidate and all live-agent positives as provisional
  until fresh, isolated, adequately powered confirmation is available.
