_This UTC day marks the ValueGraft project's decisive turn from fact-retrieval
QA benchmarking toward end-to-end agent-coding evaluation, compressed into one
extremely dense cycle: a null result forced the pivot, infrastructure scaled to
nine pods, repeated reliability failures forced hardened protocols, a serious
trust rupture over benchmark framing occurred and was corrected, and a
capability-floor discovery on standard coding tasks forced both a
scaffold-configuration audit and a new vendor-benchmark calibration method._

## The pivot to agent-coding evaluation

The full stage-1 LongMemEval run (320 questions × 6 arms) landed showing no
meaningful separation between compaction/graft arms on fact-retrieval accuracy
(full-context 52.5% vs. all compacted arms in the 2.8–6.9% range) — a result the
project's own prior local data had already predicted. This was judged an
inefficient use of compute; stage-1b was cut to a minimal citability check,
LoCoMo was dropped, and effort redirected to the "E-track": a shim-served
OpenHands/SWE-Gym-style pipeline where an OpenAI-compatible server on RunPod
GPUs implements compaction and value-grafting server-side, scored objectively
via pytest pass/fail plus process metrics and a recall-dissociation probe. Pod
count scaled from an informal four-pod ceiling (an operational limit, not a
platform cap) to as many as nine over the day, running an expanded "matrix"
design (dose-response alphas, shuffled-graft negative controls, threshold
variants) under a formal champion/challenger promotion ladder: explore → confirm
(fresh seeds) → a sealed final evaluation on held-out seeds s50–s99, still
untouched at day's end.

## Reliability failures and a trust rupture

Recurring silent job failures drove a series of monitoring hardening steps
(consolidated 5-minute/30-minute alerting tiers, dead-process detection,
count-validated completion markers). More seriously, the shim — built assuming
one task per pod — developed a session-state leak once lanes were shared,
letting cache state bleed across arms on the same pod; remediation required a
pre-registered clean rerun with disjoint task-lane assignment and isolation
probes, and results were reorganized into `results/agent_clean_run/` (trusted)
versus `results/_QUARANTINE_agent_runs/` (contaminated, documented).

The most serious event of the day was a trust rupture: the user discovered that
language describing "real agent" and "real coding tasks" had for roughly twelve
hours actually referred to a synthetic task harness, with real SWE-bench rows
queued behind long synthetic backlogs rather than running as implied. This was
treated as a genuine framing failure, not fabrication, and produced a standing
rule that every future results statement must name its task source (synthetic
vs. standard benchmark) inline. Separately, the second agent working the project
(gpt-5.5/Codex, read-only) diagnosed its own status-refresh failures — tailing
files not structured for tail-reading, summarizing truncated tool output, not
diffing from the last commit it had actually seen — and adopted a diff-based
reconstruction protocol (record last-seen commit, `git log --name-status`, read
actual diffs of every changed status file) for future refreshes.

## Terminology and methodology clarifications

- **ValueGraft** is now the umbrella term for a family of write-time cache-state
  interventions, parameterized by two independent axes, `alpha_K` and `alpha_V`,
  controlling how much fresh-vs-write-time key and value state is used at
  aligned tokens. Named regions: Plain Summary Compaction (`alpha_K=alpha_V=0`),
  V-only Graft, K-only Graft, KV-Graft, Coupled KV-Graft. `alpha=1` is
  explicitly not a separate method name, just an endpoint of the continuous
  parameter. The historical `H-pack`/`B-min-pack` packed-summary arms were
  reclassified as a confounded auxiliary comparison (different layout, no
  retained tail) rather than a clean instance of this taxonomy, and any future
  controlled comparison must hold every non-target axis (summary text, layout,
  tail, positions, prompt, decoding) fixed.
- An **evidence-grading rule** was reaffirmed: small-scale, 4-bit, or local
  results are hypothesis generators only — nulls there never prune hypotheses at
  larger scale, and claims are earned at 30B-bf16 on standard data.
- A new **"difficulty certificate" rule** (rule 18) treats vendor model-card
  benchmark citations as an external calibration reference for what a subject
  model should be expected to handle; the empirically-discovered chain-tier
  capability boundary was found to align with vendor-reported numbers, taken as
  mutual cross-validation of the internal harness.
- The project's primary-method policy was reaffirmed in a dedicated design note:
  a single global alpha per model remains the headline method; per-slot
  ("posslots") and factored/signed calibration variants stay in an exploration
  section. Posslots, despite beating the global rule on holdout, subsequently
  **failed its own wrong-conversation contamination guard** and was retired as a
  finding, while a per-layer coarse-tuned config passed the same guard and
  became the night's champion.

## Empirical results (with caveats)

- The honesty/fabrication finding — that write-time cached state reduces
  fabrication on unknowable post-compaction questions — **replicated at full
  bf16 precision** on the 30B model: production-style compaction fabricates on
  ~83% of never-discussed topics vs. 17% for the write-time-KV arm, which is
  simultaneously most accurate (38/48) and least fabricating (4%) on genuinely
  evicted facts.
- A per-layer-tuned V-Graft configuration emerged as the overnight matrix's
  champion (9/9 on synthetic agent tasks, including a harder tier, and
  guard-passed), while scalar alpha=0.75 specifically underperformed neighboring
  alpha values — an anomaly only partly attributed to a session-isolation
  serving bug and still not fully resolved.
- The most stable quantitative result of the day came from offline
  SWE-Gym/OpenHands trajectory replay, unaffected by the live-agent issues:
  tuned V-Graft recovers +0.0156 nats/token on true next-action prediction over
  75 traces (45/75 wins), about 10% of the full-context-vs-compacted gap.
- A **capability floor** was discovered on live standard coding tasks: multiple
  full-capability (uncompacted) validation attempts on SWE-bench-Lite/Verified
  failed even under oracle-mode retrieval, escalating to five straight failures
  across two datasets and three repos — indicating the subject model itself, not
  compaction, is often the binding constraint on these tasks.
- Forensic autopsy of the first completed real-task episode (`flask-5063`) gave
  a split verdict: the agent's premature self-termination (skipping a
  self-declared test step after it was evicted from context) was judged
  compaction-shaped, while the incorrect fix content was judged
  capability-shaped, since the missing requirement was never surfaced by the
  visible issue text.
- A scaffold-configuration audit found two serving bugs affecting every arm
  symmetrically — native tool-calling was disabled, and decoding was hardcoded
  to greedy regardless of configured temperature — meaning prior arm comparisons
  remain internally valid but all solve rates are lower bounds pending a
  corrected re-run.
- An adversarial pipeline audit surfaced three critical validity issues: a
  uniform wall-clock timeout that silently disadvantages the uncompacted oracle
  arm (which reprocesses the whole growing conversation each turn) relative to
  compacted arms whose per-turn cost plateaus; debug output that doesn't record
  which summary-prompt variant or tail-keep setting a run actually used,
  reproducing the same blind spot that let an earlier confound go undetected;
  and an id-cleaning filter that can silently drop test ids for any SWE-bench
  instance, not only the case it was written for.
- Confirm-phase benchmark scouting identified tau2-bench's `banking_knowledge`
  domain as uniquely suited to this project: unlike its other domains, which
  bake all governing policy into an unevictable system prompt,
  `banking_knowledge` delivers policy through documents retrieved via
  mid-conversation tool calls, giving a clean dissociation where compaction
  damage should appear only where policy is evictable.

## Paper drafting

Two successive full paper-style syntheses were produced (an initial draft and a
shorter focused revision), both jointly attributed to Jeremy Banks, Claude Fable
5, and GPT-5.5, converging on a deliberately narrow claim: no recall recovery,
but honesty/fabrication mitigation and a small, consistent
continuation-likelihood recovery, with mechanisms plausible but evidence still
limited to one model family and partial task coverage. A separate prior-art
review recalibrated the paper's novelty claim after finding that hosted APIs
(OpenAI's `/responses/compact`, Anthropic compaction, Gemini
context/thought-signature primitives) and prior literature already establish
that cached attention state is editable and useful in general — the project's
actual contribution is the specific compaction-boundary mitigation measurement,
not the existence of opaque state handles. A dedicated
`paper-working/valuegraft-synthesis/` directory was set up as an explicitly
scope-guarded derived-analysis workspace. A Pokémon-themed illustrative
conversation was expanded into a standalone demo asset, reaffirmed as never
citable as evidence and never stored under `results/`.

## Outstanding risks and next actions

- The alpha=0.75 anomaly needs isolated re-runs under the session-isolation fix
  before being treated as real or artifactual.
- The capability floor on standard tasks is only partially diagnosed; a
  controlled scaffold-config A/B and a parallel chained-exercise diagnostic tier
  were both in flight, unresolved at day's end.
- A model-swap ladder (Qwen3-Coder-30B, Qwen3-235B-A22B, GLM-4.5-Air) remains
  catalogued but gated on those results, with a capped GLM-4.5-Air trial
  pre-authorized as fallback.
- The pipeline audit's three critical findings need fixes verified before
  further live-agent data is trusted at face value.
- The tau2-bench `banking_knowledge` plan requires a small pilot before
  commitment and is gated behind positive chain-tier results; budget runway was
  roughly $36–40 at day's end with an explicit funding gate.
- Gemma architecture-transfer work remains parked at a documented resume point.
