_The project’s central claim was narrowed and clarified: ValueGraft is a
mitigation study for compaction-induced behavioral damage, not a claim that
KV-state preservation or opaque compaction handles are novel in general. Work
also shifted toward audited, capability-gated coding-agent evaluation after
contaminated live comparisons and an infeasible SWE-bench track._

**Participants/contributors:** User; claude-fable-5; claude-sonnet-5;
gpt-5.5-xhigh.

## Main developments

- Prior-art review found close public analogues in hosted APIs: OpenAI exposes
  compaction items and opaque continuation state; Anthropic and Gemini expose
  related compaction, caching, or reasoning-state primitives. Their internal
  mechanisms are undocumented. Relevant literature already establishes editable,
  composable, reusable, and compressible KV state. The defensible novelty is
  therefore the controlled question of whether preserving or reconstructing
  write-time state at a summary boundary reduces behavioral damage.

- The experimental vocabulary was reset around separate key and value policies:

  ```text
  K(αK) = (1 − αK) Kfresh + αK Kwrite-time, rerotated
  V(αV) = (1 − αV) Vfresh + αV Vwrite-time
  ```

  Current `E` arms are V-only Graft experiments (`αK=0`); `B` is plain summary
  compaction. Historical packed `H-pack`/`B-min-pack` results vary both layout
  and K/V source and remain auxiliary evidence, not a clean key-versus-value
  comparison. Existing code and result identifiers remain frozen for provenance.

- The primary method remains a simple global value blend per model:
  approximately `αV=0.25` at 4B and `αV=0.75` at 30B. Per-layer tuning is a
  candidate practical extension; slot-mask tuning failed its wrong-conversation
  contamination guard and is demoted to exploratory evidence. Signed
  coefficients remain geometrically plausible but must be bounded and
  regularized; the 4B holdout favored clamped factored fitting over signed
  fitting, without closing the question at 30B.

- The evidence hierarchy was formalized. Small or 4-bit results generate
  hypotheses and cannot rule out effects at larger scale. Primary claims require
  30B bf16 validation on standard or production-shaped data. Recall, honesty,
  and continuity are separate outcomes: current evidence supports honesty and
  continuity effects, not recovery of facts whose evidence was evicted.

- Established results include severe text-compaction damage on controlled and
  standard material, a +0.0156 nat/token next-action gain on 75 clean offline
  coding traces, and a 30B bf16 honesty replication in which plain compaction
  fabricated on 20/24 decoy questions versus 4/24 for the historical
  write-time-KV packed arm. The latter is auxiliary summary-layout evidence, not
  proof of the current V-only coding method. No result yet demonstrates reliable
  end-to-end live-agent improvement.

- The live coding program underwent a major validity reset. Cross-arm session
  leakage, duplicate runs, dead-shim artifacts, broken probes, configuration
  ambiguity, and cache-memory failures were quarantined or fixed. Trusted rows
  require completion evidence plus a score; invalid episodes receive no score.
  Legacy synthetic results remain mechanism-oriented support, while
  production-faithful humane and standard-task rows must be reported separately.

- SWE-bench validation was effectively retired for this model after repeated
  full-context failures and an apparent capability floor. The active fallback is
  capability-gated chained exercises, with Original-arm solvability checked
  before constrained-arm comparisons. Vendor difficulty tables are now the first
  calibration certificate for future task sources. `tau2-bench`’s
  `banking_knowledge` domain is the strongest future standard candidate because
  relevant policy enters through evictable retrieval/tool turns, while core tau
  domains provide a negative control with policy in the system prompt.

## Methodological clarifications to preserve

- Keep layout, summary text, retained tail, token positions, prompt shape,
  decoding, and provenance fixed when isolating K or V policy.

- Write-time keys moved into a packed layout require RoPE re-rotation; values do
  not. Exact literal token alignment is used in implemented V-Graft arms.

- Treat summary packing and write-time state as distinct axes. Do not call
  `H-pack` a V-Graft or present it as a clean K-only/KV comparison.

- Report task source, model precision, configuration, compaction threshold, tail
  size, summary variant, cache generation, and timeout status in every result
  artifact. Process liveness is not evidence of useful progress.

- Baselines require the same auditing as interventions. Fixed wall-clock limits
  can disadvantage the full-context arm when incremental caching is disabled, so
  timeout and prefill-cost asymmetries must be measured before interpreting
  real-task pass rates.

- One-off demonstrations and illustrative material remain quarantined from
  results and evidence lists. Controlled sense-level experiments, not
  demonstrations, provide mechanism evidence.

## Outstanding risks and next actions

- The decisive question—whether V-only Graft improves genuine coding-agent
  outcomes under real compaction pressure—remains unresolved. Complete the
  chain-task capability gate and obtain a clean paired comparison before
  additional expensive expansion.

- Verify that all active shims use current code and record their actual summary,
  tail, cache, and timeout configuration. Reconcile stale logs and ensure no
  score is accepted without `score.json`, completion markers, and provenance.

- Fix or explicitly handle the SWE-bench ID-cleaning issue before any future
  reuse; the current filter can silently drop valid test identifiers, including
  all bare SymPy identifiers.

- Repair the recall/dissociation probe and normalize synthetic versus standard
  diagnostic scoring. Do not interpret the earlier zero-recall capture as a
  scientific null.

- Pilot `tau2-bench banking_knowledge` only after confirming token lengths
  exceed the compaction threshold, deterministic rewards, and manageable
  user-simulator cost. Keep core tau domains as the planned negative control.

- Preserve sealed seeds and sight-unseen templates until the champion
  configuration is stable. Any future K-only or coupled KV-Graft study should
  use a matched production-shaped context and pre-registered controls.
