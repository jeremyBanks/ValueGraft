_The 2026-07-10 UTC work narrowed value grafting to a provenance-conscious
bounding result: broad conversation-recovery benefits remain null, and the
initial brief SWE-Gym proxy positive failed independent replication. In-domain
layer selection produced a small coding-task signal on its selection-adjacent
pool, but the final evidence does not support a general technique claim._

**Participants/contributors:** User; claude-opus-4-8; claude-sonnet-5;
gpt-5.5-xhigh; Fable, a labeled assessment/subagent represented by
claude-opus-4-8. Authors are not separately identified in the source notes.

The main empirical picture is now:

- Held-out per-head, per-layer, intersection, and union champion families did
  not beat baseline on conversation recovery. The synthetic compression sweep
  was flat to slightly negative from ultra through realistic summaries, refuting
  the hypothesis that recovery gains concentrate only under aggressive
  compression.
- Production-faithful SWE-Gym summaries were null, while the original
  brief-summary scalar result was small and fragile: approximately +0.013–+0.016
  in a teacher-forced next-action log-probability proxy. The free discrete
  action-match check pointed in the same direction but was underpowered and did
  not establish task success.
- The independent disjoint SWE-Gym confirmation overturned that apparent scalar
  effect: scalar α=0.75 was approximately −0.0035 with a confidence interval
  spanning zero, and no tested α improved reliably over baseline. The discrete
  action-match increase also had a confidence interval touching zero.
- In-domain selection of a 12-layer middle-region champion produced
  approximately +0.011–+0.012 versus baseline on the tune/evaluation pool and
  beat the naive scalar arm. This result is useful as a bounded observation, but
  remains vulnerable to pool/regime and selection effects; it should not be
  treated as independent confirmation of broad graft efficacy.
- Content-specificity was observable in slot-heavy configurations but was
  non-additive: matching content or regions did not translate into net recovery
  improvement. The measured outcomes are proxy metrics from one bf16
  Qwen3-30B-A3B setup, not end-to-end task success.

Methodological clarifications to preserve are that scalar and champion
interventions must be reported separately; all conditions require explicit
pod-manifest declarations, born annotations, held-out or disjoint splits,
placebo controls where applicable, bf16 verification, silent-default/forwarding
checks, measured compression ratios, and provenance records. Repeated
measurements on the same trajectories denoise estimates but do not increase
independent sample size. The discrete next-action metric is behavioral but
generation-parsed and should remain secondary to direct task-success measures.

The follow-up also exposed an important operational failure: a filename
collision erased the prod-champion arm and five brief-champion trajectories
before the cutoff. The paper documents the missing data and excludes the absent
arm rather than inferring its result. Future runs must use unique per-run
directories and immediate incremental harvesting.

agent’s responsibilities, so the remaining workflow should not reintroduce it.

Open questions are limited and should be treated as optional future work:
whether an independently selected in-domain champion can replicate across
genuinely new trajectory pools, whether direct task-success evaluation can
replace the log-probability proxy, and whether an own-summary mechanism
experiment would clarify the observed content/state dependence. Further compute
should be justified only by these targeted questions; the current evidence
supports narrow null or bounding conclusions, not a coined reusable method.
