_The project converged on a narrow, mostly-null result: value grafting showed a
small brief-summary proxy effect in the original SWE-Gym pool, but the effect
failed independent replication. The paper and repository were finalized around
this evidence-bounded conclusion, with provenance and notes-hygiene issues
documented._

**Participants/contributors:** User; claude-opus-4-8; claude-sonnet-5;
gpt-5.5-xhigh.

## Research findings

- Held-out conversation recovery was null across per-layer, per-head,
  intersection, and union champion families. The compression sweep was flat to
  slightly negative from ultra through realistic summaries, refuting the
  hypothesis that aggressive compression concentrates a general recovery
  benefit.
- The original brief-SWE-Gym scalar result was positive—approximately `+0.013`
  to `+0.016` teacher-forced next-action log-probability nats/token—but remained
  a proxy rather than task success and was based on a limited trajectory pool.
- Independent disjoint SWE-Gym trajectories overturned that result: scalar
  `α=0.75` was approximately `−0.0035` with a confidence interval spanning zero,
  and no tested α improved reliably over baseline. The discrete action-match
  metric moved in the favorable direction but its interval also touched zero.
- In-domain layer tuning briefly produced a positive held-out champion result
  within the later 98-trajectory pool, but this did not establish generality.
  The final interpretation treats it as pool- or regime-sensitive rather than a
  confirmed transferable technique.
- Content-specific value concentration was observed in slot-heavy
  configurations, but it did not yield net recovery improvement. The graft
  therefore appears non-additive or otherwise insufficient for broad downstream
  benefit under the tested conditions.

## Durable methodological clarifications

- Claims must distinguish scalar grafts from per-layer champions and must remain
  tied to the tested model, task, summary regime, metric, and held-out split.
- The primary metric is a teacher-forced log-probability proxy; discrete action
  matching is a useful but underpowered behavioral supplement, not task-success
  measurement.
- Valid runs require explicit manifest conditions, born annotations, held-out
  evaluation, placebo controls where applicable, bf16 confirmation, alignment
  checks, compression-ratio reporting, and detection of silent
  environment-variable forwarding or defaulting failures.
- Repeated measurements on the same trajectories reduce measurement noise but do
  not increase independent sample size.
- A camel-case method name should not be introduced unless later evidence
  establishes a genuinely reusable technique; ordinary descriptive terminology
  is preferred.

## Provenance and repository state

A filename collision caused loss of the persisted production-champion arm and
five brief-champion trajectories. The loss occurred before the budget cutoff,
not during final harvesting; surviving scalar arms, four-way comparisons,
compression results, and most brief-champion data remain usable. Earlier claims
that no data were lost must not be repeated.

The paper was finalized as a science-first empirical bounding result with a
postmortem, promoted to `README.md`, and pushed to `origin/trunk`; the draft was
moved into `notes/`. A separate speculative pedagogical note was produced, while
speculation about non-additive content-specific values remains outside the main
paper.
