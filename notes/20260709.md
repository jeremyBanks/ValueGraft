## Research: reproduction collapse and the pivot to a bounding paper

A disciplined reproduction check — re-rendering the same twelve reference
conversations under clean current code — collapsed the judged-sense headline
from +12pp to +1.0pp (CI spanning zero). A same-judge disentangle isolated the
cause: judge recalibration explained only part of the drop (+12.0→+8.7pp); the
render itself explained the rest (+8.7→+1.0pp), matching the earlier collapse of
the referent-logprob anchor. Both of the project's positive meaning-recovery
results are now established as render-fragile at n=12 under MoE hardware
nondeterminism, where render-to-render noise is comparable in size to the
claimed effect. This was recorded as the most consequential finding of the day.

A subsequent deep provenance audit (invoked after the user objected that "we
never reproduced our core results at 16-bit") found the situation was worse than
the reproduction failure alone suggested: neither the recovery pipeline nor the
proposed honesty/anti-fabrication pipeline had ever been scored at bf16 — both
existed only as 4-bit local MLX runs, despite a draft briefly and incorrectly
labeling one as bf16. Separately, the honesty result's intervention arm
("H-pack") was confirmed to retain write-time keys as well as values in a
packed, tail-less layout — a materially different, coupled-KV intervention from
the paper's named value-only method — and a further check showed H-pack's
fabrication suppression is not even content-specific (a wrong-conversation
control suppressed fabrication just as effectively). A dedicated postmortem note
records this as a doubly disqualified cornerstone (wrong precision and wrong
arm) rather than a simple mislabeling.

Following that audit, a Fable consultation re-derived every load-bearing number
directly from disk and recommended abandoning the synthetic sense/referent probe
as the project's primary instrument, since it is null-to-harmful at bf16 and
disagrees with the one real-task result available. The only artifact confirmed
to be simultaneously bf16, value-only, and statistically sound and positive is a
SWE-Gym teacher-forced-logprob effect (+0.0156 nats, CI [+0.005, +0.027]
excluding zero, changing 49/75 greedy generations) — small, a proxy rather than
task success, and measured under an aggressively brief summary condition. A
placebo-controlled bf16 bounding test of the same value-only method on the
synthetic recovery plants (`effect_bound`) was null on two models, and
cross-architecture bf16 replications were flat-to-negative. The recommended
framing, adopted for the day's paper work, treats this bound plus the SWE-Gym
positive as the honest deliverable, with a candid process postmortem alongside
it rather than interleaved through the results.

A working paper draft ("a mostly-null bounding result") was written to this
framing and briefly archived in notes/, reporting the placebo-controlled null,
the one narrow SWE-Gym positive, and a content-specificity finding (grafting
correct values reliably avoids the harm a wrong graft causes, even though it
never beats the re-encoded summary). The owner then reviewed this draft and
judged it premature and internally inconsistent — written before the deciding
experiment had run — and directed that it be rewritten from scratch once a newly
designed champion-configuration validation experiment resolves, rather than
patched. Accompanying style guidance was recorded for that rewrite: drop
internal jargon (arm names, "H-pack") in favor of plain descriptions; stop
overusing "honest" as a hedge word; only coin a named technique ("ValueGraft")
if the final result is a real, reusable effect, otherwise use plain descriptive
language; do not oversell narrow, condition-specific findings; and aim for
enough methods detail that a reader could conceptually (not literally) reproduce
the experiment. Fable is to author most of the paper's prose directly, under
full framing latitude, reviewed by several single-purpose passes (adversarial,
proofreading, fresh-eyes readability) rather than one omnibus review.

## New experiments queued or designed

The owner reopened per-layer ("champion") tuning as an untested variable: a bf16
placebo-controlled null exists only for the flat default configuration, while a
separate tuned-config comparison showed the tuned configurations outperforming
baseline more consistently — but never under a placebo control, so it is unknown
whether the larger tuned effect is content-specific or just an amplified version
of the generic "any graft helps a little" artifact. A detailed,
placebo-controlled, per-quantization champion-validation experiment was designed
and partly implemented (4-bit first, bf16 second, each with its own tuning
pass), together with a follow-on per-head champion scan comparing per-head,
per-layer, intersection, and union configurations against held-out
conversations, and a compression-level sweep designed to test whether the
graft's effect concentrates specifically under aggressive/lossy compaction
(where the positive headlines lived) versus realistic-length summaries (where it
is currently null). None of these had produced results by day's end.

A separate provenance ledger effort re-audited existing on-disk artifacts
against the same standard, and found the SWE-Gym anchor's dtype, exact
intervention alpha, and summary-condition were all inferred from directory names
and code defaults rather than recorded in the result files themselves —
motivating new manifest-stamping instrumentation added to the harnesses going
forward.

## Methodological clarifications recorded for persistence

- The 2026-07-05 shift toward a mitigation-first framing was the owner's
  original intent, not a correction driven by external review; earlier summaries
  describing it as a "reframing" reflect agents' contemporaneous
  misunderstanding and should not be read as project history.
- Every serious Fable consultation should write its full output directly into a
  dedicated notes/ file rather than living only in agent-to-agent reply text,
  and should be given full trajectory context rather than a compressed summary.
- Before claiming a dataset answers a given question, every controlled variable
  (model, dtype/quantization, exact intervention, metric, condition, split) must
  be verified as proven from the data or runtime rather than inferred from a
  directory name — named as a systemic, repeated failure this session.
- A related root-cause pattern was named explicitly: a tendency to chase a fast
  apparent win rather than stopping to plan properly when a problem proves hard,
  cited as the underlying cause of the provenance-glossing failures.
- PushNotification is now a standing habit for major results and milestones (not
  routine progress).

## Notes-tooling developments

Substantial parallel work hardened the notes/conversation-archive pipeline: a
6-hour note-duration split policy, filtering of app-generated
compaction-scaffolding messages out of summarizer inputs, a git-blob-keyed date
cache to stabilize archive renaming, and a two-layer meta-summary system
(per-day summaries plus an overall `notes/README.md`) with a shared
sensitive-topic filter. Bulk archive renormalization and regeneration surfaced
and fixed two further bugs — a participant-block sync regex that could delete
note content, and an oscillating rename chain caused by unreliable git-history
dates, resolved by seeding the date cache from the conversation manifest
instead. A repo-local git identity change intended to attribute Codex commits
separately was reverted after it silently reattributed unrelated commits; future
Codex attribution should use per-command environment variables or commit
trailers instead. The scheduled heartbeat automation for periodic notes updates
missed its firing window at least twice during the day and had to be run
manually each time — confirmed unreliable and flagged for future agents to
verify rather than trust.

## Outstanding risks and next actions

The champion-configuration validation experiment (4-bit then bf16,
placebo-controlled) is the identified decisive test for whether a positive
result can still be salvaged; per-head and compression-sweep variants are
designed but unlaunched. The paper rewrite is explicitly blocked on that
experiment's outcome. The heartbeat-automation unreliability remains unresolved
as a process risk for unattended note maintenance. The provenance-ledger gaps
(missing manifests on already-collected data) mean some existing results can
only be cited with hedged, INFERRED-labeled caveats until reprocessed under the
new instrumentation.
