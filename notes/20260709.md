## Notes-archive tooling

The conversation-archive and meta-summary system reached a mature,
self-validating state. Work included: a 6-hour conversation-duration split
policy for note generation; structural filtering of Claude's `isCompactSummary`
continuation-notice messages and Codex's `compacted`/`replacement_history` rows
out of summarizer inputs (cutting summarizer-prompt size by several percent on
affected notes); a git-blob-keyed date cache so archive renaming and the
conversation-note updater stop re-deriving dates from unreliable git rename
history; and a bulk-commit rename mode for large renormalization batches. The
two-layer meta-summary system (`notes/YYYYMMDD.md` daily summaries plus an
overall `notes/README.md`) was built out with explicit head/tail excerpting
thresholds (ordinary notes: full to 8 KiB else 6+2 KiB; conversation notes: full
to 16 KiB else 12+4 KiB), a shared sensitive-topic forbid-regex filter
configurable via dotenv, and a `deno fmt`-before-hashing policy for generated
files.

Two structural bugs surfaced and were fixed during a live regeneration pass: a
participant-block sync regex that could delete note content, and rename-chain
oscillation caused by trusting git rename history for dates on multiply-renamed
files (fixed by seeding the date cache from the conversation manifest instead).
A repo-local git identity change (`.git/config`) intended to attribute commits
to a Codex identity instead silently reattributed all commits repo-wide; it was
caught quickly and reverted, with the durable lesson that Codex attribution
should use per-command author/committer env vars or commit trailers, never
repo-level config.

**Heartbeat automation is confirmed unreliable.** A scheduled four-hour
heartbeat missed its firing window by roughly two hours and had to be run
manually; it was later reset (not duplicated) and retargeted; a subsequent
firing found the working tree 21 commits ahead of `origin/trunk` from a
concurrent research-side stream (see below) plus an untracked result file
consistent with that work, and correctly deferred both its own commit and any
push decision rather than assuming it was safe to publish over unrelated
in-flight work. Future agents should treat the heartbeat as a trigger only and
verify actual execution via dry-run/validation output, not assume it ran.

## Research: precision/provenance crisis and reframing

The day's central development was a cascading correction to what the ValueGraft
project can honestly claim.

**Reproduction collapse.** An independent re-render of the same twelve reference
conversations under clean current code reduced the judged-sense recovery
headline from +12pp to +1.0pp (CI spanning zero). A same-judge disentangle
attributed most of the drop to render fragility under MoE hardware
nondeterminism (+12.0→+8.7 judge-calibration, +8.7→+1.0 render variance) rather
than judge artifact, matching an earlier referent-logprob failure pattern. Both
of the project's positive recovery headlines are now considered non-reproducing
at n=12.

**Fable review layer (two notes).** A holistic assessment flagged that both
marquee positive results had been measured only under a deliberately terse
"brief" summary condition designed to handicap the baseline, with no positive
result yet existing under a production-faithful summary — ranked as the
project's biggest unaddressed risk. A second, un-anchored assessment of the
"native rendering is required" premise found it only partly supported: the
self-generated-summary requirement is cleanly verified, but the native-reply
requirement rested on a cherry-picked low render draw, with a second on-disk
native draw reproducing the authored result — meaning render-to-render noise,
not nativeness, likely explains the apparent collapse.

**Precision/arm audit.** Direct owner challenges ("we never reproduced our core
results at 16-bit"; skepticism that the honesty arm represents the paper's named
method) triggered a full audit establishing that neither behavioral cornerstone
had ever been scored at bf16: both recovery and honesty pipelines ran only on
local 4-bit MLX. Separately, the honesty result's intervention arm ("H-pack")
was confirmed to retain write-time keys in a packed layout — a materially
different, coupled-KV intervention from the paper's named value-only ValueGraft
method. A wrong-summary control (H-pack built from a different conversation's
state) suppressed fabrication just as effectively as real H-pack, indicating the
honesty effect is driven by the packed layout/regime rather than by grafted
content specifically. A postmortem note records the root causes: a split-brain
runtime (local MLX 4-bit vs. pod HF bf16, no unifying harness) and an
arm-vocabulary reframing that let historical arm IDs drift from the current
canonical method, plus a lesson that effect size was treated as a proxy for
validity without checking precision or arm identity first.

**Correction on Fable's second pass.** Once fully briefed (including the owner's
explicit authorization to treat a negative/postmortem result as an acceptable
primary deliverable), a re-derivation of the SWE-Gym bf16 value-only result
found the prior audit had undersold it: properly bootstrapped, E−B = +0.0156
nats with CI [+0.005, +0.027] (excludes zero), changing the greedy generation on
49/75 tasks — a genuine, correctly-precision, correctly-armed positive, not
render-null as first assessed. This is currently the only bf16 + value-only
positive result on disk. A separate placebo-controlled bf16 value-only test
(`effect_bound`) remains a preregistered null on two models, and bf16
cross-architecture data on synthetic recovery plants is null-to-negative.

**Resulting paper posture.** A draft was restructured around: the
placebo-controlled bf16 bounding/null result as the honest spine, the SWE-Gym
bf16 positive as a small caveated supporting effect, the packed-KV admission
effect reported honestly as supporting-only (not the named method), and a
specific, transcript-grounded process postmortem — explicitly avoiding
motivational framing. This was written to `paper/DRAFT.md` and committed, not
yet pushed or promoted to README. A same-day bf16 rescoring of
previously-unscored honesty answers confirmed the packed-KV admission effect
replicates at full precision (decoy +62.5pp, evicted +20.8pp, both
CI-significant), reinforcing it as a real but supporting-only finding.

**Publishing boundary.** The owner authorized the agent to promote a
fully-reviewed paper to README and push to the repository autonomously once
confident, but drew a hard line that nothing leaves the repository externally
(no HF blog, no external posting) without direct owner involvement — recorded
durably in project protocol.

**New variable reopened at day's end.** The owner reopened the
champion/per-layer-tuned graft configuration (top16-layer α=1, position-tuned
"posslots" α=1) as an untested factor: these configs show larger bf16 effects
than the default global-α config but were never placebo-controlled, so it is
unknown whether the larger effect is genuine or an amplified version of the
already-characterized generic placebo effect. The owner directed a structured
validation along two precision tracks (bf16 and 4-bit, 4-bit prioritized first
as cheaper and parallelizable across warm pods), each requiring its own separate
per-layer tuning pass rather than reusing prior 4-bit results, with Fable
consulted only for minor refinement of this plan. A placeholder note for a
runnable experiment spec was created but not yet filled in; the
automation-triggered check late in the day found this work already in progress
by a concurrent agent (unpushed commits, a new bf16 honesty-verdicts result
file), with no further detail yet available.

## Environment/dependency retrospective

A read-only mining pass into repeated pod-provisioning failures (triggered by a
missing-`pandas` failure during an attempted production-faithful SWE-Gym run)
found a genuine, previously undocumented root cause: pod-side dependency
installation is copy-pasted and drifted across seven job-launch scripts with no
single source of truth, including a live contradiction where two scripts pin
`transformers==5.0.*` while a third avoids that exact pin because it breaks pod
weight loading, and only one of seven scripts installs `pandas`/`pyarrow`. The
proposed fix — a shared `pod_env.sh` sourced by every job plus a fail-closed
dependency preflight check — is recorded as a pending, not-yet-implemented
recommendation.

## Standing methodological/terminology clarifications

- **ValueGraft** = value-only intervention (α_K=0, tuned α_V) in the production
  summary+tail layout; **H-pack** = a distinct packed coupled-KV intervention
  (α_K=1, α_V=1, no conversation tail). These must never be conflated; a
  cornerstone claim requires verifying both precision (from on-disk model/dtype
  fields) and arm identity before use.
- "Brief" vs. "production/std" summary condition: brief is a deliberately terse
  condition designed to handicap the compacted baseline; no positive headline
  result yet exists under the production-faithful condition.
- Render-to-render variance under MoE nondeterminism can be comparable in
  magnitude to the effects being measured at n=12; single-draw comparisons
  across render conditions are no longer trusted without an estimated noise
  envelope.
- The 07-05 mitigation-first reframing was a correction of the agents' earlier
  misunderstanding of the owner's intent, not a change in the owner's goals —
  this should not be narrated in the paper or summaries as an
  initial-belief-then-corrected arc.
- "38/48 = 79% accurate" evicted-fact recall claim is retracted (true recall
  0/24); the corrected, verified claim is that packed write-time KV converts
  fabrication into admission, not that it restores recall.

  is the next concrete experiment and appears to already be underway via a
  concurrent agent stream as of day's end, with no results yet reflected in

  trigger notes maintenance; manual verification remains the fallback.
- Concurrent-agent coordination requires care before pushing: unpushed commits
  from other streams should be inspected, not assumed safe to bundle into a
  push.
