## Research developments

- Precision and intervention audits invalidated the proposed honesty cornerstone
  as a ValueGraft result. Its scored data were 4-bit MLX and used a packed
  coupled key/value intervention rather than the current value-only
  production-layout graft. The associated behavioral improvement was also
  content-agnostic and appears to reflect the packed layout. It remains
  supporting calibration evidence, not a cornerstone claim.

- The cleanest existing bf16 value-only recovery test (`effect_bound`) is null
  on both tested models: approximately +0.017 with CI [−0.033, +0.065] on
  Qwen3.6-27B and −0.049 with CI [−0.113, +0.014] on Qwen3-30B-A3B. The graft is
  distinguishable from corrupted-value placebos, establishing content-specific
  intervention effects, but not improvement over ordinary compaction.

- The bf16 value-only SWE-Gym result remains a modest positive: +0.0156
  teacher-forced next-action log-probability, CI approximately [+0.005, +0.027],
  with changed greedy outputs on 49/75 tasks. This is a proxy rather than task
  success, uses scalar α=0.75 rather than the tuned champion, and comes from an
  aggressively brief summary that disadvantages the baseline. A matched
  production-summary run was active but not yet complete.

- Held-out validation of the per-layer champion on the primary bf16
  Qwen3-30B-A3B model produced raw graft-versus-baseline −0.0026, CI [−0.041,
  +0.043]. The position-shuffle contrast was strongly positive (+0.1925, CI
  [+0.118, +0.266]), showing that aligned values outperform scrambled values,
  but this does not demonstrate a net gain over compaction. Remaining placebo
  modes were deferred after this decisive result.

- Per-head selection and intersection/union configurations were implemented with
  held-out, conversation-clustered placebo CIs. A compression sweep was also
  implemented to test whether effects concentrate under ultra-brief or brief
  summaries rather than realistic summaries. Both experiments were still running
  or queued.

- Earlier native-render results were reinterpreted as render fragility rather
  than evidence that native replies invalidate authored renders. Independent
  draws on the same conversations varied from positive to null, while the
  self-generated-summary dependence remained supported. Native rendering must
  not be treated as a proven validity requirement without a render-variance
  envelope.

## Decisions and methodological clarifications

- The project’s central framing is now determined by bf16, value-only,
  provenance-matched evidence. No result may answer a question unless model,
  precision, intervention, alpha/configuration, metric, summary condition,
  corpus split, and code provenance are verified rather than inferred from
  directory names or labels.

- Teacher-forced log-probability is a proxy, not behavioral success, resolution
  rate, or test-pass rate. “Content-specificity” and “net recovery over
  baseline” are separate quantities and must be reported separately.

- The primary champion criterion is held-out `E − placebo`, with the same
  selected slots, positions, and alpha in both arms and conversation-clustered
  bootstrap confidence intervals. A positive graft-versus-placebo result alone
  cannot establish improvement over compaction.

- The owner’s mitigation-oriented framing was present from the beginning.
  Earlier summaries that describe a project pivot away from a mechanism-first
  goal record agent misunderstanding, not a change in owner intent.

- Serious external review must receive full trajectory context and leave a
  durable notes-file record. Future papers should be written from the final
  audited evidence, with detailed conceptual methods, plain terminology, and
  distinct adversarial, statistical, proofreading, and fresh-eyes readability
  reviews. A coined technique name should be retained only if the final evidence
  establishes a reusable method.

- Compute work must checkpoint each conversation, validate resource headroom
  before launch, use idle capacity only for preemptible work advancing the
  highest-value goal, and stop promptly after data collection. Scheduled
  automation must be verified against repository state; missed runs require
  manual catch-up.

## Operational and repository state

The notes-summary system was expanded to sparse day → month → year → README
rollups. Validation passed with 41 focused tests, clean normalization and
meta-summary dry-runs, complete manifests, and no generated-rollup leakage. The
latest pushed state was current except for the intentionally untracked bf16
honesty-verdict artifact and unrelated result material.

A recurring pod-environment failure was documented: local MLX/4-bit Python 3.12
and pod HF/bf16 Python 3.11 are intrinsically separate, but pod dependencies
lack a canonical source of truth. Contradictory Transformers pins, incomplete
pandas/pyarrow installation, repeated torchvision workarounds, and unsafe torch
upgrades motivate one shared pod environment script plus fail-closed dependency
validation.
