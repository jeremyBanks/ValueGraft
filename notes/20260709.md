_The day opened with notes-infrastructure work (daily meta-summaries, a project
README overview, sensitive-topic filtering, and recovery from a git-identity
misconfiguration) but was dominated by a research inflection point: an
independent reproduction check collapsed both of the project's remaining
positive "recovery" headlines, a subsequent precision-and-arm audit found that
neither the recovery nor the honesty cornerstone had ever been validated at full
precision on the method the project is actually named after, and the project
moved from chasing a recovery headline toward an honest bounding-and-postmortem
framing, with a placebo-controlled champion-validation experiment queued as the
one remaining path to a legitimate positive result._

**Notes/tooling work.** Early in the day the archive tooling matured: a
two-layer meta-summary system (per-day summaries plus an overall
`notes/README.md`) was built and backfilled across all existing days, a shared
sensitive-topic filter was wired into every summary generator, and `deno fmt`
was made a mandatory pre-hash step. A repo-local git-identity change intended to
attribute commits to a Codex identity briefly reattributed all commits repo-wide
before being caught and reverted; going forward, Codex attribution should use
per-command author/committer variables, not repo-level config. Several
rename-chain and unstable-date bugs in the archive normalizer were fixed by
seeding file dates from the conversation manifest rather than git rename
history. The scheduled heartbeat automation intended to trigger periodic notes
updates was found to be unreliable — it missed its firing window on at least two
occasions — and manual invocation of the updater (with its dry-run/validation
checks) remains the working fallback.

**Reproduction collapse.** A disciplined re-check reproduced the judged-sense
recovery result on a fresh render of the original conversations under clean
current code: the previously reported +12pp collapsed to +1.0pp, with a
confidence interval spanning zero. Decomposing the drop showed judge-calibration
accounted for only a small part of it (+12.0→+8.7pp); the render itself
accounted for the rest (+8.7→+1.0pp), matching the render-fragility pattern
already seen in the referent-recovery metric. Both of the project's positive
recovery headlines are now understood to be render-fragile at the tested sample
size and non-reproducing under independent draws (MoE hardware nondeterminism),
rather than judge or measurement artifacts. In parallel, per-layer champion
scans were banked for three additional models (Qwen3-30B-A3B MoE, Qwen3-32B
dense, Mistral-Small-24B), and a claims-ledger discipline caught a separate
overclaim: an earlier "38/48 accurate" evicted-fact recall claim did not
reproduce (true recall was 0/24); the correct claim is that packed write-time KV
state converts fabrication into admission rather than restoring recall.

**Native-render premise re-examined.** A dedicated review split the "grafts
require native self-rendering" claim into two limbs: requiring the model's own
self-generated summary (verified, mechanistically clean) versus requiring
natively-rendered replies specifically (not well supported — two independent
native draws of the same conversations produced +0.116 nats, CI excluding zero,
and +0.012 nats, CI spanning zero, bracketing the "foreign" authored-reply value
of +0.147; render-to-render noise at this sample size is roughly the same
magnitude as the effect being tested). The recommendation was to keep presenting
native rendering as a defensible design choice rather than a proven requirement.

**Precision-and-arm audit.** A deep audit of on-disk provenance (model, dtype,
intervention identity) found that neither headline result had ever been scored
at bf16 on the project's actual value-only method: the recovery result was 4-bit
only (its bf16, placebo-controlled counterpart was null on two models), and the
honesty/anti-fabrication result — while robust and replicated across scale and,
once scored, precision — used a different intervention (packed retention of
write-time keys and values, not value-only grafting) and was shown to be
layout-driven rather than content-specific (a summary state from a different
conversation suppressed fabrication just as effectively as the matching one). A
same-day postmortem documented the root causes: a persistent split between the
local 4-bit MLX evaluation pipeline and the pod-based bf16 pipeline, and an
earlier arm-vocabulary reframing that let an older arm's identity drift from the
project's current canonical method without being re-verified before being used
as a headline.

**Revised standing/next-step assessment.** A second independent review corrected
part of the audit: recomputed properly, the one SWE-Gym result that is both bf16
and value-only is a real, modest positive (+0.0156 nats teacher-forced
log-probability, CI [+0.005, +0.027], changing 49 of 75 greedy generations), not
the render-null result it was first taken to be — though it remains a
log-probability proxy rather than a behavioral/task-success metric, and was
measured only under a deliberately terse "brief" summary condition. The
resulting recommendation, adopted by the owner: write the honest
bounding/negative result together with a candid methodological postmortem now
(this needs no further compute), stop investing further in the synthetic
sense/referent probe as the primary instrument since it is null-to-harmful at
bf16 and disagrees with the real-task result, and treat a production-faithful,
behaviorally-scored re-test of the SWE-Gym-style task as the only remaining
route to a positive headline.

**Terminology and framing decisions to persist.** The owner clarified that the
mitigation-first framing adopted around 2026-07-05 was the owner's intent from
the start, not a pivot driven by external review — the earlier "mechanism as
finding" framing was the agents' misunderstanding, corrected but not a
redirection, and future summaries should not narrate it as a reframing. For the
next paper draft (deferred until the champion-validation experiment resolves),
the owner set firm guidance: strip internal arm-naming jargon for external
readers; stop overusing "honest/honestly" as a rhetorical tic; do not coin a
persistent branded term like "ValueGraft" for a technique that doesn't robustly
work — use a plain, lowercase, glossary-defined phrase instead; keep every claim
scoped exactly to what was tested (bf16 Qwen3-30B-A3B, a log-probability proxy,
the aggressive-compaction regime) without overselling, while still reporting the
genuine small effect plainly; structure the paper as rigorous science first with
an elaborate postmortem at the end, not interleaved; and aim for enough methods
detail that a motivated reader could conceptually reconstruct the experiment.
Fable is to draft the paper itself with full framing latitude, iterated for
coherence, then passed through several distinct review lenses (adversarial,
proofreading, readability/flow) in addition to numeric verification.

**Follow-on work queued during the day (implementing the redirected plan).** A
placebo-controlled, per-quantization champion-validation experiment was designed
and wired into the harness: its decisive statistic is the paired,
conversation-clustered-CI contrast between the tuned champion graft and the same
champion applied with corrupted source values, run at 4-bit first (cheaper) and
then bf16, with the champion re-derived separately per quantization. A parallel
design compares per-head against per-layer (and combined) champion
configurations at bf16 on the primary MoE model under the same held-out,
placebo-controlled protocol. A provenance ledger audited existing artifacts
field-by-field, finding that the SWE-Gym anchor's dtype, alpha value, and
brief-condition status had all been inferred from directory names or code
defaults rather than recorded — prompting new born-annotated provenance
manifests and an explicit "champion" arm added to the SWE-Gym runner for a
direct, provenance-complete comparison against the scalar graft. A
compression-severity sweep was also built (queued, not yet launched) to test
whether the graft's effect concentrates as summaries become more aggressively
compressed, holding model, dtype, champion, and placebo battery fixed while
varying only the summarization request and measuring the realized compression
ratio per conversation.

**Separately**, a subagent-mined retrospective on pod environment failures
diagnosed the recurring dependency breakage as a consequence of an intrinsic
split between the local (4-bit MLX) and pod (bf16 HF) runtimes, compounded by
pod-side dependency installation being copy-pasted and drifted across seven job
scripts with no single source of truth — including a live three-way
contradiction on the `transformers` version pin and a structural gap where most
job scripts never install `pandas`/`pyarrow`, needed for SWE-Gym-style tasks.
The proposed fix (a shared `pod_env.sh` plus a fail-closed dependency preflight
check) has not yet been implemented.

**Outstanding risks and next actions:** whether the champion-validation
experiment's content-specificity confidence interval clears zero will determine
whether the next paper is a rigorous positive result or a clean
bounding/negative writeup; whether the SWE-Gym-style effect survives a
production-faithful summary under a behavioral metric is the only open path to a
positive headline; the compression-severity sweep's outcome (concentration vs.
flat null) remains unmeasured; the pod dependency single-source-of-truth fix is
still pending; and the notes-update heartbeat automation's unreliability means
manual verification remains necessary until it is fixed or replaced.
