_The day combined a substantial overhaul of the notes/conversation-archive
tooling with the most consequential research turn of the project to date:
independent verification collapsed both of ValueGraft's headline "recovery"
results, a deep precision/provenance audit found the project's honesty
"cornerstone" was built on the wrong quantization and the wrong intervention
arm, and Fable was given full authority to redirect the paper toward an honest
bounding/postmortem framing while one narrow, correctly-precisioned positive (a
small bf16 SWE-Gym effect) and an unresolved champion-tuning question were
carried forward into new, still-running validation experiments._

## Notes/archive tooling

A long session rebuilt the conversation-note pipeline: a real `--dry-run` mode
and a 6-hour conversation-span duration policy were added, then applied to
regenerate 10 policy-violating notes into 27. Investigation of the summarizer
inputs found app-generated compaction-continuation messages (`isCompactSummary`)
leaking into prompts as ordinary user text; filtering them cut prompt size by up
to ~26% per note with no loss of real content. Archive-naming drift
(non-contiguous filename counters across ~90% of files) was traced to
git-history-based date inference being unreliable once a file had been renamed
multiple times; the fix seeds dates from the conversation manifest (which stores
each note's canonical first-message date) rather than trusting git rename
history, and bulk-commits normalizer renames instead of committing per file.

A new two-layer meta-summary system was built: per-day summaries at
`notes/YYYYMMDD.md` (this file's own format) and an all-days synthesis at
`notes/README.md`, both keyed by content-hash manifests so renames don't force
rebuilds, both excluded from ordinary archive-naming, and both required to run
through `deno fmt` before their manifest hash is computed. A shared
forbidden-term/sensitive-topic filter (`scripts/notes_summary_filters.py`,
configurable via CLI/env/gitignored dotenv) was wired into all generators.
`notes/AGENTS.md` was rewritten so agents no longer manage filenames/prefixes
manually — that's owned by the scripts. A local git-identity override intended
to attribute commits to a Codex identity was found to have silently reattributed
all repo commits and was reverted; future per-agent attribution should use
per-commit env vars, not repo-level config.

Two automation heartbeats (four-hour scheduled reruns of the update pipeline)
missed their firing windows during the day and had to be run manually or
retargeted; the mechanism is now treated as unreliable, with manual invocation
plus its dry-run/validation checks as the working fallback.

## Research: reproduction collapse and the precision/provenance crisis

An independent reproduction of the judged-sense "+12pp" headline, run on a fresh
render of the same conversations under clean current code, collapsed to +1.0pp
(CI spanning zero); a same-judge disentangle attributed most of the drop to
render fragility rather than judge calibration, matching an earlier collapse
already seen in the referent-logprob metric. Both of the project's positive
"meaning recovery" headlines are now established as non-reproducing at n=12
under MoE render nondeterminism, where render-to-render noise is comparable in
size to the claimed effect.

A separate deep audit (prompted by direct owner objection that core results had
"never been reproduced at 16-bit") found the problem was worse than assumed:
**both** headline pipelines — recovery and the honesty/fabrication effect — had
been run and scored only on the local 4-bit MLX backend, never at bf16. A raw
bf16 honesty answer set existed (`results/honesty_30b_bf16`, later reformatted
as `phase2_30b_bf16`) but had never been judged; scoring it was completed
same-day and confirmed the packed-KV fabrication-reduction effect replicates at
full precision (decoy fabrication −62.5pp, evicted-fact −20.8pp, both
CI-significant).

A second, independent problem surfaced alongside the precision gap: the honesty
result's intervention arm, "H-pack," retains write-time **keys** as well as
values in a packed layout with no conversation tail — a materially different,
coupled-KV configuration from the paper's named value-only ValueGraft method
(α_K=0, aligned positions, production layout). A control condition (H-pack built
from a _different_ conversation's summary state) suppressed fabrication just as
effectively as the real arm, indicating the honesty effect is driven by the
packed layout/regime rather than by matching content — so even at the correct
precision, H-pack cannot serve as a ValueGraft cornerstone.

Consequently, no on-disk result satisfied "bf16 and value-only ValueGraft and
positive" as of the audit. The one qualifying positive is a SWE-Gym
teacher-forced-logprob effect (E-tuned, +0.0156 nats, paired CI [+0.005, +0.027]
excluding zero, changing 49/75 greedy generations) — real but small, run under a
deliberately terse ("brief") summary condition designed to handicap the
baseline, and not yet tested under a production-faithful summary. The
placebo-controlled bf16 value-only test of the core recovery claim
(`effect_bound`) is a preregistered null on two models.

## Fable consultations and the pivot decision

Three substantial Fable consultations, each written to a durable notes file per
established practice, shaped the day's direction:

- A holistic assessment flagged that both positive headlines lived only under
  the handicapping "brief" summary condition (no positive existed under a
  production-faithful one) and questioned the plan's priority ordering.
- A focused review of the "native rendering is required" premise found it only
  partially supported: the requirement that the graft's summary be
  self-generated by the model held up cleanly, but the claim that replies must
  also be natively rendered rested on a single low draw from render-to-render
  noise of comparable size to the effect itself.
- With the recovery headlines dead, Fable was given full authority to decide the
  paper's direction. Its verdict: lead with the honesty/anti-fabrication effect
  (now correctly bf16-confirmed, though still framed as supporting rather than
  the named method) and elevate render-fragility itself into a co-headline
  methodological contribution, rather than trying to rescue a "ValueGraft
  recovers meaning" claim. A further un-anchored consult, invoked with authority
  to redirect the whole project, independently re-derived the same
  disqualifications and recommended two viable honest deliverables — a
  negative/bounding-plus-instrument-dissociation science note, or (its stronger
  recommendation) a candid postmortem with the bounding result as its technical
  core — while cautioning against further investment in the synthetic
  sense/referent probe, which disagrees with the one real-task (SWE-Gym) signal.

## Terminology and methodological clarifications set today

- **H-pack ≠ ValueGraft.** H-pack is a packed, coupled-KV (keys+values)
  auxiliary arm; ValueGraft is specifically the value-only, aligned-position
  graft. Do not conflate them in any future claim.
- **Provenance must be proven, not inferred.** A new project-wide standard (and
  memory) requires every claimed result to have its model, dtype/quantization,
  exact intervention, metric definition, and summary condition read from the
  artifact/runtime — never inferred from a directory name — after repeated
  instances of "yes we have data for X" turning out to be the wrong variable.
- **The 07-05 "mitigation-first reframing" was owner intent, not a pivot.** The
  owner corrected the historical narrative embedded in earlier generated
  summaries: mitigation-first framing was the goal from the start; the
  mechanism-as-finding framing was an agent misunderstanding, not something
  review corrected mid-project. Future summaries should not narrate this as a
  redirection.
- **Naming discipline for the eventual paper:** coin no branded term (e.g.,
  "ValueGraft") unless the final result is a genuinely reusable technique; if
  the outcome is mostly negative/narrow, use plain descriptive language instead.
- **Scope discipline:** any surviving positive claim must be stated as narrow —
  one model family, a teacher-forced-logprob proxy (not task success), and
  effect concentrated (if at all) in the aggressive-compaction regime — with
  methods detail sufficient for a reader to conceptually (not literally)
  reproduce the experiment.
- A provenance ledger (`notes/2026070931`) was built auditing existing artifacts
  field-by-field against these standards, and manifest-stamping instrumentation
  was added to the harnesses so future results self-document these variables.

## New experiments queued/launched by end of day

Three follow-on designs were specified and partly executed, all
placebo-controlled and CI-gated: (1) a champion (per-layer-tuned) graft
validation against a content-corrupted placebo, at 4-bit first then bf16, to
test whether the previously-observed large tuned-config improvement is
content-specific or an amplified version of the already-known generic placebo
effect; (2) a per-head (vs. per-layer) champion scan on the primary bf16 model
to see whether finer-grained head selection improves on the per-layer champion,
validated on a disjoint held-out split; (3) a compression-level sweep testing
whether the graft's effect concentrates under more aggressive summarization. A
`SC_CHAMPION_CONFIG` arm was also added to the SWE-Gym runner so the tuned
champion (not just the flat scalar graft) can be tested on real coding
trajectories. None of these had produced final validated numbers by day's end;
`results/champion_validate/`, `results/swegym_30b_bf16_prod/`, and a fresh
`phase2_30b_bf16_verdicts.json` were left as untracked in-progress artifacts.

The owner also issued detailed guidance for the eventual paper rewrite (deferred
until the champion validation resolves): drop internal jargon, minimize the
honesty-word tic, lead with rigorous methods and results before an elaborate
postmortem tail, and route drafting primarily through Fable with multiple
distinct fresh-eyes review passes.

## Open risks and next actions

The paper currently has no proven bf16, value-only, statistically significant
positive result beyond the narrow SWE-Gym effect, and the champion-tuning
question — whether per-layer or per-head tuning yields a genuinely
content-specific effect rather than an amplified generic placebo effect — is the
one live variable that could still change the paper's framing from
bounding/negative to a modest real positive. The immediate next actions are to
complete the queued champion, per-head, and compression-sweep validations at
bf16, decide the paper's shape based on those results, and only then proceed
through the owner-specified review-and-rewrite process before any promotion to
README or external publication (which remains gated on direct owner
involvement).
