_July 9 (UTC) was dominated by two intertwined threads: a notes-archive
infrastructure overhaul that stabilized daily/overall meta-summarization while
exposing unreliable scheduling automation, and a pivotal stretch in the
ValueGraft research project in which both previously-proposed positive headlines
were independently audited and disqualified — prompting a reframing toward an
honest bounding/postmortem paper, followed by a late-day reopening of an
untested per-layer-tuning variable that could still salvage a positive result._

## Notes-archive tooling

The archive/summarization pipeline was substantially reworked: a 6-hour
conversation-span duration policy for splitting notes, structural filtering of
app-generated compaction-continuation scaffolding out of summarizer inputs
(cutting prompt size ~4.25% across existing conversation notes), a
git-blob-keyed date cache to stabilize archive-filename renumbering (including
fixing rename chains where a target path was itself mid-rename), and a two-layer
meta-summary system — per-day files at `notes/YYYYMMDD.md` and an all-days
synthesis at `notes/README.md` — both gated by manifest hashes and a shared
configurable forbidden-term filter. A `deno fmt`-before-hashing policy was
established for generated Markdown/JSON. A repository-local git-identity change
intended to attribute commits to a Codex identity briefly and unintentionally
reattributed all commits repo-wide; it was traced and reverted, and the guidance
going forward is to use per-commit author/committer overrides rather than local
git config for this.

Scheduled heartbeat automation for periodic archive updates proved unreliable
twice during the day — missing its firing window by roughly two hours once, and
colliding with an already-active heartbeat on a retry attempt (resolved by
retargeting rather than duplicating). Manual invocation of the updater, together
with its dry-run/manifest/forbidden-term validation checks, remains the working
fallback. A later heartbeat-triggered run found the working tree 21 commits
ahead of `origin/trunk` from a concurrent research work stream (plus an
untracked bf16 result file), and deferred any push decision pending inspection
of final state — an item not resolved within this day's sources.

## ValueGraft: cornerstone collapse and precision crisis

The day's central research thread continued the erosion of the project's
positive headlines and ended with two successive reframings.

- **Recovery headline confirmed dead.** A disciplined re-render of the same
  twelve reference conversations under clean current code, checked under one
  consistent judge, showed the previously-reported judged-sense recovery of
  +12pp collapsing to +1.0pp (CI spanning zero). A same-judge disentangle
  isolated the cause: judge recalibration accounted for only part of the drop
  (+12.0→+8.7pp), while render-to-render variance under MoE nondeterminism
  accounted for the rest (+8.7→+1.0pp) — the same failure pattern previously
  seen on the referent-logprob metric.
- **Native-render premise partially deflated.** A focused Fable analysis split
  the claim that the graft requires natively self-rendered replies into two
  parts: the requirement that the _summary_ be self-generated (rather than a
  foreign one) held up under a clean isolation test; the requirement that the
  _replies_ be natively rendered did not — the "null" native draw used to argue
  for it sat within a render-noise band whose size was comparable to the effect
  itself, and a second independent native draw essentially reproduced the
  originally-reported authored effect.
- **Honesty headline provisionally promoted, then disqualified.** The packed-KV
  honesty/anti-fabrication effect (compacted models fabricate about evicted
  content; write-time KV retention converts fabrication into admission) cleared
  conversation-clustered bootstrap significance thresholds and was provisionally
  elevated to lead the paper. A subsequent independent precision/provenance
  audit found two disqualifying problems: the result had only ever been scored
  at 4-bit (never at bf16), and its intervention arm ("H-pack") retains
  write-time _keys_ as well as values in a packed layout — a materially
  different, coupled-KV intervention from the paper's named value-only
  ValueGraft method. The audit further found the honesty effect is largely
  layout-driven rather than content-specific: a control built from a _different_
  conversation's summary state suppressed fabrication about as effectively as
  the real arm.
- **No valid bf16 + value-only cornerstone found on first audit.** The
  placebo-controlled bf16 value-only recovery test (`effect_bound`) was a
  preregistered null on two models, and the only bf16 value-only positive on
  disk — a small SWE-Gym logprob effect (+0.0156 nats) — was initially assessed
  as too thin or possibly render-null to carry a paper.
- **Postmortem and correction.** A detailed postmortem documented the root
  causes of the wrong-precision/wrong-arm conflation: a "split-brain" runtime
  (local 4-bit MLX core-eval pipelines vs. pod bf16 side pipelines) that made a
  bf16 label available nowhere it was actually earned, and an earlier
  arm-vocabulary reframing that decoupled historical arm IDs from the current
  canonical method. A later, independent Fable pass recomputed the SWE-Gym
  number itself and corrected the earlier audit: its confidence interval
  ([+0.005, +0.027]) actually excludes zero, and it changes the greedy model
  output on 49 of 75 tasks — a genuine, correctly-precision, correctly-armed
  positive, not a null.

Given this, the recommended framing shifted twice: first toward an honesty-led
paper with render-fragility promoted to a co-equal methodological headline (full
section restructuring specified), and then, after the arm/precision
disqualification and the corrected SWE-Gym reading, toward treating a candid
process postmortem as the primary deliverable, with the bf16 placebo-controlled
null as its technical core and the SWE-Gym real-task result as the only
real-task positive signal worth further investment — specifically if re-run
under a production-faithful (not brief) summary condition with a behavioral
rather than logprob metric. Both Fable passes agreed the synthetic
sense/referent probe should no longer be treated as the primary instrument,
since it is null-to-harmful at bf16 and disagrees with the real-task data.

Separately, an attempted production-faithful SWE-Gym run failed on a pod
dependency error (missing pandas), which triggered a retrospective tracing this
to a broader, unaddressed structural issue: pod-side dependency setup is
copy-pasted per job script with no single source of truth, including a live
contradiction where some scripts pin `transformers==5.0.*` and others explicitly
avoid that version because it breaks pod weight loading. A shared `pod_env.sh`
plus a fail-closed dependency preflight check was recommended but not yet
implemented.

Late in the day, the owner reopened a separate, previously untested variable:
early per-layer ("champion") tuning had appeared to produce a much larger effect
than the default configuration, but this had never been run through the same
placebo-controlled design as the bf16 null. The owner directed a structured
validation along two precision tracks in parallel — 4-bit first (using an
official or closest-available quantized checkpoint, prioritized for speed and
parallelism across warm pods) and bf16 — each with its own separate per-layer
tuning pass. A placeholder note was created for Fable to produce the precise
runnable experiment specification; the spec itself had not yet been written by
day's end.

A standalone correction was also recorded: the mitigation-first framing
(evaluating write-time KV retention as a deployable compaction mitigation) was
the owner's intent from the project's start, not a course-correction introduced
by later review — the "reframing" language in earlier generated summaries
reflects agents' contemporaneous misunderstanding of the owner's intent, not a
change in project goals, and should not be narrated as a pivot in the paper or
future summaries.

## Results and caveats

- Referent recovery: +12.0pp → +8.7pp (judge recalibration) → +1.0pp
  (render-fragility), final CI spans zero.
- Honesty effect (4-bit, H-pack arm): decoy fabrication −66.7pp [45.8, 87.5],
  evicted-fact fabrication −62.5pp [41.7, 83.3]; a matched control using a
  different conversation's summary state suppressed fabrication comparably,
  indicating the effect is layout-driven rather than content-specific.
- `effect_bound` (bf16, value-only, placebo-controlled): null on both tested
  models; content-specificity and non-destructiveness vs. a random-value placebo
  were separately established.
- SWE-Gym (bf16, value-only): +0.0156 nats mean logprob effect, CI [+0.005,
  +0.027] excluding zero, changes greedy output on 49/75 tasks — run under a
  brief (baseline-handicapping), not production, summary condition.
- Cross-architecture bf16 value-only results on synthetic plants were
  null-to-negative outside the one MoE anchor model.
- **Unresolved discrepancy:** one account in today's sources states a same-day
  pass scored the previously-unscored bf16 honesty answers and confirmed
  replication (decoy +62.5pp, evicted +20.8pp, both CI-significant); a separate
  independent audit and postmortem, written afterward, state the bf16 honesty
  set (`results/honesty_30b_bf16`, `phase2_30b_bf16`) was still unscored and
  recommend scoring it as a next step. An untracked
  `results/phase2_30b_bf16_verdicts.json` appeared on disk by day's end,
  consistent with a scoring pass having run, but this has not been reconciled
  against the audits' claims — treat the bf16 honesty replication as unconfirmed
  until checked directly against that file.
