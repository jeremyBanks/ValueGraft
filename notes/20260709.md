## Research state

The central recovery claim is no longer supported as a robust effect. The
original judged and referent improvements were 4-bit or render-fragile, and the
native-render comparison was confounded by substantial MoE generation variance.
Independent native draws of the same conversations differed by roughly the size
of the apparent referent effect. The self-generated-summary dependence remains a
supported mechanism-level observation, but native reply rendering is not
established as necessary.

The provenance audit also disqualified a previously proposed behavioral
cornerstone: its scored data used local 4-bit inference and a coupled key/value
intervention in a packed layout, rather than the canonical bf16 value-only
intervention in the production compacted layout. It must therefore remain
supporting or adjacent evidence, not ValueGraft evidence. This reinforced the
rule that model, precision, intervention identity, summary condition, metric,
split, and code provenance must be verified from artifacts or runtime state
before a number is used in a headline.

The decisive bf16 champion validation on Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 used a
held-out conversation split, a per-layer tuned value-only configuration,
realistic self-generated summaries, and a conversation-clustered bootstrap.
Graft versus compacted baseline was −0.0026, CI [−0.041, +0.043]: null or
underpowered. The positive position-shuffle contrast, +0.1925, CI [+0.118,
+0.266], shows that scrambling values is harmful, but does not show that
correctly grafted values improve over ordinary compaction. Content specificity
and net recovery must remain separate claims.

The existing SWE-Gym result is a legitimate but narrow supporting observation:
approximately +0.0156 teacher-forced next-action log-probability under brief
summaries, with a reported paired CI excluding zero and changed greedy outputs
on 49/75 tasks. It is not task success or resolve rate, does not validate the
tuned champion, and belongs to an aggressively compressed, baseline-handicapping
condition. Historical files inferred some controls from directory names rather
than recording them; later instrumentation and the production-summary run were
introduced to prevent this ambiguity. The production-faithful comparison is the
important missing counterpart.

Cross-architecture results remain null-to-negative on the synthetic recovery
plants, including substantially negative results for dense Qwen variants and
null-to-negative results elsewhere. These findings support architecture
dependence or measurement fragility, not a settled reversal law. The
preregistered QK-norm hypothesis remains unsupported.

## Methodological and workflow decisions

- The canonical intervention is value-only: keys remain untouched, aligned
  write-time values are blended into the compacted layout, and α or a
  per-layer/per-head configuration must be recorded explicitly. Packed
  coupled-KV variants and other layouts are distinct experiments.

- The primary recovery estimator is raw graft-minus-baseline log-probability,
  not the retired ratio estimator. Content specificity is the paired
  graft-minus-placebo contrast, with conversation-clustered confidence
  intervals.

- Tuning must occur on a disjoint validation set and be evaluated on held-out
  conversations. The champion experiment derives from c01–c06 plus auxiliary
  validation conversations and evaluates c07–c24. Placebos use identical slots
  and α values with position-shuffled, cross-conversation, norm-matched random,
  or mean source values.

- Teacher-forced log-probability is a proxy, not behavioral task success. Brief
  summaries and production-faithful summaries are separate conditions; results
  must not be pooled or described as interchangeable.

- The current paper should be determined by completed evidence. If the remaining
  experiments do not establish a reusable effect, the appropriate output is a
  bounded/null scientific paper with a detailed methods postmortem, not a
  technique-forward paper. No capitalized or camelCase method name should be
  promoted unless the final evidence earns a reusable technique.

- Fable consultations must receive full trajectory context and point to the
  conversation logs. Serious consultations write their assessments to durable
  `notes/` files. The owner’s mitigation-first framing was original intent;
  earlier “reframing” language records agent misunderstanding and must not be
  presented as a change in project goals.

## Operations and repository state

Checkpointed per-conversation render artifacts are now atomic, reusable, and
resumable. Validation showed fresh, resumed, and simulated-kill paths agree,
while a corrupted checkpoint diverges. Render fingerprints remain safe only
while the scaffold is frozen because scaffold content is not yet hashed.

A compression sweep was built to test whether any effect is gated by aggressive
compaction, holding model, precision, champion, held-out split, and placebo
design fixed while measuring realized summary/context ratios. The per-head
champion scan and compression sweep were queued or active; any positive result
must clear the same held-out placebo criterion. The earlier 4-bit champion
effort was abandoned after environment churn and memory failures.

The recurring operational risk is a split runtime: local MLX/4-bit/Python 3.12
versus pod HF/bf16/system Python 3.11. Pod jobs contain contradictory
Transformers pins, inconsistent pandas/pyarrow setup, repeated torchvision
workarounds, and no canonical environment artifact. A shared pod environment
script plus fail-closed dependency validation is the proportionate fix before
further environment-sensitive work.

The archive and summary pipeline was substantially repaired. Daily and
hierarchical rollups now use manifest/blob provenance, validate generated
output, preserve original note dates, and include deterministic source footers.
Focused tests, normalization, manifest checks, leakage scans, and summary
regeneration passed. Scheduled automation missed one expected run, so repository
state must be checked rather than inferred; manual catch-up remains the
fallback. Unrelated experiment artifacts were intentionally preserved.

## Open questions and next actions

- Complete and audit the per-head and compression-sweep results, especially
  whether any aggressive-summary effect survives placebo control and whether it
  transfers to production-faithful summaries.
- Score the unscored bf16 behavioral answer set only as supporting evidence; do
  not let it become a ValueGraft cornerstone.
- Update the claims ledger so every surviving number records precision, arm
  identity, condition, metric, split, and provenance status.
- Freeze GPU spending after the precommitted experiments, turn pods off, and
  perform an un-anchored evidence valuation.
- Rewrite the paper from scratch after the final data: rigorous methods and
  bounded results first, postmortem later; then obtain distinct Fable,
  adversarial-statistics, proofreading, and fresh-eyes readability reviews.
- Add the shared pod environment specification and fail-closed dependency checks
  before any further warm-pod or coding-trajectory experiment.
