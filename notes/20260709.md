_This was an unusually dense UTC day: notes-archive tooling was hardened further
(duration policy, compaction-scaffolding filtering, archive renaming,
sensitive-topic filtering, a two-layer daily/overall meta-summary system), while
on the research side both of the project's headline positive results —
meaning-recovery and the honesty/anti-fabrication effect — were independently
shown to be non-reproducing or built on the wrong precision/arm, triggering a
full precision-provenance audit, a candid postmortem, and a pivot toward either
a rigorous positive paper or an honest bounding writeup, gated on a newly

## Notes/workflow tooling

The conversation-archive system gained several durable pieces: a 6-hour
conversation-span duration policy for splitting notes, structural filtering of
app-generated compaction-continuation messages out of summarizer inputs
(distinct from Codex's own compaction representation, which was already
excluded), and a shared date-cache module so archive renaming no longer depends
on unreliable git rename history. That last fix mattered in practice — a chain
of files kept oscillating between successive renaming runs until the cache was
seeded from the conversation manifest's canonical per-note dates instead of git
history for conversation notes. A daily meta-summary layer (`notes/YYYYMMDD.md`,
this file's own format) and an overall `notes/README.md` synthesis were built
and backfilled for all existing days, both gated on `deno fmt` before hashing
and on a configurable forbidden-term/sensitive-topic filter loadable via CLI,
environment variable, or a gitignored dotenv file. `notes/AGENTS.md` was
rewritten so agents no longer manage archive filenames/prefixes manually — that
is now entirely script-owned. A repo-local git identity change (intended to
attribute commits to a Codex identity) briefly and unintentionally reattributed
all commits repo-wide before being caught and reverted; going forward, Codex
attribution should use per-command environment variables or commit trailers, not
repo-level git config. Separately, the scheduled heartbeat automation meant to
trigger periodic notes updates missed its firing window at least twice during
the day; manual invocation of the updater (with its dry-run and validation
checks) remains the working fallback, and future agents should verify the
automation actually ran rather than trust the schedule.

A standing correction was recorded: the "mitigation-first" framing (write-time
KV state differs from re-encoded text) that earlier daily summaries describe as
a course-correction driven by external review was, per the owner, the owner's
original intent all along — the earlier mechanism-as-finding framing was agents'
misreading of that intent, not a pivot. Future narration of the project's
history should not describe this as "we initially believed X until review
corrected us."

## Research: reproduction collapse and precision/arm audit

A disciplined re-check reproduced the judged-sense recovery result on a fresh
render of the same twelve conversations and found it collapsed from the reported
+12pp to +1.0pp, with a confidence interval spanning zero. Disentangling the
cause showed judge recalibration accounted for only part of the drop
(+12.0→+8.7); the render itself accounted for the rest (+8.7→+1.0), matching an
earlier pattern seen with the referent-logprob metric. A related re-examination
of the "native rendering is required" premise found it should be split in two:
the requirement that the graft use the model's own self-generated summary
(rather than a foreign one) held up cleanly, but the requirement that replies
also be natively rendered rested on a single low draw — a second independent
native render of the same conversations reproduced the original (non-native)
result, and render-to-render variance at this sample size was found to be
roughly the same magnitude as the effect itself.

With the recovery headline weakened, attention turned to the
honesty/anti-fabrication result (decoy fabrication suppressed from 79% to 12%
admission at the 30B scale) as a candidate replacement cornerstone, and a
Claims-ledger audit caught a related overclaim in passing: a previously reported
"38/48 (79%) accurate evicted-fact recall" figure did not reproduce (true recall
was 0/24 at both tested scales); the corrected, supportable claim is that the
intervention converts fabrication into admission rather than restoring recall. A
subsequent deep precision/provenance audit, however, disqualified the honesty
result as a cornerstone on two independent grounds: every scored honesty run was
4-bit only (a bf16 answer set existed but was unscored at audit time), and the
intervention tested (retaining write-time keys and values in a packed layout) is
materially different from the paper's named value-only method — a follow-up
control showed the effect is driven by the packed layout itself rather than by
matching content, since substituting another conversation's state suppressed
fabrication just as effectively. The same audit found the recovery headline was
also 4-bit-only, and that its placebo-controlled bf16 analogue (value-only,
content-specific by construction) was null on two models. The one confirmed
bf16, value-only, statistically significant positive on disk was a small SWE-Gym
teacher-forcing effect (initially misjudged as too thin to matter, then
recomputed with a bootstrap CI excluding zero and shown to change 49 of 75
tasks' greedy output) — measured, however, under a deliberately terse ("brief")
summary condition designed to handicap the baseline, not a production-faithful
one.

This sequence produced a formal postmortem documenting the root causes: a
split-brain runtime (local 4-bit MLX vs. pod-side bf16) with no single
dependency source of truth on the pod side, and an arm-naming drift that let a
differently-configured intervention be conflated with the paper's canonical
method. A separate environment retrospective independently confirmed the
pod-dependency fragility, finding contradictory `transformers` version pins
across job scripts (including two scripts installing a version a third
explicitly avoids as pod-breaking) and a missing `pandas`/`pyarrow` dependency
on most pod bootstraps — proposing a shared pod-environment script and a
fail-closed dependency preflight check as the fix, not yet implemented.

## Where the paper stands and what's next

The net effect of these audits is that neither original headline currently
qualifies as a cornerstone under the standing rule that a cornerstone must be
both bf16-precision and the actual value-only method. Guidance going forward:
(1) do not lead the paper with the synthetic recovery probe, which is
null-to-harmful at bf16 across several architectures and disagrees with the one
real-task result; (2) the only live path to a genuine positive headline is the
SWE-Gym real-coding-task instrument, re-run at bf16 under a production-faithful
(non-brief) summary with a behavioral rather than teacher-forcing metric; (3) if
that doesn't hold up, the honest deliverable is a bounding/negative result
combined with a specific, detailed process postmortem, not a mitigation win.
Paper-writing itself is deferred until a champion-configuration validation
experiment resolves: whether a per-layer (and, in a follow-on design, per-head)
tuned graft configuration is genuinely content-specific — beating a placebo
grafted through the identical slots — or merely an amplified version of the
already-characterized generic, content-independent lift. That experiment is
designed (4-bit first, then bf16, tuned separately per precision) but not yet
launched as of day's end; its outcome determines whether the eventual paper is a
rigorous, elaborately-structured positive result or a coherent negative/bounding
writeup, with explicit style guidance already set (drop internal jargon like
"H-pack" and "arm E," stop over-using "honest/honestly," and route final
drafting and multiple independent review passes through Fable rather than
patching the existing premature draft).
