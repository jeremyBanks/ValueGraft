# 2026-07-04 UTC — Daily Meta-Summary

_The day was devoted to launching the ValueGraft semantic-continuity research
program: a firm experiment brief and two companion documents were drafted, an
MLX-based harness was built and validated through a full identity-test ladder, a
probe corpus was synthesized and repaired for contamination, and the first
interim (partial, n=4) results began to show category-specific signal on the 4B
development model while the full local batch continued running._

## Research and workflow developments

The core research question — whether retaining write-time KV cache state across
a context-compaction boundary preserves measurable semantic continuity that
text-identical recomputation loses — was formalized into a firm experiment brief
covering environment choice (`mlx-lm` on Apple Silicon, Qwen3-4B for development
and Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 for production, explicitly excluding Gemma and
Gated-DeltaNet/Mamba-hybrid architectures as unsuitable for clean cache surgery)
and an initial arm set: A (oracle full context), B (standard text-summary
compaction), C (gapped KV retention with an in-context-generated summary), D
(no-summary ablation), and E (value-only transplant, fresh keys/old values, in
both post-hoc and layer-interleaved variants).

An agent (claude-fable-5) built the harness, resolved an `mlx-lm`/`transformers`
version conflict, and implemented and passed a full L0–L4 identity-test ladder
before running any experimental arm — cache serialization/rebuild is
bit-identical, gapped-cache surgery is bit-identical under null conditions (and
loses only 0.04 nats evicting an irrelevant span), and value-transplant
machinery passes null-transplant identity checks. Three bugs (a
batched-vs-single-token kernel mismatch, an unstable `<think>`-block
chat-template issue, and a difflib alignment bug affecting tail alignment) were
caught by the ladder before they could contaminate results — validating the
project's discipline of testing machinery before trusting outputs.

A synthetic+natural probe corpus (12 synthetic scenarios with 120 hand-planted
probes across referent/sense/stance/evicted-fact categories, plus 8 natural
conversations) was generated with a division of labor: Claude subagents wrote
scenario and probe text, but the local subject model generated all
in-conversation assistant replies, since the continuation-logprob metric
requires text the subject model would itself produce. An initial contamination
audit found only 77/120 probes clean of tail leakage; a per-conversation repair
pass raised this to 115/120, with all five probe categories fully clean of tail
contamination (the remaining 5 are harmless early-section leaks in material that
is evicted anyway regardless of arm). With the corpus treated as final, the full
19-conversation × 12-arm-variant run (L5) began on the 4B model.

Two companion planning documents were also written and committed, queued behind
the core A–E analysis: a follow-up-explorations document proposing Arm H
(minimal in-context summary retention, with matched control B-min, isolating the
value of write-time-encoded summaries alone) and Arm G (retrieval-based value
transplant via one-shot cross-attention, generalizing Arm E to paraphrased
rather than verbatim content, addressing the fact that Arm E requires literal
token twins between old and new context); and a Phase 2
scale-up/coding-agent-extension document, explicitly framed as written without
visibility into local results and deferring entirely to empirical findings,
proposing a ~$100–200 cloud-credit budget split between a scale strategy (one
larger model) and a power strategy (more local-scale trials), plus a
coding-agent realism leg using real SWE-bench-style agent trajectories with
machine-checkable behavioral probes (e.g., does the agent re-run a command
already known to fail).

## Decisions, terminology, and methodological clarifications

- **Naming (non-binding, for eventual writeup):** ValueGraft is the umbrella
  term for the value-transplant family (Arm E and generalizations); SelfGist
  names Arm H (self-generated, in-context-encoded summaries used as
  training-free gist tokens); SoftGraft is a candidate name for Arm G
  (retrieval-weighted grafting).
- **Metric hierarchy amendment:** probe accuracy was promoted to the headline
  metric, with continuation NLL demoted to secondary — already reflected in the
  scoring code — because NLL proved noisy with wide per-conversation swings,
  while probes gave clearer, categorized signal.
- **A confound was identified and queued for correction:** Arm C generates its
  summary after seeing the full conversation, while Arm B's summary precedes the
  tail — an unaddressed ordering asymmetry. A new **B-causal** control
  (causal-order-matched to C) was specified so that C vs B-causal becomes the
  clean mechanism claim, B vs A remains the realism baseline, and B vs C is
  reported only as a deployment-flavored contrast, not a mechanism claim.
- Other amendments queued for application at analysis time: negative controls
  for value grafts (wrong-conversation and shuffled-value grafts), a GQA
  aggregation rule for Arm G (entropy-weighted average with per-slot entropy
  gating), a six-class leakage classifier per probe, contradiction probes, and
  positioning against nearest prior art (including arXiv 2606.17107, "Models
  Take Notes at Prefill").
- Judging and analysis work (scenario/probe authoring, probe grading,
  spot-checks) was moved from the local judge model to Claude subagents
  (Haiku/Sonnet-class), batched over the undecided set rather than called
  per-row, with the local model kept only as fallback — the local model's
  exclusive role is now generating experimental-subject text.
- Several operational practices were adopted and one — tee-before-tail — was
  saved to persistent cross-session memory ([[tee-before-tail]]): bracket
  long-running commands with wall-clock timestamps; serialize rather than
  parallelize GPU-bound jobs of similar size; use unbuffered (`python -u`)
  output for detached long jobs to avoid mistaking healthy buffering for a
  stall; commit frequently at milestones, keeping model weights out of the
  repository.

## Empirical results (interim, n=4, partial batch, caveated)

These figures come from a keyword-scoring pass only, on a partial (10/19
conversations complete) 4B-model batch, with no judge pass yet applied — treat
as provisional and subject to revision once the classifier/judge passes run.

- Single-conversation continuation NLL (c01): Arm A −0.77, Arm B −1.11. Arm D
  (tail-only, no summary) closed 63% of the A–B gap; Arm E-inter at α=1 closed
  33%; Arm C sat at roughly B's level; Arm E-post was at-or-below B and degraded
  as α increased. Arm H-gap (−1.47) beat its matched control B-min (−1.55) on
  the single sharpest same-text contrast available.
- Aggregated probe accuracy (n=4, now the headline metric): retained-state arms
  led where predicted on referent probes (C 6/8, H-gap 6/8, vs B 4/8; A 7/8
  ceiling; D 0/8, confirming the no-summary control behaves as expected) and on
  sense-disambiguation probes (H-gap 3/8 vs B 2/8). Arm E (value-only
  transplant) was not yet beating B on probes at this sample size.
- Known artifacts flagged rather than trusted at face value: stance probes are
  currently uninterpretable pre-judge, since anti-keyword scoring penalizes Arm
  A for quoting disliked terms even while complying; evicted-fact probes show
  clear summary leakage (Arm B nominally recovering 4/8 evicted facts), to be
  quarantined once the six-class leakage classifier is applied.

## Outstanding risks and open questions

- The 4B full batch (19 conversations × 12 arm variants) was still in progress
  and healthy at day's end (10/19 complete, ~9–9.5 minutes per synthetic
  conversation), with supplementary arm runs (B-causal, negative controls),
  classifier/judge passes, and the 30B production run all queued to follow.
- The C-vs-B ordering confound (summary generated post-history vs pre-tail)
  remains unresolved until B-causal is implemented and run.
- Interim probe/NLL results are keyword-scored only and pre-judge; stance and
  evicted-fact categories are known-unreliable until the judge and
  leakage-classifier passes are applied — no conclusions should be drawn from
  them yet.
- The Phase 2 scale-up plan is explicitly contingent and unvalidated against
  local results; the strategy choice (scale vs power) is deferred to a decision
  memo written only after local contrasts are in.
- Per the brief's own risk list: gapped-cache generation (Arm C) is mildly
  out-of-distribution and should be quantified via step-1 KL if the 30B model
  behaves erratically; Arm E's fresh-K/old-V decoupling could in principle
  degrade below baseline at high α, which would itself be a reportable finding
  about head tolerance for decoupling.
