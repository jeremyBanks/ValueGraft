_This day launched and substantially executed the ValueGraft investigation — a
new research program measuring whether KV-cache state carried across
context-compaction boundaries preserves semantic continuity that pure-text
recomputation loses — building the full experimental harness on local MLX
models, validating it end-to-end, and producing the first interim (partial-n)
results alongside two companion planning documents for follow-on arms and a
possible scale-up phase._

## Research program and core hypotheses

The founding brief frames the problem precisely: production compaction
re-encodes retained "tail" turns from scratch against a fresh summary,
destroying whatever meaning was resolved (referents, senses, stance) in the
original write-time KV entries. Two hypotheses anchor the work:

- **H1**: retaining write-time KV state across compaction preserves measurable
  semantic continuity that identical-text recomputation cannot.
- **H2 (factorization)**: the preserved meaning lives mainly in cached
  **values**, not position-stamped **keys** — implying a "fresh keys, old
  values" transplant could capture most of the benefit without any positional
  surgery.

The novel contribution is treated as the _measurement instrument_ (a probe suite
of referent, sense-disambiguation, stance, and evicted-fact questions) rather
than any single technique, since downstream task-accuracy work exists in
adjacent literature (CacheBlend, C2C, LatentMAS) but direct
interpretation-fidelity measurement across a compaction boundary does not.

## Experimental design

Work runs on `mlx-lm`, with Qwen3-4B as a fast development model and
Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct (4-bit) as the primary/reportable model — deliberately
excluding Gemma and any Gated-DeltaNet/Mamba-hybrid architecture, since
linear-state layers would make the key/value comparison ill-defined. An
identity-test ladder (L0–L4) gates every downstream claim: cache
serialize/rebuild must be bit-identical, gapped-cache and value-transplant
machinery must pass null-surgery identity checks before any arm is trusted.

Six arms were implemented, each isolating one factor:

- **A** — full-context oracle; **B** — standard text-summary compaction
  (production baseline)
- **C** — gapped KV retention (summary generated in-context, middle evicted,
  positions _not_ re-indexed)
- **D** — no-summary ablation of C
- **E** — value transplant ("fresh keys, old values," tested both post-hoc and
  layer-interleaved), with an α blend parameter
- Two later additions, **H** (minimal in-context summary retention, no tail) and
  its matched control **B-min**, were folded in early since they ride the same
  snapshot machinery at negligible cost.

A companion "follow-up explorations" document specifies two further arms, queued
behind the core analysis: **Arm H** (self-generated summary retained via its own
write-time KV, contrasted against **B-min**, the same summary text freshly
encoded — the single cleanest test of H1, since it isolates _encoding_ alone
with no tail or alignment machinery) and **Arm G** (retrieval-based value
transplant, generalizing E to paraphrased content via a one-shot cross-attention
lookup against the old context's keys — sound in principle where naive
value-space nearest-neighbor search is not, since value space has no privileged
basis). A four-way contrast matrix (B-min vs H, B vs E, E vs C, E vs G) is
intended to factor "meaning survival" into encoding, payload, address, and
correspondence-mechanism components respectively. Tentative naming for eventual
writeup: **ValueGraft** (the value-transplant family), **SelfGist** (Arm H),
**SoftGraft** (Arm G).

## Corpus and methodology decisions

- 12 synthetic scenarios (120 hand-planted probes) plus 8 natural conversations
  were composed with a division of labor: Claude subagents write scenario/probe
  text and do all judging/analysis, while the local subject model generates only
  its own in-conversation replies — necessary because the continuation-logprob
  metric requires text the subject model would naturally produce.
- An initial contamination audit found substantial tail leakage of planted
  facts; a repair pass brought the corpus to 115/120 probes clean, with all five
  probe categories fully clean of _tail_ contamination (remaining flagged leaks
  are in the evicted early section, which is harmless).
- A methodological gap surfaced during external review: Arm C's
  summary-after-full-history ordering versus Arm B's summary-before-tail
  ordering was an unaddressed confound. A new **B-causal** control
  (causal-order-matched to C) was added so that C vs B-causal becomes the clean
  mechanism claim, B vs A remains the realism baseline, and B vs C is reported
  only as a deployment-flavored contrast.
- The probe-scoring hierarchy was flipped: probe accuracy is now the headline
  metric, continuation NLL secondary (already implemented in scoring code).
  Other queued-but-not-yet-applied refinements: negative controls for value
  grafts (wrong-conversation, shuffled-value), a six-class leakage classifier
  per probe, contradiction probes, and a GQA aggregation rule for Arm G.

Several operational practices were adopted and logged as durable habits: bracket
long commands with wall-clock timestamps; serialize rather than parallelize
GPU-bound jobs; always `tee` long output to a scratch file before filtering with
`tail`/`grep` (formalized as a standing memory note); use unbuffered
(`python -u`) output for detached long jobs; commit frequently at milestones,
keeping model weights out of the repo.

## Empirical results (interim, partial data — caveat heavily)

At n=4 conversations (4B model, keyword-scoring only, no judge/classifier pass
yet), on the now-headline probe-accuracy metric: retained-state arms C and H-gap
led on referent probes (6/8 each vs B's 4/8), with the no-summary control D
correctly bottoming out at 0/8 and oracle A at 7/8; H-gap also led modestly on
sense probes (3/8 vs B 2/8). Arm E had not yet beaten B on probes at this sample
size. Continuation NLL, now secondary, was noisy and non-monotone across α for
arm E. Known confounds flagged for the judge/classifier passes rather than
trusted as-is: stance probes are currently uninterpretable pre-judge, and
evicted-fact probes show clear summary leakage in Arm B. **These numbers are
from an incomplete batch (10/19 conversations) and should not be treated as
final results.**

## Outstanding risks and next actions

- Gapped-cache generation (Arm C) is mildly out-of-distribution; if the 30B
  model behaves erratically this needs quantifying (step-1 KL) rather than
  silently tuning around.
- Arm E's fresh-key/old-value decoupling could degrade below baseline at high α
  — a non-monotone curve there is itself a finding, not just noise.
- Summary-text leakage into probes remains the single biggest validity threat
  and needs the automated audit applied to every arm, not just the initial
  corpus pass.
- Immediate next steps: finish the 4B full batch (19 conversations × 12 arm
  variants), run supplemental arms (B-causal, negative controls) and the
  classifier/judge passes, then proceed to the 30B production run.
- A Phase 2 document (scale-up + coding-agent realism extension) is drafted but
  explicitly contingent — it was written without visibility into local results
  and activates only if at least one contrast in the matrix shows a consistent,
  non-trivial, control-surviving effect. It proposes a real coding-agent
  trajectory corpus (SWE-bench-style) with machine-checkable behavioral probes
  (especially whether an agent re-proposes an already-rejected approach) as the
  realism leg, and a single larger-model run as the scale leg, gated by a short
  decision memo on which strategy the cloud budget (~$100–200) should buy.
