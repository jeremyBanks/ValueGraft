_The day established and began validating a controlled experiment on semantic
continuity across context compaction, with emphasis shifting toward probe-based
evidence after noisy continuation results. The 4B development batch was running
successfully, while 30B-scale evaluation, leakage quarantine, and
reproducibility decisions remained pending._

**Participants/contributors:** User; claude-fable-5.

The central hypotheses are that preserving write-time KV state can retain
meaning lost by text-only recomputation, and that values carry more of this
meaning than position-dependent keys. The planned contrast matrix now separates
summary encoding (B-min vs. H), value payloads (B vs. E), addresses (E vs. C),
and correspondence mechanisms (E vs. G). Suggested terminology is ValueGraft for
value-state transplantation, SelfGist for in-context-encoded summary retention,
and SoftGraft for retrieval-weighted transplantation; these names remain
provisional.

The implementation ladder L0–L4 passed identity and cache-surgery checks.
Development exposed and corrected batched versus single-token kernel
differences, unstable Qwen `<think>` template behavior, and summary/tail
alignment errors. An isolated sense test found transplanted values contributing
approximately +0.8 nats toward the planted sense at α=1, compared with +3.8 for
the full-context oracle and +1.9 for combined key/value retention; the α
response was monotone. The synthetic corpus contains 12 scenarios and 120
validated plants. After repair, all five probe categories had clean 24/24
tail-contamination checks, although harmless early-section leakage remained and
must be classified.

Early full-pipeline results are directional only. On one conversation, D closed
63% of the A–B continuation gap, E-inter at α=1 closed 33%, C was approximately
equal to B, and E-post degraded as α increased. H-gap slightly exceeded B-min.
With n=4, referent probes favored C and H-gap (6/8 each versus B at 4/8), while
sense probes modestly favored H-gap (3/8 versus B at 2/8); E had not yet
exceeded B. Continuation scores were noisy and generally below B, so
continuation is now secondary to calibrated, leakage-controlled probes.
Evicted-fact results showed summary leakage and should be quarantined using the
six-class classifier. Stance keyword matching is invalid because compliant
answers may mention rejected options; judging and paraphrase checks are
required.

Operational practices now worth preserving include serializing GPU-bound jobs
when concurrency increases memory pressure, bracketing long commands with
timestamps, capturing complete output with `tee` before filtering, using
unbuffered output for detached jobs, monitoring process health and file counts,
and committing useful milestones while keeping weights and large binaries
outside the repository. All deviations from the experimental brief should be
recorded in `DECISIONS.md`.

The current 4B batch was healthy at 10/19 conversations, with synthetic runs
taking roughly 9–10 minutes and natural conversations expected to complete
faster. Supplement arms were queued after the main A–E batch. The immediate
priorities are to finish the batch, apply leakage classification and judging,
analyze H-gap/B-min and the main contrast matrix, and pin model files,
quantization, library versions, seeds, manifests, and code commits before
scaling.

Important risks remain: gapped caches may be mildly out of distribution;
fresh-key/old-value pairs may fall below baseline at high α; summary leakage can
invalidate evicted-fact claims; and continuation metrics may lack power or
stability. Any 30B result should follow the validated ladder and use fp16
caches. If local effects are consistent, a short decision memo should choose
between spending future cloud budget on a larger model for scale or on more
trajectories/probes for statistical power. A later realism phase could evaluate
behavioral retention on coding-agent trajectories, where ruled-out approaches,
API constraints, identifiers, and coding conventions provide more objective
probes.
