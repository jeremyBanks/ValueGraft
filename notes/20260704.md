_The day opened the ValueGraft research program: a formal experiment brief on
whether write-time KV cache state survives context compaction better than
text-identical recomputation, followed by rapid harness construction, corpus
generation, and the start of the first (4B, development-scale) experimental run,
which reached interim n=4 results by day's end._

## Research program and design

The core hypothesis (H1) is that retaining write-time KV cache state across a
compaction boundary preserves measurable semantic continuity that standard
text-recompaction destroys — production compaction re-encodes retained recent
turns against a summary rather than the history they were actually generated in,
discarding resolved referents, disambiguated senses, and established stance that
lived only in the original KV entries. A secondary factorization hypothesis (H2)
holds that this preserved meaning lives mainly in cache **values**
(position-free payloads) rather than **keys** (position-stamped addresses); if
so, a "fresh keys, old values" transplant should capture most of the benefit
without any position-index surgery.

The experimental design specifies six named arms:

- **A** — full-context oracle (ceiling).
- **B** — standard text-based compaction (production baseline): summary +
  verbatim tail, fresh KV throughout.
- **C** — gapped KV retention: summary generated in-context with full history
  attendable, then middle evicted while sinks, tail, and summary keep their
  original KV entries and original position indices (a positional gap, not a
  re-index).
- **D** — ablation of C without the summary, isolating the summary's
  contribution.
- **E** — value transplant: Arm B's context prefilled normally, then values at
  verbatim-twin positions swapped from the old context's write-time values
  (`V ← (1−α)·V_fresh + α·V_old`), keys left fresh; run both post-hoc (E-post)
  and layer-interleaved (E-inter) variants.
- **F** — span retention (optional/stretch), CacheBlend-style contiguous-span
  transplant with position re-rotation.

Two companion documents extend this design but are queued behind the core A–E
analysis: a follow-up-explorations document adding **Arm H** (minimal in-context
summary retention — sinks plus the summary's own write-time KV, nothing else —
with matched control **B-min**, the sharpest single-variable test of the core
hypothesis) and **Arm G** (retrieval-based value transplant generalizing E to
paraphrased content via a one-shot cross-attention pass against the old
context's keys, since Arm E requires verbatim token twins); and a Phase 2
document covering a possible cloud-scale and coding-agent extension, explicitly
contingent on local results being meaningful and marked as advisory rather than
binding.

Models: Qwen3-4B as the fast development model for shaking out the full
pipeline, Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct as the primary/reportable model, both via
`mlx-lm` on Apple Silicon. Architectures with sliding-window or linear-state
(Mamba/Gated-DeltaNet) hybrids are excluded by design since they make the KV
comparison ill-defined.

## Methodological clarifications adopted

- **Identity-test ladder (L0–L4) is a hard gate**: no arm's results may be
  reported from a configuration that hasn't passed the relevant identity tests.
  This caught three real bugs before they could corrupt results (a
  batched-vs-single-token kernel mismatch, an unstable chat-template block, and
  a difflib alignment bug affecting tail alignment order).
- **Metric hierarchy flipped**: probe accuracy (referent, sense-disambiguation,
  stance, evicted-fact categories) is now the headline metric; continuation
  log-likelihood is secondary, since NLL proved noisy with wide per-conversation
  swings.
- **Division of labor**: local subject models generate only in-conversation
  dialogue (load-bearing for the continuation-logprob metric, which needs text
  near-modal for Arm A); all scenario/probe authoring, judging, and analysis is
  delegated to cloud subagents rather than the local model.
- **New control arm identified via external review**: **B-causal**, a fresh
  prefill of system+tail+summary with causal ordering matched to Arm C, needed
  because Arm C's summary-after-full-history ordering versus Arm B's
  summary-before-tail ordering was an unaddressed confound. C vs B-causal is now
  the clean mechanism claim; B vs A remains the realism baseline; B vs C is
  reported only as a deployment-flavored contrast.
- **Contamination control**: summary leakage into probes is treated as the
  single biggest validity threat requiring automated auditing; an initial audit
  of the corpus found only 77/120 probes clean, and a targeted repair pass
  (resampling/truncating leaking replies per conversation) raised this to
  115/120, with all four probe categories clean of tail contamination in the
  final corpus.
- **A six-class leakage classifier** (explicit-in-summary /
  paraphrased-in-summary / absent-from-summary / contradicted-by-summary /
  tail-visible / evicted-only) is queued to properly quarantine evicted-fact
  probes, which show clear leakage in interim scoring.
- Several operational habits were adopted as standing practice: bracketing long
  commands with wall-clock timestamps, serializing rather than parallelizing
  GPU-bound jobs, `tee`-ing full output to scratch files before filtering, using
  unbuffered output for detached long-running jobs, and committing frequently at
  milestones while keeping model weights out of the repository.

## Empirical observations (interim, n=4, unaudited by judge pass)

Caveats: these numbers come from keyword-only scoring on an early slice of the
4B batch, before the judge and leakage-classifier passes; stance probes are
currently uninterpretable pre-judge, and evicted-fact probes are known to be
leakage-contaminated.

- On probes (the headline metric): retained-state arms lead where predicted —
  Arm C and Arm H-gap both outperform Arm B on referent probes (6/8 vs 4/8),
  with the oracle at 7/8 and the no-summary ablation (D) correctly floored at
  0/8; H-gap also leads on sense-disambiguation probes (3/8 vs B's 2/8). Arm E
  (value-only transplant) had not yet shown an advantage over B on probes at
  this sample size.
- On continuation NLL (secondary, noisy): Arm D closed 63% of the A–B gap on the
  single sharpest conversation; E-inter at α=1 closed 33%; Arm C sat near B's
  level; E-post was at or below B and degraded further with increasing α. H-gap
  outperformed its matched control B-min on the single sharpest same-text
  contrast (−1.47 vs −1.55).

None of this should be read as a settled result — it is a partial slice awaiting
the judge pass, the leakage classifier, and the remaining ~9 of 19 conversations
in the batch.

## State and outstanding items

The 4B full batch (19 conversations × 12 arm variants) was in progress and
healthy at day's end, roughly halfway through at a steady per-conversation pace.
Queued after batch completion: the supplementary arm runs (B-causal, negative
controls for value grafts such as wrong-conversation and shuffled-value grafts),
the judge and leakage-classifier passes, and then the 30B production run. The
Phase 2 scale-up/coding-agent extension and Arm G/H implementation remain gated
on the core A–E results showing a consistent, non-trivial, control-surviving
effect — no commitment to cloud spend or a specific model-scale strategy (bigger
single model vs. more statistical power) has been made yet; that choice is meant
to be decided by a short memo once local results are in. The main open
scientific question the day's design is aimed at resolving is the E-vs-C
contrast: if value transplant alone (E) matches gapped KV retention (C), meaning
survives in values and a much simpler, prefix-cache-friendly technique suffices;
if C clearly beats E, positional addressing carries real information and the
more complex gapped scheme is justified.
