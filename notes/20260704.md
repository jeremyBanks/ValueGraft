_The project established and validated a controlled experiment on semantic
continuity across context compaction, with the 4B development batch underway.
Early results are directional: some retained or in-context-encoded summaries
outperform text-only compaction on targeted probes, while continuation
likelihood is noisy and does not yet support broad claims._

**Participants/contributors:** User; assistant model `claude-fable-5`;
autonomous coding-agent and other labeled subagents referenced in the source
notes, without additional source-supported personal identities.

The experiment compares full-context oracle, standard summary compaction,
retained-cache variants, and fresh-key/old-value transplantation. The central
contrasts are now more sharply defined:

- `B-min` versus `H` isolates whether a summary’s write-time encoding preserves
  meaning beyond its text.
- `B` versus `E` isolates contribution from tail value states.
- `E` versus `C` tests whether addresses/keys add value beyond transplanted
  payloads.
- `E` versus retrieval-based `G` would test exact-token correspondence against
  attention-mediated retrieval for paraphrased summaries.

The L0–L4 ladder passed identity and cache-surgery checks, including correction
of batched versus single-token kernel differences, unstable Qwen `<think>`
template behavior, and summary/tail alignment errors. A focused test found
planted-sense information in transplanted values: approximately +0.8 nats toward
the target sense at α=1, compared with +3.8 for the full oracle and +1.9 for
transplanting keys and values. The α curve was monotone in that isolated test,
but this does not establish end-to-end superiority.

The synthetic corpus contains 12 scenarios and 120 validated plants. After
repair, all five probe categories had clean tail-contamination checks (24/24
each), while harmless early-section leakage remained. Evicted-fact results are
therefore subject to quarantine using the six-class leakage classifier. Stance
probes require semantic judging and paraphrase checks; keyword scoring is
invalid because a compliant answer may mention a rejected option.

Early full-pipeline observations on `c01` were mixed: `D` closed 63% of the A–B
continuation gap, `E-inter` at α=1 closed 33%, `C` matched `B`, and `E-post`
degraded as α increased. `H-gap` slightly exceeded `B-min`. With n=4, referent
probes favored `C` and `H-gap` (6/8 each versus 4/8 for `B`), and sense probes
modestly favored `H-gap` (3/8 versus 2/8 for `B`); `E` had not yet exceeded
baseline. Continuation scores were generally noisy and below `B`, so probe
accuracy is currently the more informative metric.

The 4B batch was healthy at the latest handoff, with 10 of 19 conversations
complete and no reported errors; natural conversations were expected to run
faster, with supplement arms queued after the main A–E batch. Results remain
preliminary until the full batch, leakage exclusions, judging, and confidence
intervals are complete.

Operational decisions that should persist include temperature 0 evaluation, fp16
caches even with 4-bit weights, message-boundary compaction, serialized
GPU-bound jobs, complete logging via `tee` before filtering, unbuffered detached
output, health/file-count monitoring, and keeping model weights and large
binaries outside the repository. Every deviation from the brief should be
recorded in `DECISIONS.md`; useful milestones should be committed frequently.

The proposed terminology is `ValueGraft` for value-state transplantation,
`SelfGist` for in-context-encoded summary retention, and `SoftGraft` for
retrieval-weighted transplantation. These remain write-up vocabulary rather than
settled claims. The primary validity risks are summary leakage,
fresh-key/old-value incompatibility, out-of-distribution behavior from gapped
caches, quantized-cache interactions, and insufficient statistical power.

Likely next actions are to finish and validate the 4B A–E run, apply leakage and
judging controls, run `H-gap`/`H-pack` and `B-min` comparisons, and complete the
scale-versus-power decision memo. Before any external scaling, pin model files,
quantization, library versions, seeds, manifests, and the validated surgery
commit. If the local effect survives controls, choose either a larger
dense-model scale point or substantially more local trajectories/probes; a later
coding-agent evaluation should prioritize behavioral probes and task outcomes,
especially failures to remember ruled-out approaches.
