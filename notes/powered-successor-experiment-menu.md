# Powered successor: pre-commit experiment menu

**Date:** 2026-07-12  
**Status:** generated before freezing the successor design  
**Purpose:** satisfy the handoff's anti-fixation requirement. This is a menu,
not an authorization record. Rankings may change only from new evidence or a
documented feasibility constraint, not because one path is easier to code.

## Opening gap and runway

- Independent engineered semantic `N = 1`; at least 11 more conversations are
  needed merely to reach the handoff's floor, and a powered bound will likely
  need more.
- A provider read at `2026-07-12T14:39:21Z` observed `$57.1287946692` and no
  active pods.
- The exact e01 run observed about 22.30 nats of focal damage, but the strongest
  value-only cell recovered only 0.18 margin nats and no graft corrected the
  generated answer. That point estimate has no population interval and no
  available applied-bf16 placebo.
- One old e01 Phase-A plus treatment path spent about 772 seconds reloading and
  re-attesting the model per case. A one-load warm batch is therefore a large,
  scientifically neutral efficiency gain if order and eviction equivalence are
  demonstrated.

## Goal A candidates: obtain the requested confident upper bound

### A1. Powered engineered-carrier benchmark at R2/N

Run independently authored paired histories with identical visible carrier
tokens and positions. Score fresh, correct-history full-KV, wrong-history
full-KV, correct-history value-only, and wrong-history value-only. Use two
independently generated target-neutral carrier realizations per fixture, then
collapse within fixture before inference. Persist every text and token stream.

- **Strengths:** closest clean extension of e01; exact same-token causal
  contrast; cheap five-cell core; separates utility from history specificity;
  compatible with exact self-replacement and whole-bf16 V-row placebos.
- **Weaknesses:** engineered population; forced replay remains conditional on
  chosen tokens; R2 may be the wrong biological locus; purpose-authored cases
  do not automatically justify a broad population CI.
- **Use:** leading Goal-A design, provided the sampling frame and real render
  generation are frozen before treatment and the claim remains recipe-scoped.

### A2. Powered old c-bank summary experiment

Reuse `c01`--`c54` bodies and saved/native summary artifacts to scale the older
summary-plus-tail value graft with conversation-clustered intervals.

- **Strengths:** large supply, some saved renders, direct comparison with much
  of the project's prior record.
- **Weaknesses:** mixed authorship and model provenance, long and sometimes
  clipped bodies, known source-dependent sign reversal, historical estimator
  and schedule defects, and unclear sampling population. Repairing all of that
  risks another apparatus spiral.
- **Use:** bounded salvage/sensitivity only unless a literal audit unexpectedly
  identifies a homogeneous, fully bound subset. Never silently mix sources.

### A3. Native-summary crossover

For each paired history, generate multiple subject summaries, force each exact
token stream under both histories, and cross source history with fixed visible
summary text.

- **Strengths:** directly measures summary-render variability and text/history
  congruence; closest to the motivating question.
- **Weaknesses:** treatment-neutral leakage screening is essential; native
  summaries may explicitly state the target and remove damage; four-way
  crossover is materially more expensive.
- **Use:** add to Goal A only if a small outcome-blind pilot shows target-neutral
  summaries with retained damage. Otherwise run on a frozen subset after A1.

### A4. Fixed finite benchmark without population rhetoric

Measure a named panel exhaustively and report exact panel means/distributions,
plus uncertainty only over independently seeded carrier renders.

- **Strengths:** avoids pretending purposive fixtures are iid natural traffic;
  exact and reproducible.
- **Weaknesses:** cannot by itself satisfy a population-style upper-confidence
  claim over conversations.
- **Use:** always report as a companion target; use as the fallback if the
  recipe-inference assumptions do not survive audit.

## Locus candidates

### L1. Full-history request/header island before carrier content

Replace correct/wrong source rows for the carrier request and assistant header,
then causally recompute the carrier and everything after it.

- Clean, previously untested near-source locus.
- Tests whether state is deposited before the fixed carrier begins.
- More mechanistically plausible than repeating three nearly identical summary
  regions, but less strongly motivated than downstream tokens.

### L2. Downstream anchor / retained-tail aggregator rows

Replace rows written after the carrier while the model processes the fixed
anchor exchange and early retained tail; then recompute later tail/probe rows.

- Highest-priority surplus experiment. The primary-source note-taking result
  places causal mass on downstream aggregators and reports less than 1% on a
  field's own K/V.
- Requires a newly named planner/region; R3 cannot be relabeled as retained
  tail.
- Use a small locus-discovery subset, then confirm a frozen winning locus on
  untouched fixtures if it is materially positive.

### L3. Entire visible compacted prefix / maximal-state restart

Carry all available post-compaction write-time rows while keeping visible text
fixed, approximating the cleanest MEMENTO-style extra-state channel.

- Answers whether *any* same-token write-time state is useful before asking
  where it lives.
- Less diagnostic and less deployable, but a strong positive control for the
  overall channel.
- High priority if A1 is null and L2 is unclear.

### L4. Layer, head, and alpha targeting

Search held-out layers/depths and graft strengths rather than uniform full-depth
replacement.

- Could uncover cancellation hidden by full-depth replacement.
- Extremely vulnerable to selection bias. Discovery and confirmation fixtures
  must be disjoint; a scan result is not confirmatory evidence.
- Defer until a locus shows a signal or after the primary bound is complete.

## Numerical/configuration candidates

### C1. Shorter histories

Author 600--1,200-token cases to reduce the observed schedule/query-shape floor
and increase case throughput. This changes the target population and must be a
prespecified stratum, not a quiet optimization.

### C2. fp32 attention/cache computation

Measure whether the roughly 0.06--0.13-nat schedule sensitivity contracts. Full
fp32 30B may not fit an A100-80GB; selective fp32 cache/attention or a smaller
checkpoint can serve as a numerical diagnostic. Do not call a smaller model a
replicate of the exact subject.

### C3. True low-precision KV

Quantize cached K/V themselves with an explicit, audited scheme. The existing
NF4 runs quantized weights and changed kernels while leaving KV bf16, so they do
not answer this question. Do not pursue if the serving stack cannot expose the
actual KV dtype cleanly.

### C4. Alternate serving schedule

Run the turn-aligned P schedule on a calibration subset. It is a condition, not
a replicate. If it changes a decision-bearing contrast beyond a frozen
tolerance, scope the result to N schedule rather than averaging schedules.

## Subject/model candidates

### M1. Second instruction checkpoint, same architecture family

Replicate the best frozen design on another Qwen size/checkpoint after its own
build ladder. Useful for checkpoint specificity; less architectural diversity.

### M2. Different model family

Run a compatible dense or MoE instruction model with accessible exact K/V and
eager attention. Highest external-validity value, but only after Goal A and a
bounded feasibility check because new chat templates and cache layouts are a
known defect source.

### M3. Coding checkpoint and real agent traces

Use a coder model plus saved SWE/agent trajectories, with task success and
target likelihood. High deployment relevance, high confounding and authoring
cost. This is later exploration, not a substitute for the controlled bound.

## Data and framing candidates

### D1. Engineered exact-answer decisions

Threshold/rule, referent, and terminology strata with exact target pairs and a
byte-identical nonfocal control. Best measurement sensitivity and auditability;
claim must remain engineered-recipe scoped.

### D2. Natural saved conversations

Sample real transcripts or natural corpora, then author counterfactuals and
probes without touching treatment outcomes. Better realism, much harder
counterfactual validity. Use as confirmation of a discovered channel, not as a
first-stage noisy search.

### D3. Self-summary mitigation rather than latent-state detection

Compare production compaction with and without the graft, allowing ordinary
summary text. This asks a practical mitigation question but text may already
carry the target. It should be a separate endpoint from latent history-specific
state.

### D4. Distinguish three possible conclusions

1. a narrow summary/boundary transplant has a small upper bound;
2. write-time information exists but is concentrated downstream or requires a
   maximal state channel;
3. no tested channel transfers enough information to alter behavior in the
   scoped benchmark.

Never turn (1) into “the cache contains no hidden information.”

## Control candidates

- **Exact fresh self-replacement:** mandatory technical identity; must be
  bit-exact, not within a behavioral tolerance.
- **Wrong-history graft at the same position:** primary semantic-specificity
  control, not a nonsemantic placebo.
- **V-row permutation within frozen event classes:** feasible whole-bf16
  disruption placebo with fresh K, selected without outcome access and matched
  to a frozen displacement profile.
- **Unrelated full-KV donor at identical logical positions:** clean full-KV
  negative graft if decoded-valid matched donors can be authored.
- **Invalid option:** shuffling post-RoPE K rows across token positions; this
  adds a positional-phase intervention and is not a semantic-only placebo.

## Provisional ranking

1. **Goal A:** A1 at R2/N, full-KV and value-only, with real fixture N,
   independent carrier renders, exact identity, wrong-history controls, and an
   observed available V-row placebo.
2. **First surplus:** L2 downstream/retained-tail locus, with L3 maximal-state
   restart as the strongest channel test.
3. **Then:** L1 request/header, L4 layer/alpha scan with held-out confirmation,
   and C1/C4 numerical/schedule strata.
4. **External validity:** M1/M2 and D2 only after the exact-subject bound and
   highest-value locus test are durable.
5. **Bounded salvage:** A2 and historical results only where literal provenance
   and estimator validity can be demonstrated cheaply.

This ranking intentionally preserves multiple ways for the result to be
scientifically informative: a tight narrow null, a positive downstream channel,
a configuration interaction, or an honest unresolved bound.
