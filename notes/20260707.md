## Chain-arm and tau2 closure

The chain-arm grid finished with a mixed but clean verdict: the per-layer-tuned
("champion") grafting configuration cures the α=1.0 full-strength collapse (4/4
on seed s1 vs 0/4 untuned), but the Compacted baseline also scored 4/4 on every
seed, meaning these chains are too compaction-robust to demonstrate a repair
effect — a ceiling problem flagged earlier and now confirmed. The recall probe
meant to catch subtler damage was found invalid for this tier (it referenced
constants from an earlier task type), voiding that channel as evidence. All
compute pods were terminated; three forward options (harder cross-dependent
chains, adopt tau2, or stop the agent-task line and write up existing results)
were left for user decision.

Tau2 integration (banking_knowledge domain) was separately pursued and then
formally retired as non-viable (recorded as finding F4): even after swapping in
a capable simulator and lowering the compaction threshold, sessions were too
short to force genuine early-content eviction, a structural property of the
domain rather than a project failure.

## The F1 finding and its corroboration

The session's headline empirical result, F1 (established from a re-analysis
pivot toward "semantic richness" rather than fact-recall), states that
value-grafting recovers meaning on **sense** and **referent** probes (~+10–12pp
over plain compaction) but shows a near-null effect on **stance** probes, where
a good summary already preserves the disposition — and that the effect size
tracks where compaction actually caused damage. This was corroborated by an
independent judge-free teacher-forced logprob metric (same direction, smaller
magnitude) and a strict robustness cut (PARTIAL scored as failure preserves
ordering), but did **not** replicate at 4B scale (pattern muddled) — an
honestly-recorded scope bound, not a suppressed one. A per-layer K/V probe
closed Phase 2 cleanly: no single layer rescues key-grafting on the referent
category; value-grafting remains the operative axis. A subsequent, more careful
re-run of the paper's own gap-closure script on 30B was launched as a live
gating check after a newer "effect-bound" probe produced numbers that
contradicted the published 81%/64% (referent/sense) headline on identical cases
and model — currently unresolved and blocking all downstream work (including the
cross-architecture sweep) per explicit user instruction, pending confirmation of
which script is correct.

## J-lens interpretability track

A large side-investigation used the newly published Jacobian lens (J-lens, a
refinement of the older logit-lens technique) on Qwen3.6-27B, for which
pre-fitted lens weights exist (the main behavioral work uses a different model,
Qwen3-30B-A3B, so lens illustrations and behavioral results initially ran on
different checkpoints — later reconciled by re-running gap-closure on 27B too).
Work progressed through several stages, each correcting the last:

- Early qualitative demos (Pokémon private-referent recall, an ordinary
  block-party planning conversation, SWE-Gym next-action traces) showed vivid
  write-time-vs-fresh-encoding readout differences on private/ambiguous labels,
  illustrating that the same visible summary text can carry different internal
  semantics depending on whether it was written under the original conversation
  or freshly re-encoded.
- A next-token control established these readouts are not merely restating
  ordinary next-token probabilities — overlap is low at layer 48, higher (more
  next-token-like) at layer 62 — motivating layer 48 as the more distinctive
  "semantic" layer for illustration.
- A full-layer/all-token sweep (74,025 token-layer rows) confirmed this layer
  pattern at scale and flagged that raw high-change rows are dominated by
  punctuation/formatting noise, arguing for span-first rather than token-first
  presentation in future work.
- A significant process correction occurred when it was discovered that the
  first shareable J-lens explainer had never actually captured the
  grafted-intervention state — only two-state (write-time vs fresh) comparisons
  — because the underlying probe silently failed to snapshot grafted cache for
  Qwen3.6's non-standard architecture. This was treated as a methodological
  failure requiring a genuine three/four-condition probe (full, fresh,
  alpha-zero sanity control, grafted) before any public claims about the
  intervention itself could stand.
- The corrected four-sequence probe found real but modest effects: alpha-zero
  exactly reproduces the fresh baseline (sanity check passes); aligned α=0.75
  grafting shows mixed per-layer effects; shifted/misaligned controls behave
  differently, giving a useful negative control. A larger "ordinary" batch and a
  stricter "sparse challenge" batch later showed small alpha-sensitive positive
  effects at lower alpha, confirmed shifted-controls are worse, and demonstrated
  a clean scoped negative: value-only, summary-token-only grafting does not
  recover facts the summary aggressively omitted (only "reduces reinterpretation
  error" for facts already present in text).
- A free-generation divergence probe (designed to find a discrete internal
  "fork" toward the correct concept) produced a clean, pre-registered negative:
  0 of 43 cases showed a clean fork, reinforcing the paper's framing of the lens
  as a low-resolution corroborator rather than a source of vivid single-example
  evidence.

Standing terminology and process rules established: ValueGraft is the family
name, with V-Graft (value-only) and KV-Graft (full) as named variants; the
J-lens is explicitly a hypothesis-generation tool, not validation, per outside
review, though the project's stance is that its own independent behavioral
apparatus can cross-check lens-derived hypotheses more aggressively than that
caution alone would suggest; two-condition (old-vs-fresh) J-lens comparisons
must never be presented as intervention evidence — only the explicit
grafted/alpha-zero-control conditions qualify; and any artifact-schema surprise
must trigger a stop-and-validate step before interpretation, a rule adopted
after an earlier query-mistake was initially misread as a data problem.

## Report drafting

The main synthesis report went through multiple full rewrites (report-draft.md →
report.md → report-synthesis.md) as its center of gravity shifted. An early
version over-indexed on the J-lens material; the user redirected to keep
ValueGraft's behavioral dissociation as the star and the lens as a secondary,
honestly-scoped corroborating instrument. Multi-pass adversarial review
(through-line, gaps, over-justification, accessibility, accuracy,
terminology-consistency) and fresh-eyes prose bake-offs (Fable 5 vs Opus 4.8)
were used repeatedly to tighten narrative and cut AI-typical over-justification.
The current published framing states the graft "reduces reinterpretation error
around facts the summary carried" rather than "recovers omitted facts" — a
deliberately narrowed, more defensible claim following the sparse-challenge
negative result.

effect-bound probe and the original gap-closure script on 30B data — until a
live reproduction confirms which is correct, the cross-architecture sweep, any
α-sweep/champion-tuning protocol, and reliance on the effect-bound probe's
earlier 27B result all remain suspended. Secondary open threads include the
three forward options for the agent-task line (harder chains, tau2, or
write-up-only), the K-vs-V mechanistic question for short-identifier-style
recovery (flagged as inadequately tested, queued for a future pre-registered
run), and further validation of the story-continuation and concept-readout task
designs, none of which had been executed at full scale as of this day's notes.
