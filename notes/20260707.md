_This was the most experimentally dense day yet in the ValueGraft project: the
synthetic chain-arm agent-task line was closed out (a real tuning win riding on
top of a compaction-damage ceiling that the task design couldn't expose), tau2
was piloted and then retired as structurally unable to force real eviction, and
the project's headline finding — that grafting recovers semantic sense/referent
while correctly doing nothing for stance — was established, corroborated across
two instruments, written up through several report iterations, and probed
extensively with a borrowed interpretability tool (the J-lens). That
interpretability work caught and fixed its own serious blind spot mid-day (early
J-lens demos never actually captured the grafted-intervention state, only a
pre-existing gap), and by day's end a new placebo-controlled measurement script
had produced numbers on 30B that contradict the already-published headline — an
unresolved discrepancy that froze all downstream scaling work pending a live
reproduction check._

## Chain-arm closure and tau2 retirement

The synthetic chain-exercise grid (4 seeds × 5 arms) finished with a real but
narrower result than hoped: the per-layer-tuned "champion" grafting
configuration passed 4/4 on every seed, including the seed where naive
full-strength (α=1.0) grafting had collapsed to 0/4 — confirming that
layer-tuning cures a genuine full-strength-grafting instability. But the
Compacted baseline also scored 4/4 on every seed, meaning these chains showed no
compaction damage at the binary pass/fail level for grafting to visibly repair.
The recall probe meant to catch subtler damage turned out to be invalid for this
tier (its hardcoded questions referenced constants from an earlier, unrelated
task type), and an effort-proxy metric showed no separation between conditions
either. With both auxiliary instruments ruled out, the chain-arm result was
closed as: real tuning fix, but no demonstrated damage-recovery on this task
set. All compute was shut down, and three forward options — building harder
cross-dependent chains, adopting tau2, or writing up on existing strength — were
left explicitly undecided pending user direction.

A parallel effort to stand up tau2 (`banking_knowledge` domain) succeeded
technically — harness, retrieval, and scoring path all integrated — but the
domain itself was diagnosed as structurally unusable: even after switching to a
capable simulator and lowering the compaction threshold to force eviction,
sessions stayed too short to create genuine early-content-evicted-later-needed
structure, and all arms scored reward 0.00 with no separation. This was recorded
as a scope-boundary finding about standard interactive benchmarks (F4) rather
than a project failure, and tau2 was formally retired.

## The headline finding and its corroboration

Re-analyzing the existing synthetic probe corpus through a "semantic richness"
lens rather than exact fact-recall produced the project's central result: at
30B, grafting recovers roughly +12pp on `sense` (disambiguation) and +10pp on
`referent` (recovering a specific evicted decision) relative to plain
compaction, while showing a near-null +2pp on `stance` (an evicted preference a
good summary already preserves). The effect size tracks where compaction
actually did damage — near zero where Compacted already scored ~93–96%,
substantial where Compacted dropped to 17–46% against a ~100% ceiling — read as
the signature of a real mechanism. This was corroborated by an independent,
judge-free teacher-forced logprob metric agreeing in direction (smaller
magnitude, as expected if grafting restores sense rather than verbatim wording),
and survived a stricter robustness cut. It did **not** replicate at 4B scale
(the pattern muddled, with stance showing the most apparent help), which is
recorded as an honest scope bound rather than suppressed.

## The J-lens interpretability track

A separate collaborator's handoff note introduced the Jacobian lens (J-lens), a
recently published interpretability readout that projects residual-stream
activations into a ranked vocabulary basis. This spawned an extensive, isolated
side investigation (kept out of the live pipeline throughout): qualitative demos
on toy, Pokémon-memory, and ordinary-conversation examples showing that the same
visible summary token has a different lens-readable neighborhood depending on
whether it was processed with the original context present ("write-time") or
freshly re-encoded after compaction; a next-token control establishing that this
signal is not simply next-token prediction (layer 48 was much more distinct from
ordinary continuation behavior than layer 62); and a full sweep of every summary
token across every fitted layer (1,175 tokens × 63 layers) confirming layer 48
as the point of maximum semantic separation.

Midway through, user-driven scrutiny surfaced a significant methodological gap:
none of this work had actually captured the grafted-intervention state — it only
compared two states (old-context vs fresh), which shows a state gap exists but
says nothing about whether ValueGraft closes it. This was treated as a real
process failure, not a footnote, and required rebuilding the cache-capture
machinery (Qwen3.6's hybrid architecture doesn't expose a standard KV cache) to
add a genuine four-condition probe: full context, fresh compacted, an alpha-zero
sanity control (which exactly reproduces fresh compaction, confirming the
machinery), the aligned graft, and a shifted-alignment control. On a single
coding example, aligned α=0.75 grafting moved layer-48 readouts closer to full
context and rescued a next-token argmax, while α=1.0 and the shifted control
produced argmax rescues without the internal improvement — establishing that
argmax rescue alone is not sufficient evidence of a good graft. Broader ordinary
and deliberately sparse-summary batches sharpened this into a more honest claim:
value grafting reduces _reinterpretation error_ around facts a summary still
names, but does not recover facts a summary aggressively omitted. A subsequent,
pre-registered free-generation divergence probe (looking for a clean internal
"fork" toward the correct concept) came back clean-negative (0 of 43 cases
forked cleanly toward the correct concept, 26 disconfirming), reinforcing the
lens's role as a low-resolution corroborator rather than a source of vivid
standalone exhibits.

## K/V axis, cross-architecture design, and cheap prospecting

A dedicated per-layer sweep found that key-grafting does not help
semantic-phrase referent recovery at any of 12 sampled layers — a clean
negative, closing that question for the tested regime — while a cross-check
against an independent agent's exploratory work left open, but unresolved, the
narrower possibility that key-grafting might help short-identifier-style
targets; this was deferred to a properly pre-registered follow-up after the
paper ships rather than pursued further now. Separately, cheap local 0.6B
screens (`referent_recovery_microtest`, then `policy_registry_followup`) went
through a design iteration: an initial exact-payload recovery task was too harsh
and returned uniformly negative for every graft policy, which was diagnosed as a
task-shape flaw rather than a refutation of the effect. A redesign into "private
policy label → familiar action" and "named low-entropy transform" families
produced a broad, stable, low-dose value-grafting win on the policy family (28
of 30 cases improved) and a narrower but stronger, distinctly key-sensitive win
concentrated in identifier/timezone-normalization cases within the transform
family — explicitly framed as noisy prospecting for future harness design, not
as evidence.

A cross-architecture generalization sweep was designed to port grafting across
model families (Qwen3.6-35B/27B, Qwen2.5-32B, Gemma-4-27B, Mistral-Small-4,
GLM-4.7-Flash, OLMo-2-32B; DeepSeek/Kimi excluded as architecturally blocked by
multi-head latent attention), with a confound fix holding summary text fixed
(generated by Sonnet, not a sweep member or a frontier model) across all models
being compared. This sweep did not launch — see below.

## Report drafting and process discipline

The main report went through several full iterations across the day
(`report-draft.md` → `report.md` → `report-synthesis.md`), converging on a
consistent structure: the compaction problem, the value-grafting intervention
with clarified terminology (ValueGraft as the family name, V-Graft for
values-only, KV-Graft for both channels with RoPE re-rotation on keys), the
sense/referent/stance dissociation as the centerpiece, concrete transcript
exhibits (a fabricated consultant name, a stated-but-uncitable preference,
referent confabulations), the tuning story, honest scale bounds (no 4B
replication), and the K/V and lens findings folded in as secondary
corroboration. This went through a deliberately structured multi-pass process —
adversarial critics for through-line, gaps, over-justification, and
accessibility, a fact-checking gate against raw artifacts, and Fable/Opus prose
bake-offs — established and reinforced as a standing practice for any future
shareable document. Terminology cleanup was flagged as incomplete: the word
"lens" was found used ambiguously across 76 references spanning three distinct
instruments (logit lens, tuned lens, J-lens).

## An interpretability tangent and repository hygiene

A separately scoped, isolated probe examined whether the model shows internal
signs of conflict when discussing a historically sensitive topic, explicitly
framed as behavioral/interpretability research rather than an attempt to bypass
any safety behavior, with file and path names deliberately kept generic. It
found no blanket refusal but a language- and framing-dependent narrative-routing
pattern (a Chinese-language framing redirected to a bland reform narrative;
English framings leaned toward official-stability language; other historical
analogs were answered directly), and a companion raw logit-lens comparison
showed the older, simpler technique already recovers much of the same signal —
so the J-lens's contribution here is clarity, not unique access. Results are
qualitative and heavily caveated as exploratory rather than causal.

Unrelated repository hygiene work moved stale top-level planning documents into
a dated `docs/` archive, fixed a six-hour git-trailer misattribution (Opus work
briefly stamped as Fable across a quota-driven handoff) via an append-only
correction note rather than history rewriting, and reinforced standing
operational rules: verify a job reached real computation (not just launched)
before trusting it; keep monitors silent except on completion/error/state-change
with increasing backoff; and choose subagent models by task fitness (Opus by
default, Sonnet for genuine perspective diversity, Fable for prose quality with
minimal context) rather than cost.

## Outstanding risks and next actions

The most urgent open item is a late-day discrepancy: a new placebo-controlled
"effect-bound" measurement script, run on 30B, produced gap-closure numbers (37%
of cases helped) that contradict the already-published headline (64%/81% helped)
on what should be identical cases and settings. The original paper-producing
script was confirmed to still reproduce its own published numbers, so the
leading hypothesis is a bug in the new script rather than a collapse of the
primary effect — but this is unconfirmed. A live reproduction run was launched
and all downstream work (the cross-architecture sweep, further reliance on the
effect-bound probe, and its earlier 27B "content-specific" finding) was
explicitly frozen pending its result; resolving this is the clear first priority
for the next session. Beyond that: the chain-arm agent-task line has three
undecided forward paths and no chosen direction; the
key-grafting-for-identifiers question and a story-continuation contradiction
task (built but unrun) remain queued; and the "lens" terminology disambiguation
across the report corpus is still incomplete.
