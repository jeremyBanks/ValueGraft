_The ValueGraft project consolidated its main behavioral result while sharply
narrowing the mechanistic claim: moderate value grafting can reduce semantic
reinterpretation after compaction, but it does not reliably recover relations
omitted from the summary. Parallel validation exposed an unresolved evaluator
discrepancy, paused cross-model expansion, and left the repository in a
documentation-and-audit phase._

**Participants/contributors:** Jeremy Banks (user); `claude-fable-5`;
`claude-opus-4-8`; `claude-sonnet-5`; and `gpt-5.5-xhigh` (GPT-5.5 with xhigh
reasoning effort). Source notes also attribute one synthetic-harness proposal to
Gemini Pro 3.1, but do not identify it as an active conversation participant.

## Research and workflow developments

The primary behavioral evidence remains strongest at the larger-model scale:

- On the tested 30B model, value grafting improved sense and referent judgments
  while leaving stance effectively unchanged, with roughly 10–12 percentage
  points of recovery in the damaged semantic categories.
- Exact-token teacher-forced scores moved in the same direction but more weakly,
  supporting recovery of meaning rather than verbatim wording.
- The sense/stance dissociation replicated on the tested 27B model; referent
  recovery was strong at 30B but essentially absent on 27B. The 4B result
  preserved the honesty/fabrication effect but not the semantic dissociation.
- Full-strength grafting can destabilize behavior. Per-layer tuning eliminated
  the observed collapse and recovered the champion chain result, but the current
  chains were too robust to demonstrate compaction damage.

Tau2 banking integration was completed but retired as a confirmatory benchmark:
natural sessions were too short to reach meaningful compaction, while forced
compaction evicted little useful context and all arms scored 0.00. The agent
track therefore remains unresolved. A harder cross-dependent chain or a better
benchmark is needed before spending on confirmation.

The J-lens work matured from an old-context-versus-fresh diagnostic into a
controlled intervention study. The valid comparison is full context, fresh
compaction, aligned value-grafted compaction, and shifted-value control, with
alpha-zero and alpha sweeps. The Qwen3.6-27B hybrid cache required explicit
support for recurrent/linear-attention state and provenance recording.
Validation confirmed aligned token counts, graft availability, alpha-zero
equality with fresh compaction, and changed cache layers.

The broad J-lens sweep covered 1,175 summary tokens across 63 layers and 74,025
token-layer rows. Layer 48 showed the strongest write-time/fresh separation
while remaining relatively distinct from ordinary next-token probabilities;
layer 62 was more readable but substantially more continuation-like. Span-first
reporting is now preferred over raw token ranking because punctuation,
formatting, and subword fragments dominate many high-divergence rows.

Controlled intervention probes found small aligned shifts toward full-context
readouts, worse aggregate behavior for shifted grafts, and better internal
closure at moderate alpha than at alpha 1.0. The sparse challenge produced
near-zero recovery when the summary omitted the relation itself. Free-generation
probing found 0 decisive forks toward full context, 17 subtle leans, and 26
disconfirming cases, reinforcing the view that J-lens effects are low-resolution
corroboration rather than vivid causal demonstrations.

K/V experiments further scoped the mechanism. V-only grafting was the operative
axis for semantic-phrase tasks; uniform K-only grafting was negative or
unhelpful. Correct RoPE re-rotation was validated, but a weak 0.6B prospecting
screen found possible K-favorable behavior for short identifier-like targets.
This is too small and selection-biased to support a general key claim, but it
motivates a narrowly defined future identifier-morphology experiment.

## Persistent decisions and terminology

- ValueGraft names the intervention family; V-Graft means fresh keys with
  blended write-time values; K-only and KV variants must be named separately.
- Keys provide addressability and values provide retrieved content; moved keys
  require positional re-rotation.
- J-lens reads residual-stream state after cache influence. It does not read the
  KV cache directly, does not equal task success, and should be treated
  primarily as hypothesis-generating or corroborative evidence.
- The defensible claim is reduced reinterpretation error around facts retained
  in the summary, not recovery of facts the summary discarded.
- “Semantic continuity,” “sense,” “referent,” and “stance” should remain
  distinct outcome categories.
- Alpha-one results must not be treated as the default. Moderate or per-layer
  doses are safer, and shifted/wrong-context controls are required.
- Artifact schemas must be inspected and validated before analysis; surprising
  query output is not evidence of malformed data.
- Reports should foreground the behavioral result, keep the interpretability
  tangent proportionate, and place process incidents and rejected approaches in
  internal worklogs.

## Empirical caveats and repository state

A newer placebo-controlled effect-bound probe conflicts with the trusted F1
evaluator. The newer 27B result was small with a confidence interval crossing
zero; its 30B result was negative or null depending on window length, and
recomputed help rates contradicted the original 30B results. The saved F1
artifacts reproduce the published values exactly, so the discrepancy is
currently treated as an implementation or teacher-forcing mismatch. The original
F1 evaluator must be rerun and compared line by line before accepting the new
bound, repairing it, or extending across architectures.

The polished report was finalized, README-promoted, and pushed. It presents
ValueGraft as the primary contribution and J-lens as secondary evidence, with
explicit limits on model mismatch, scale, omitted-fact recovery, and agent-task
validation. The repository was cleaned and historical documents moved under
`docs/`; scientific artifacts remain available as an audit trail, while
oversized raw probe files remain local/ignored.

An exploratory Qwen topic-sensitivity audit found language- and
framing-dependent narrative routing rather than a simple refusal pattern. Its
token alignment and candidate-scoring limitations were documented, repaired
where possible, and kept separate from the ValueGraft claims. This remains an
interpretability tangent, not evidence about a general safety mechanism.

Cheap 0.6B task-shape searches identified two possible future lanes: private
policy labels mapped to familiar actions, which were broadly V-sensitive, and
short identifier/normalization transforms, which showed the most promising but
noisy K-sensitive cases. These are prospecting results only and should not be
promoted without preregistered policies, fixed summaries, larger-model
validation, and full-surface reporting.

## Outstanding risks and next actions

- Reconcile the original F1 evaluator with the newer placebo-controlled probe;
  pause cross-architecture work until the trusted apparatus is established.
- Preserve the paper’s current scoped claim unless the reconciliation changes
  the evidence materially.
- If proceeding, use a shared fixed summary across models, report pre-graft gaps
  and normalized closure, include placebo and positive-direction controls, and
  treat per-model results as directional.
- Consider a purpose-built benchmark with genuine eviction, cross-exercise
  dependencies, and enough task capability to expose recoverable damage.
- Keep future K-focused work limited to identifier-like or normalization
  targets, with low-dose policies and preregistered analysis.
- Maintain launch verification, resolved-model checks, GPU validation, unique
  output paths, quiet backoff monitors, and prompt pod shutdown after
  validation.
