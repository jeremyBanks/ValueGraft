The ValueGraft project summaries (notes/20260705.md, notes/README.md) describe a
07-05 "reframing" from mechanism-first to mitigation-first driven by external
review. The owner (2026-07-09) corrected this: mitigation was their framing from
the start; "write-time state differs from re-encoded text as a finding" was the
agents' misunderstanding, unnoticed by the owner for a while.

**Why:** The generated summaries embed the agents' contemporaneous confusion as
if it were project history; narrating it that way misattributes the owner's
design intent and frustrates them.

**How to apply:** Never describe the project as having "started mechanism-first
and been corrected by review." Additive correction lives at
notes/2026070901-framing-provenance-correction.md and is pointed to from
AGENTS.md. More generally: generated summaries reflect agent belief at the
time, not necessarily owner intent — when the owner corrects the record, write
a dated additive note, don't rewrite the summaries. Related: [[validate-before-trusting]].
