# Independent paired-v11 design review

**Reviewer:** GPT-5.6 Sol, extra-high reasoning effort (`corrected_assay_design`)  
**Preserved by:** Sol — GPT-5.6 Sol, extra-high reasoning effort  
**Date:** 2026-07-11

## Recommendation

Do not repair c02/c10 or use the capped banked 30B text as the primary corpus.
Build a new, concise, fully authored twelve-conversation paired corpus and freeze
all twelve before any model outcome. The inherited c02/c10 defects are broad
enough that symmetric repair would effectively create new conversations. The
banked 30B text has severe cap incidence and lacks native-cache and exact runtime
provenance after replay.

The conversation body does not need to be subject-native for the proposed causal
estimand. Both `P` and `O` replay imported tokens. The exact object claimed as
write-time state is the summary K/V freely generated by the exact subject after
one explicit replay schedule. A genuinely live subject-generated conversation
is a separate ecological/agent evaluation.

## Corpus requirements

- Twelve independent conversations, each with one referent and one overloaded-
  sense plant.
- One correct, one referent-specific minimally counterfactual, and one sense-
  specific minimally counterfactual literal history per conversation.
- Complete natural replies, roughly 5,000–8,000 canonical source-prefix tokens,
  varied domains and structures, and no clipping.
- Plants established in the evicted block; retained tail coherent under all
  variants and non-revealing.
- Exact roles, message counts, per-message token widths, message boundaries,
  P/O schedules, summary positions, and unchanged non-focal bytes.
- Blind randomized whole-history review, target-aware paired review, mechanical
  validation, diversity audit, and disagreement adjudication before freezing.

These requirements were adopted in
`COHERENT-STATE-PAIRED-V11-CORPUS-CONTRACT.md`.

## Proposed source and destination conditions

- `P = turn_aligned_replay`, derived from every exact canonical message start;
  only an individual message longer than 4,096 may be internally split.
- `O = ordinary_4096` over the identical complete source prefix.
- `D = gapped_destination_canonical`, the fixed compact physical destination
  with summary rows at their original logical positions and retained-tail state
  causally recomputed after insertion.

For each case, freely generate one greedy summary under correct-history P and
persist it before semantic scoring. Force the identical IDs through correct O,
both focal counterfactual histories under P and O, and the fresh D encoding.
Generated P and forced P must replay deterministically. P and O are distinct
conditions, not an equality gate.

## Proposed outcomes

Let `Y` be the correct-target minus counterfactual-target mean token-log-
probability margin. At conversation level:

```text
GF_i,s = mean_p [Y_i,p(C_s) - Y_i,p(F)]
GMC_i,s = mean_p [Y_i,p(C_s) - Y_i,p(W_p,s)]
```

Also score every focal counterfactual on the unchanged plant. A focal-selectivity
contrast subtracts that non-focal disturbance from the focal `C-W` change. A
positive GMC without selectivity supports generic history-conditioned disruption,
not target-specific semantic state.

Report P/O interactions, `W-F`, forced-summary NLL, correct and counterfactual
log-probability components, full-history competence/headroom, and whether
correct-target log probability itself improves. A larger margin alone does not
necessarily mean less loss.

The reviewer proposed requiring positive lower 95% conversation-level intervals
for GF and GMC under both P and O. Sol has not yet frozen that multiplicity/claim
rule: a separate adversarial statistical review will decide whether P is the
deployment-primary condition with O as sensitivity, or whether dual-schedule
intersection is co-primary. No outcome exists while that decision remains open.

## Execution proposal

The smallest local diagnostic uses the first two already-frozen corpus members
on pinned Qwen3-0.6B bf16 eager CPU and exercises every source/destination/plant
cell. Its only valid verdict is technical completeness or invalidity, never
efficacy or schedule equivalence.

The exact confirmatory subject remains
`Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507` revision
`0d7cf23991f47feeb3a57ecb4c9cee8ea4a17bfe`, bf16, eager, temperature zero.
One technical-only canary precedes the sealed N=12 run. Every generated summary,
trace, row hash/state artifact, arm lineage, raw target score, and analysis input
must be durable and committed. No semantic early stopping or post-outcome case
replacement is allowed.

The review estimated roughly 6–14 A100 hours and $8–$20 from the prior observed
rate, with a $25 compute hard cap and canary-based forecast. This is a planning
estimate, not an observed runtime or price.

## Stop conditions

Stop before semantic scoring for model/revision/dtype/backend mismatch, P
derivation failure, nondeterministic same-schedule repeats, generated/forced P
replay failure, decoded-review failure, summary cap/no-EOS/special tokens,
position or lineage failure, persistence failure, or engineered-control failure.

After scoring starts, do not change cases, targets, schedules, thresholds, or
inclusion rules. Hardware interruption may resume only from bound durable
artifacts. An incomplete N=12 run is descriptive, not confirmatory.

## Implementation boundary

Create a new additive `coherent-state-paired-v11` namespace. Reuse stable cache
row extraction/insertion, hashing, gapped-D, and target-scoring primitives. Do
not reuse `matched_wrong_prefix_ids`, old donor constants, old wrong-arm names,
v10 store schemas, or v10 release contracts. The independent harvester must
rederive P from complete rendered bytes rather than import the runner's boundary
constructor.
