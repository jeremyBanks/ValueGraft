_This UTC day carried the compaction-mitigation project from a completed 4B
pilot through a full mechanism-to-mitigation reframing, parallel 4B/30B
experimental campaigns, a multi-pod cloud scale-up, a prior-art investigation
into provider compaction APIs, and a paper draft — closing with a real
coding-agent evaluation launched but still unresolved._

## Reframing and restructuring

The day's most consequential development was methodological rather than
empirical. An external review (GPT-5.5, read-only) argued that the project's
original headline question — "does write-time KV cache state differ from
re-encoded text?" — is close to self-evident and not by itself a contribution.
The user validated this as a genuine correction of misfocus, not merely a
rephrasing, and redirected the project toward a mitigation-first question: can a
small, practical cache-state intervention reduce the behavioral damage that
text-only compaction causes? This produced a standing rule, revisited more than
once during the day, that **compaction damage itself is an assumed baseline used
to normalize mitigation results, not a finding to report** — any document
phrasing it as a result needed rewriting.

Following this, the user issued a two-phase restructuring: Phase 1 closes out
the already-in-flight 30B run at a clean stopping point without expanding the
old arm matrix; Phase 2 designs and runs one narrow, clean mitigation contrast
rather than a broad sweep. The chosen Phase 2 design, H-pack vs. B-min-pack,
isolates write-time vs. fresh-time encoding of an identical packed summary,
scored primarily on fabrication-vs-admission over unknowable content, with a
wrong-summary donor control.

## Terminology and arm naming

Several naming and framing conventions were fixed for future write-ups: prefer
"value states carry context-conditioned information" over "meaning lives in KV";
distinguish value **blending** (α≤1) from value **steering/extrapolation** (α>1,
analogized to classifier-free guidance); and, in paper drafts specifically,
rename H-pack→**ValueGraft-Pack**, B-min-pack→**FreshPack**, and promote
"ValueGraft" to an umbrella family name covering both Pack and
**ValueGraft-Blend** (the alpha-blended arm). Repo code and internal arm names
were left untouched — renaming applies only inside an isolated `paper-working/`
synthesis directory. A separate git-hygiene rule was fixed given concurrent
multi-agent activity in the repo: never stage speculatively or with
`add -A`/`add .`; always add-and-commit new files by explicit pathspec, verified
against the staged diff first.

## Empirical results (4B and 30B, both provisional/pilot-scale)

- **4B pilot (closed out):** no probe-accuracy mitigation on leakage-clean
  interpretation probes — referent resolution floors for every compacted arm.
  But write-time encoding measurably matters: H-gap beats B-min on continuation
  NLL (+0.093 nats, 10/12 convos), light value grafting helps (E-post α=0.25,
  +0.08 closure), and grafted values shift word-sense margins in an isolated
  micro-test. An unplanned finding — the "honesty effect" — showed
  retained/grafted-state arms tend to admit ignorance of evicted facts where
  text-only compaction fabricates confidently.
- **30B replication:** the encoding advantage strengthens with scale (H-gap
  +0.128 nats, 12/12); the alpha dose-response **inverts** — full-strength
  grafting (α=1), harmful at 4B, becomes net-positive at 30B (+0.052 nats, ~29%
  gap closure); gapped retention (arm C), harmful at 4B, becomes roughly
  neutral. The honesty effect strengthens further at 30B (near-total fabrication
  suppression). None of the synthetic-corpus effects replicate on free-form
  natural conversations, where compaction barely hurts continuation quality at
  30B in the first place.
- **Phase 2 (packed write-time summary vs. fabrication, both scales):** the
  packed layout itself (bare sinks + summary, no template) induces most of the
  honesty gain; write-time encoding adds a further component **that grows with
  scale** (matched-pair decoy fabrication 4B: 4→3, essentially nil; 30B: 10→3,
  substantial).
- **Tuned ValueGraft (validation→holdout alpha sweep):** best configuration
  differs qualitatively by scale — 4B favors mid-layer-band gating at low α
  (+0.017, ~10% gap closure); 30B favors a global, much higher α (~0.75–1.0,
  tolerating extrapolation past 1.0) with no gating needed (+0.033, ~24%
  closure). A dedicated per-layer/per-head profiling investigation found real
  structure only on the layer axis at 4B (mid-depth band, peak ~L17); an
  apparent per-head signal did **not** survive a pre-registered holdout test and
  was logged as a validated null / overfitting artifact.
- **LongMemEval (standard benchmark, real multi-session data):** full-context
  accuracy (71–81%) collapses to ≤11% under every compacted arm at both scales —
  no method recovers evicted recall, an honest null. The honesty/fabrication
  benefit is **frame-dependent**: it holds clearly at 4B (~35% fabrication
  reduction) but nearly vanishes at 30B on this benchmark's personal-QA framing,
  because the larger model's own refusal calibration already covers most of the
  gap there — in contrast to the project's own agentic-style synthetic corpus,
  where the 30B baseline still fabricates heavily and the intervention cuts it
  substantially. This narrows the deployment claim to mid-task, agentic-style
  compaction rather than retrieval-style personal QA.
- **SWE-Gym real coding-agent trajectories:** a modest but statistically clean
  effect (+0.0156 nats, 45/75 wins, 95% CI [0.005, 0.027], ~10% gap closure),
  accepted by the user as a reasonable outcome given the instrument (off-policy
  trajectory replay, exact-token scoring) is an unusually strict lower bound.

## Cloud scale-up and prior-art work

A multi-pod RunPod campaign (bf16 Qwen3-30B-A3B on CUDA, prepaid-credit budget
guardrails, ~$100 authorized) was launched to run full-scale LongMemEval, real
SWE-Gym coding traces, and a planned second standard benchmark, correcting an
early planning drift toward model-family diversity back toward the user's actual
goal: scaling evidence volume against established benchmarks on the known model,
not swapping models for their own sake. A deployment-overhead analysis
quantified that a SelfGist-style summary-state sidecar is tens of MiB (small
relative to a full context cache but far larger than the text it replaces) and
sketched an "opaque compaction handle" API framing as more realistic than raw
client-side KV transport. Separately, a deep prior-art investigation into
provider compaction mechanisms (OpenAI Responses API's opaque `compact`
endpoint, Anthropic's beta compaction block, Gemini's thought signatures) found
no public confirmation that any provider uses this project's specific
value-tensor mechanism, narrowing the project's defensible novelty claim to the
mitigation experiment and its evaluation rather than the general idea of opaque
or editable KV state.

## Outstanding risks and next actions

At day's end, the decisive open test — real coding-agent task success through an
OpenAI-compatible serving shim (`serve_shim.py`) with server-side compaction and
value-grafting — was actively running but unresolved. Several hygiene items
remain unaddressed: an unreconciled discrepancy between code comments and actual
behavior in the summarization scope of the serving shim and full-HF LongMemEval
runner; an unverified equivalence between the HF and MLX implementations of the
H-gap suffix; and generated pod-lifecycle state and non-atomic result writes
flagged by a cloud-stack review but not yet fixed. Repository disorganization
was explicitly acknowledged and deliberately deferred rather than cleaned up
mid-pipeline. Publication channel was decided as a private GitHub repository
only, with no arXiv or Zenodo submission planned.
