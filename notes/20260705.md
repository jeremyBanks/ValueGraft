_The project shifted from demonstrating cache-state non-equivalence to
evaluating practical mitigation for compaction damage. Completed 4B/30B
experiments show that write-time state can reduce fabrication and modestly
improve continuation quality, but has not restored reliable recall of evicted
information._

**Participants/contributors:** User; `claude-fable-5`; `claude-sonnet-5`;
`gpt-5.5-high` (high reasoning effort); and `gpt-5.5-xhigh` (xhigh reasoning
effort).

Phase 1 and the mitigation-focused Phase 2 are complete at 4B and 30B. The
cleanest deployable comparison is `ValueGraft-Pack`/H-pack versus fresh
packed-summary encoding (`B-min-pack`): identical text and positions, differing
in whether summary KV entries were written in the original context.
`ValueGraft-Blend`/E blends old values into freshly encoded keys, with
model-specific alpha tuning. The preferred framing is that cache-state
interventions may reduce semantic damage from text-only compaction, not that
“meaning lives in values.”

Key results:

- H-pack sharply reduced fabrication on synthetic unknowable and decoy probes.
  At 30B, decoy fabrication fell from 10/24 with fresh packed encoding to 3/24
  with write-time encoding; evicted-fact fabrication fell from 5/24 to 1/24.
  Much of the overall caution came from the packed layout itself, and
  wrong-conversation write-time state was also highly abstention-prone, so this
  is stronger evidence for reduced confabulation than for recovered memory.
- ValueGraft-Blend improved continuation likelihood on compaction-sensitive
  material: approximately 10% gap closure at 4B and 24% at 30B on held-out
  conversations. The optimal dose differed by scale; high alpha harmed at 4B but
  was beneficial at 30B. On 75 real coding-agent trajectories, the tuned graft
  recovered +0.0156 nats/token, about 10% of measured loss, with 45/75 wins.
  This remains an off-policy proxy because trajectories were generated by
  another agent/model.
- Probe accuracy for referents and evicted facts remained near floor across
  compacted arms at both scales. The interventions therefore improve
  continuation quality or abstention more clearly than legitimate recall.
  Natural conversations showed little recoverable compaction damage, limiting
  measurable gains.
- LongMemEval validation reproduced severe recall loss: full-context accuracy
  was 71% at 4B and 81% at 30B, while compacted variants were at most 11%
  correct. H-pack reduced fabrication at 4B, but not at 30B, where
  personal-question framing already elicited frequent refusal. Real-data
  evaluation must therefore separate answerable-item accuracy from
  fabrication/admission behavior.
- Negative controls using shuffled or wrong-conversation values caused large
  likelihood and accuracy collapses, supporting content and alignment
  specificity. Identity ladders and re-rotation checks passed, including the
  packed-layout HP-0 check. Same-shape comparisons remain necessary because
  sequence-length-dependent 4-bit numerical noise was observed.

Terminology was consolidated around `ValueGraft-Pack` and `ValueGraft-Blend`;
“cached value vectors,” “cached attention state,” and “KV-cache value tensors”
are preferred. Exact token alignment and fresh-key/old-value compatibility are
methodological constraints. Adaptive or per-layer/per-head gating remains
exploratory; the validated 4B profile showed useful signal concentrated around
middle layers and late-layer grafting was harmful. Alpha values above one should
be described as extrapolative steering, not ordinary blending.

The deployment concept is an opaque, provider-side compaction handle rather than
a raw client-uploaded KV blob. A 30B fp16 cache is roughly 96 KiB/token, so a
100–500-token retained summary costs about 9–47 MiB: small relative to the full
cache but too large for ordinary stateless requests. Any practical interface
would require model/tokenizer/template binding, expiration, revocation, privacy
and audit controls, and explicit disclosure that latent state supplements
visible summary text.

Open questions and next actions are to finish reconciling cloud results and
scoring artifacts, complete the real coding-agent end-to-end evaluation, assess
standard-benchmark fit and dataset licensing at execution time, and draft the
evidence-led technical report. The central unresolved question is whether
reduced fabrication reflects calibrated uncertainty or generalized refusal;
future evaluation must show retained accuracy on answerable and tail-visible
items while preserving abstention on evicted and never-discussed facts. The
combination of H-pack and ValueGraft-Blend remains untested, as do G/SoftGraft,
broader model families, and serialization across requests or hosts.
