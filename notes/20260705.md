## Research developments

The 4B pilot and 30B replication are complete. The central finding is
conditional: write-time cached attention state carries context-conditioned
information that fresh summary re-encoding does not reproduce, but simple cache
interventions generally do not recover answer accuracy on leakage-clean referent
or evicted-fact probes.

The strongest result is an honesty-related effect. On unknowable evicted facts
and never-discussed decoys, ordinary compaction frequently produced confident
plausible inventions, while SelfGist/H-pack-style write-time summary state
substantially increased admission of uncertainty. At 30B, packed write-time
summaries reduced decoy fabrication from 10/24 to 3/24 relative to the matched
fresh packed summary, and evicted-fact fabrication from 5/24 to 1/24. The
matched controls show that packed layout itself induces caution; the additional
write-time-encoding effect is real but scale-dependent. This should be described
as reduced fabrication or increased admission, not yet as calibrated honesty.

ValueGraft produced a smaller but measurable continuation benefit on
compaction-dependent material. Tuned holdout gains were approximately 10% gap
closure at 4B and 24% at 30B. The preferred alpha differed by scale:
low/mid-band blending near 0.25 at 4B, versus approximately 0.75 globally at
30B. A 75-trajectory coding-agent replay also showed a modest gain of +0.0156
nats/token, with 45/75 wins, while remaining off-policy and proxy-based.

Standard-data validation reinforced the boundary. In the local LongMemEval
subset, full-context accuracy was 71% at 4B and 81% at 30B, while compacted
variants reached at most 11% correct. H-pack reduced fabrication at 4B but not
at 30B, where personal-fact questions already elicited frequent refusal. No
tested intervention demonstrated reliable recovery of evicted recall.

The completed phase-2 contrast established that H-pack versus B-min-pack is the
clean deployable comparison: identical packed tokens and positions, differing
only in write-time versus fresh encoding. H-pack and related variants improved
admission behavior but left referent and sense recall near floor.
Wrong-conversation and shuffled-value controls degraded sharply, supporting
content- and alignment-specificity rather than generic smoothing.

Cloud execution expanded toward higher-n validation on Qwen3-30B in bf16,
standard benchmark material, and real coding-agent traces. Operational work
included a CUDA/Hugging Face port, identity-ladder validation, resumable cloud
runners, budgeted A100 execution, and a serving shim exposing compaction and
ValueGraft modes. An end-to-end coding task succeeded in an initial E0 run; an
E1 OpenHands run remained active, so practical task-resolution evidence was not
yet complete.

## Decisions and terminology to preserve

- Frame the contribution as testing whether compact cache-state interventions
  reduce behavioral damage from text-only compaction. The non-equivalence of
  fresh and write-time activations is a mechanism premise, not the headline
  contribution.
- Use `ValueGraft-Pack` for retained, packed write-time summary state (formerly
  H-pack/SelfGist) and `ValueGraft-Blend` for fresh-key/old-value blending
  (formerly ValueGraft/E). Preserve aliases in implementation notes where
  needed.
- Prefer “cached value vectors,” “cached attention state,” and “KV-cache value
  tensors.” Avoid metaphysical claims that meaning resides in values.
- Probe accuracy, stratified by category and leakage class, is primary.
  Continuation NLL and gap closure are secondary fluency/coherence measures.
- Keep `absent-from-summary` and `evicted-only` cases central. Summary leakage,
  fabricated-but-plausible answers, and judge artifacts must remain separately
  reported.
- Treat H-pack’s fabrication reduction as a calibration hypothesis requiring
  answerable-item accuracy checks. More admissions alone do not establish better
  honesty.
- Global alpha remains the primary reported ValueGraft-Blend method. Layer/head
  gating and adaptive attention-confidence gates are exploratory and require
  validation/holdout discipline.
- Deployment is “compaction as a choice”: the original cache must still exist
  during compaction. A provider-side opaque compaction handle is a possible
  product shape; raw KV transport, serialization, expiry, security, and
  model/template binding remain unresolved.

## Risks and next actions

The main scientific risks are abstention being mistaken for honesty, weak recall
being hidden by fabrication metrics, benchmark framing effects, small pilot
sample sizes, one model family, 4-bit local quantization, and off-policy
trajectory evaluation. Natural conversations contain little recoverable
compaction damage, so synthetic gains should not be generalized to ordinary
chat.

Immediate work is to finish judging and scoring active cloud runs, verify
atomic/resumable result handling and benchmark semantics, complete the E1
end-to-end coding evaluation, and keep `STATE.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, and cloud
documentation synchronized. Future evaluation should test answerable LongMemEval
items, tail-visible controls, evicted facts, and decoys in one calibration
matrix.

Further open questions include whether packed write-time state improves
answerable real-world memory tasks, whether ValueGraft-Pack and ValueGraft-Blend
compose, whether effects survive bf16 and larger samples, and whether
coding-agent compaction improves actual retry and rule-following behavior rather
than only teacher-forced likelihood. G/SoftGraft, hybrid attention models,
broader model diversity, and full task-resolution benchmarks remain follow-ups
rather than established results.
