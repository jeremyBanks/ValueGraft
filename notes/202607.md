# ValueGraft Notes: 2026-07

_Over six days (2026-07-04 through 2026-07-09 UTC) the ValueGraft project moved
from a mechanistic question about KV-cache continuity through a deliberate
reframing toward deployable mitigation, scaled from a single local 4B/30B pilot
into a multi-pod cloud campaign spanning standard benchmarks and real
coding-agent traces, produced and published a central positive finding — then
watched that finding, and a second independent honesty result, both fail
rigorous bf16/precision reproduction checks within the same week, forcing a full
precision-provenance audit and leaving the project's cornerstone claim genuinely
undecided at month's end._

## Origins: mechanism, then a deliberate reframe to mitigation

The project began (07-04) with a firm experiment brief testing whether retaining
write-time KV cache state across a context-compaction boundary preserves
measurable semantic continuity that text-identical recomputation loses. An MLX
harness on Apple Silicon (Qwen3-4B for development, Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507
for production) was validated through a full identity-test ladder before any
experimental arm ran — a discipline that caught three real bugs before they
could contaminate results and became the project's recurring justification for
"test the machinery before trusting the output." A synthetic+natural probe
corpus was built and contamination-repaired, and the initial arm set (A–E, later
F–H) was defined.

By 07-05, external review (including a read-only session with GPT-5.5) converged
on a correction that reshaped everything downstream: "write-time KV state
differs from re-encoded text" is close to self-evident and not itself a
contribution, and later, more sharply, "compaction destroys context-conditioned
state" is not a finding at all but an assumed baseline against which
interventions must be scored. The project adopted a mitigation-first claim
hierarchy — primary: does a small intervention reduce compaction-induced errors
on leakage-clean probes vs. a text-summary baseline; secondary: mechanism;
boundary: costs/limits — and this hierarchy remained load-bearing for the rest
of the month. (A later correction on 07-09 clarified that this reframing was the
owner's original intent, misread by agents as a pivot, not an actual
course-change driven by review.) The same day produced the project's first
genuinely positive empirical pattern: at 30B, write-time-encoded summary state
sharply suppressed fabrication on unknowable post-compaction questions relative
to text-only compaction, and a tuned value-blending intervention (α-sweep, held
to strict validation→holdout discipline) flipped from harmful at 4B to
net-positive at 30B. A public repo, paper drafts, and a RunPod-based multi-pod
cloud campaign were all launched the same day to scale this pattern onto
standard benchmarks (LongMemEval) and real coding traces (SWE-Gym).

## Scaling up, and the pivot to agentic coding evaluation

LongMemEval at full scale (07-06) returned a clean null — no separation between
compaction/graft arms on fact-retrieval accuracy — matching what local data had
already predicted, and the project judged further investment there inefficient.
Effort redirected to an "E-track": an OpenAI-compatible shim serving compaction
and value-grafting server-side, scored objectively on real coding tasks (pytest
pass/fail, process metrics, a recall-dissociation probe), run across a pod fleet
that grew from four to nine. This pivot came with real cost: a session-state
leak from an assumption of one-task-per-pod bled cache state across arms once
lanes were shared, forcing a clean rerun with disjoint task lanes and a hard
split between `results/agent_clean_run/` and a documented quarantine directory.
More seriously, the project discovered that "real agent"/"real coding tasks"
language had for roughly twelve hours actually referred to a synthetic harness
while genuine SWE-bench rows queued behind it — a trust rupture that produced a
standing rule still in force: every results statement must name its task source
(synthetic vs. standard benchmark) inline. The day also surfaced a **capability
floor**: the subject model itself, not compaction, was often the binding
constraint on live SWE-bench-Lite/Verified tasks, which forced both a
scaffold-configuration audit (native tool-calling was disabled, decoding was
hardcoded to greedy across all arms) and a shift toward vendor-benchmark
difficulty as an external calibration signal ("difficulty certificate" rule).

By 07-07 the synthetic agent-task line (the "chain" experiment) closed on a
ceiling effect — grafting fixed a real instability but the underlying tasks
showed no detectable compaction damage to repair at the pass/fail level — and
tau-bench integration was retired after its most promising domain proved
structurally too short to create eviction pressure. Both closures were recorded
as scope-boundary findings, not project failures, and compute for both was shut
down in favor of the report-writing and reproduction work described below.

## The central finding, its refinement, and its unraveling

07-07 produced the project's headline result via a reframing of the evaluation
itself: rather than scoring exact fact recall, existing outputs were re-judged
for **meaning recovery** across three probe categories — **sense**
(disambiguation), **referent** (a specific prior decision/fact), and **stance**
(a preference or disposition) — which became the standard analytic lens for the
rest of the month, replacing the coarser fact-recall framing. At 30B,
value-grafting recovered meaningful sense/referent-type meaning relative to
plain compaction with a correctly-null effect on stance (where plain summaries
already preserve disposition), corroborated by an independent judge-free metric.
This was written up, reviewed, and published to the repo as the primary README
document the same day — and almost immediately complicated by a late-discovered
discrepancy: a new placebo-controlled internal-effect measurement produced a
headline number sharply below the published figure on identical inputs, later
traced (07-08) not to architectural nondeterminism but to a Cauchy-unstable
mean-of-ratio estimator, (E−B)/(A−B), blowing up near zero denominators. On
robust metrics (raw E−B, %-helped, bootstrap CI) the original qualitative
dissociation held, and a related "keys actively hurt" claim was retracted as the
same estimator artifact — keys are neutral, value is the operative axis. Robust
metrics were locked in project-wide.

That resolution proved temporary. A second, more consequential design flaw
surfaced the same day (07-08): the value-graft effect only reproduces when the
tested model generates its own summary at the compaction boundary, not when a
semantically equivalent externally supplied summary is grafted in — the effect
depends on the model's own act of summarization, not mere availability of
full-context information. This forced every cross-architecture sweep design
toward self-generated ("matched-scaffold, model-filled") summaries and replies,
since the original corpus's assistant replies had been authored by a single
model family, risking a nativeness confound rather than a true
attention-geometry signal. Then, on 07-09, disciplined re-checks collapsed the
finding further: a fresh render of the same twelve conversations dropped the
judged-sense recovery from a reported +12pp to +1.0pp (CI spanning zero) —
roughly half attributable to judge recalibration, half to render-to-render
variance alone, which turned out to be roughly the same magnitude as the effect
itself. A parallel deep precision/provenance audit found both headline results
(meaning-recovery and the honesty/anti-fabrication effect) had actually been
scored 4-bit-only, with unscored bf16 data sitting on disk, and that the tested
honesty intervention was materially different from the paper's named value-only
method — a follow-up control showed the effect was driven by the packed
retention layout itself, not by content match, since substituting another
conversation's state suppressed fabrication just as effectively. A related
overclaim (38/48 evicted-fact recall) also failed to reproduce (true recall:
0/24 at both scales). The one confirmed bf16, value-only, statistically
significant positive remaining on disk by month's end was a modest SWE-Gym
teacher-forcing effect (49/75 tasks changed, bootstrap CI excluding zero) —
measured under a deliberately terse baseline summary condition, not a
production-faithful one.

## Methodology and infrastructure hardening

Rigor tightened continuously through the month, largely in response to failures.
Holdout/validation-split discipline, first demonstrated by a per-head null at 4B
(07-05), was reused throughout to catch overfit configurations (a per-slot
"posslots" variant beat its global-alpha baseline on holdout but failed a
wrong-conversation contamination guard and was retired, 07-06). An
evidence-grading rule held small-scale/4-bit/local results to
hypothesis-generator status only, with claims earned exclusively at 30B-bf16 on
standard data — a rule the month's final audit vindicated. On the infrastructure
side, repeated silent job failures, a shared-lane cache-state leak, and a
cascade of pod-fleet bugs (env-var forwarding, undersized timeouts, OOM
misdiagnosis, multimodal wrapper models miscounted as text models) drove the
construction of a fail-closed pre-flight gate, a continuous pod health monitor,
and mandatory shellcheck (07-08), following an explicit trust breakdown in which
unverified "it worked" claims were reported as fact and a running pod was
terminated without instruction. That incident produced two durable rules still
in force: report only directly observed, past-tense facts, and never act on
infrastructure or spend from inferred intent, only explicit instruction. A
delegated audit concluded that failures recur specifically where guidance exists
only as prose to be recalled under pressure, and stop recurring once converted
into an automated gate — reframing the reliability work as a general principle
rather than a one-off fix.

A parallel, sustained workstream hardened the notes archive itself: a
mainline-conversation summarization pipeline (structural filtering of
app-generated compaction messages, size-bounded sharding, a manifest-driven
incremental-update design, forbidden-term/sensitive-topic filtering), a compact
filename/dating scheme backed by a shared date-cache (fixing renaming
instability that had depended on unreliable git history), and — completed 07-09
— a two-layer daily/overall meta-summary system, of which this document is a
product. A repo-local git identity change briefly misattributed all commits
before being caught and reverted; Codex attribution should use per-command
environment variables or commit trailers, never repo-level git config, going
forward.

## Terminology settled during the month

- **ValueGraft** is the umbrella term for write-time cache-state interventions,
  formalized by 07-06 as two independent axes, `alpha_K` and `alpha_V`,
  controlling fresh-vs-write-time key/value state at aligned tokens (Plain
  Summary Compaction, V-only/K-only/KV-Graft, Coupled KV-Graft); `alpha=1` is an
  endpoint of a continuous parameter, not a separate method.
- **sense / referent / stance** (07-07) is the standing probe taxonomy for
  meaning recovery, superseding the earlier coarse fact-recall framing.
- **B-causal, negative controls (shuffled/wrong-conversation grafts), the
  six-class leakage taxonomy, and robust F1 metrics** (raw E−B, %-helped,
  bootstrap CI, never mean-of-ratio) are all mandatory analysis conventions.
- The interpretability readout tool ("J-lens") is a hypothesis-generating,
  aggregate-level instrument only — suggestive corroboration, never sufficient
  alone, with an uncharacterized false-positive rate; a raw logit-lens baseline
  recovered most of its late-layer signal independently on more than one
  occasion.
- A recurring, isolated interpretability side-probe on a politically sensitive
  historical topic is deliberately kept out of science documentation and
  described only generically in the archive.

## Current state / handoff

- **No claim currently qualifies as a cornerstone.** Both original headline
  results — synthetic meaning-recovery and honesty/anti-fabrication — were shown
  by month's end to be either 4-bit-only, arm-mismatched against the paper's
  canonical value-only method, or within noise at bf16. The one confirmed bf16
  value-only positive is a modest SWE-Gym teacher-forcing effect measured under
  a handicapped ("brief") baseline summary.
- **Immediate blocking work**, per the 07-09 postmortem and pivot decision:
  re-run the SWE-Gym instrument at bf16 under a production-faithful (non-brief)
  summary with a behavioral rather than teacher-forcing metric; and resolve a
  designed-but-not-yet-launched champion-configuration validation experiment
  testing whether a per-layer/per-head tuned graft is genuinely content-specific
  (beats a placebo grafted through identical slots) or merely an amplified
  content-independent lift. That experiment's outcome determines whether the
  eventual paper is a rigorous positive result or an honest bounding/negative
  writeup with a detailed process postmortem — paper-writing itself is deferred
  until it resolves.
- **Parked, not closed:** the synthetic chain agent-task line and tau-bench
  (both hit ceiling/scope-boundary nulls, 07-07); a cross-architecture
  generalization sweep (designed, self-gen-summary-corrected, paused behind the
  same reproduction concerns); an identifier-style key-grafting question
  (statistically underpowered, needs a pre-registered run); a
  QK-norm-predicts-sign hypothesis with one unresolved counterexample.
- **Known unremediated risks:** contradictory `transformers` version pins and
  missing dependencies across pod bootstrap scripts (a shared pod-environment
  script and fail-closed dependency preflight are proposed, not built); a
  credential-storage exposure risk that doesn't propagate exclusion across
  clones/agents; a required `METHODS-PROVENANCE-REQUIREMENTS.md` documentation
  gate still blocking any paper write-up; the scheduled heartbeat automation for
  notes updates has missed its firing window at least twice, with manual
  invocation as the working fallback.
- **Standing process rules to carry forward:** name task source (synthetic vs.
  standard benchmark) in every results statement; report only directly-observed
  past-tense facts, no predictive success claims; never act on
  infrastructure/spend from inferred intent; validate against producing code the
  moment a number looks structurally surprising, rather than patching around it;
  prefer automated gates over prose-only guidance for anything that must survive
  being forgotten under pressure.
