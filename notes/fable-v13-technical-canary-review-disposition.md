# Disposition of Fable's v13 technical-canary and apparatus-stop review

Date: 2026-07-12

## Decision

Fable's central diagnosis is accepted: the unpaid work completed so far was
information-critical because independent reviews repeatedly found fail-open
defects that would have silently invalidated paid data, but the current draft
also creates a sequencing deadlock by requiring exact-runtime demonstrations
before allowing the GPU needed to produce them.

The protocol will be amended before any successor outcome or production-pool
text exists to add one separately released **technical-only canary**. It is
bounded by `$1.50` total and 3,300 provider seconds, whichever is reached first;
uses only hash-allowlisted outcome-seen `e01` and a nonsemantic technical-long
fixture; has semantic `N=0`; requests no production entropy; imports no
production-pool materializer; and contributes to no eligibility, treatment,
selector, or inferential statistic. The host is harvested and deleted after the
canary rather than held warm.

This is not an immediate paid authorization. Before its dedicated parent-rooted
release, the exact subject loader/attestation, bounded launcher/watchdog and
cleanup path, technical fixture hashes, technical-only import boundary, and
release accept/reject tests must pass locally. The canary then measures the
facts static work cannot establish: acquisition/cold-start time, exact model
load, 4.5k-token eager replay/continuation time, bundle I/O, VP search overhead,
six-arm time on e01, peak VRAM, and cleanup latency.

## Corrections to the advisory review

1. The `$1.50` is a sub-cap of the existing `$12` admission/pilot/Phase-A
   bucket. It is not charged to the protected `$8` failure, cleanup, and
   artifact-recovery reserve, and it is not charged against the merely
   calculated `$2.6287946692` slack. After a full canary spend, at most `$10.50`
   remains in that phase bucket.
2. Exact subject attestation includes the minimal relevant L0/L1/L3 and
   technical replay identities; otherwise timing could describe a broken path.
   Broader candidate sampler, ranked-content, and confirmatory warm-order gates
   may wait.
3. Bundle/store repairs currently under review remain canary-blocking because
   the technical canary measures bundle reuse and VP execution. Their second
   passes are not deferred merely because the output has semantic N=0.
4. One carrier render, fewer strata, shorter contexts, a different attention
   backend, or a smaller N is not an automatic budget fallback. Any such change
   alters the estimand, precision, or runtime and requires a prospective
   amendment plus new power/economics validation. A feasibility rejection is
   preferable to quietly weakening the target.
