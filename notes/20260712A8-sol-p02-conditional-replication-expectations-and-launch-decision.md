## Predeclared reading of P02 relative to P01

- Exact reproduction of both five-cell literal tuples is cross-host fixed-fixture behavioral concordance. It strengthens the record of generic runtime-specific disruption but still does not establish semantic transfer because correct and wrong sources produce the same wrong answer and the placebo may remain missing.
- Failure of the NF4 fresh anomaly to reproduce demotes it to host/run-specific instability. It does not become evidence against all graft effects; it removes that particular descriptive pattern from any headline.

This earlier stop is permitted by the freeze rule and changes no scientific selection.
