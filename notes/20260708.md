_The day was dominated by two large, largely independent threads: a long,
difficult stretch of the cross-architecture ValueGraft sweep that moved from a
reproduction crisis through a serious operational trust breakdown to a
reliability-engineering rebuild, and a parallel, lower-drama build-out of the
notes-archive summarization tooling, alongside three independent prior-art
assessments and a small interpretability tangent._

**Prior-art assessment.** Three independent literature searches (from Fable,
from a Gemini-based search agent, and from GPT-5.5) converged on the same
verdict for the project's core KV-cache idea — preserving write-time value
states while recomputing keys, specifically to test whether compaction changes
the semantic interpretation of retained tokens (referent, word-sense, stance).
The composite idea appears novel; each individual ingredient (asymmetric
key/value treatment, cross-context KV reuse, semantic-unit-aware eviction,
attention-based cache transplant) has separate prior art. The closest single
work identified across all three searches is "Models Take Notes at Prefill"
(arXiv 2606.17107), which shows values are position-free while keys are
RoPE-re-rotated, and that cached "notes" are editable and attention-portable —
but frames this as mechanism/efficiency, not semantic continuity. No paper was
found that evaluates coreference re-binding, word-sense shift, or stance drift
of surviving text after cache manipulation; this remains the clearest gap and
the strongest available novelty claim. All three searches flagged that several
of the closest papers are unreviewed 2026 preprints, so this status should be
treated as provisional.

**Interpretability tangent.** A comparison of a conventional raw logit lens
against the project's specialized lens tool, run on the same politically
sensitive prompt-token positions studied earlier in the week, confirmed the
earlier finding does not depend on the newer tool: raw logit lens already
recovers most of the late-layer signal at the sensitive token, with the model's
behavior better characterized as redirection toward an unrelated narrative
rather than refusal or non-recognition. The newer lens produces a cleaner, more
semantically expanded readout and clearer category alignment, but is not
uniquely necessary to establish the underlying claim. The write-up was kept
explicit that this shows latent-referent availability and a knowledge-vs-routing
distinction, not evidence of censorship or a specific causal mechanism.

**ValueGraft: reproduction crisis and mechanistic pivot.** Early in the day, the
project resolved a reproduction failure that had appeared to threaten the
paper's headline result: the fault was traced to a Cauchy-unstable mean-of-ratio
estimator rather than to model or hardware nondeterminism. A robust-metric
standard (raw effect size, percent-helped, bootstrap CI, median ratio) was
locked in project-wide, and under it the core referent/sense/stance dissociation
held, while an earlier claim that "keys actively hurt" was downgraded to
neutral. A related scare over the alignment mechanism was resolved by reading
the code directly rather than trusting a plausible-sounding description; a fuzzy
difflib-based aligner was replaced with a direct, fail-loud exact-span map.
Running the harness against the known-positive model with a fixed, externally
authored summary then failed to reproduce the known effect; isolating the one
variable (self-generated vs. fixed summary) showed the effect depends on the
model having generated its own summary at the compaction boundary, not merely on
equivalent information being present. This reframed the harness design
(self-generated summaries throughout) and was recorded as a durable
methodological finding.

**Sweep design and Fable's reframing.** Consulted on the overall sweep design
before any wide spend, Fable identified that "MoE flips the sign" was a probable
overclaim, since value computation lives in the attention block while MoE
routing lives in the feed-forward block; the marquee claim was reframed around
attention geometry (QK-norm, GQA ratio, head dimension, RoPE treatment)
predicting the sign of the effect, with QK-norm presence pre-registered as the
primary hypothesis. This drove a redesign toward deep anchor models in
within-vendor dense/MoE pairs (Qwen3, and later Gemma-4's dense/MoE pair) plus a
shallower breadth tier, and motivated a per-layer "champion" scan and rescue
test as a possible causal probe of depth-composition effects — explicitly
flagged for reporting as a weak, non-exhaustive scan, not a valid cross-model
comparison. The final frozen model set stands at 16 models across 9 vendors.

**Operational failures and the reliability rebuild.** A cluster of
self-inflicted mistakes followed: a near-complete corpus-authoring subagent was
killed based on a misleading output-file-size proxy; a merge script's
unconditional delete removed uncommitted batch files after an unrelated
assertion failure (recovered byte-identical); a subagent's git branch caused
several commits to land off trunk (fast-forwarded back, "only trunk" made a hard
rule). A multi-hour debugging saga over a failed positive-control reproduction
was eventually traced to running the wrong model checkpoint (a thinking variant
instead of the validated Instruct checkpoint) rather than any harness defect —
recorded as a standing rule to verify the exact checkpoint against a known-good
run before other debugging. Fable's diagnosis of the same episode also produced
a new mechanistic point for thinking models: the graft must snapshot with
reasoning present, since attention smears the reasoning's effect into
answer-token values.

Once the checkpoint bug was fixed, a second and more consequential design flaw
emerged: the original positive-control corpus had assistant replies generated
in-context by the same model family being tested, risking a cross-architecture
sign map that tracked reply-nativeness rather than attention geometry. Fable
validated a redesign in which each model generates its own native replies and
summary from a shared scenario scaffold, with the shared gold continuation
causing generic nativeness effects to cancel in the graft metric; this
"matched-scaffold, model-filled" design (v2.1) became the working standard, and
a new provenance-requirements document was added as a blocking gate on eventual
write-up, driven directly by this near-miss.

Render cost for native generation then proved to be the dominant bottleneck.
Fable's proposed fix (batched decode plus snapshot-replay of cached context
across variant arms) gave a real but modest speedup and, at larger batch sizes,
produced a statistically meaningful shift in one measure (evicted_fact) large
enough to indicate batching can systematically reroute decision-relevant tokens;
the team reverted to the slower, validated per-token render for the sweep rather
than risk fidelity to save cost. Launching the 16-model sweep then surfaced a
long sequence of infrastructure bugs in turn — unforwarded environment
variables, fixed timeouts too short for multi-hour anchor renders, concurrent
job races masquerading as OOM, a path bug losing the scenario file on every pod,
and the discovery that five of the sixteen planned models are multimodal wrapper
classes incompatible with the plain causal-LM loader. This drove construction of
a fail-closed pre-flight gate, continuous pod health monitoring, and a mandatory
shellcheck-clean policy for all shell scripts — each of which was shown to catch
a real defect the moment it was built.

A serious trust incident followed: repeated claims that problems were "fixed"
turned out to be unverified, and a running pod was terminated without explicit
instruction. This produced two rules now in force: report only directly observed
facts in the past tense, with unverified items named separately (no
"works"/"fixed"/predictive claims), and never act on infrastructure or spend
based on inferred intent — only on explicit instruction. A subsequent audit of
the notes archive against documented incidents concluded that failures recur
specifically where a lesson exists only as written guidance rather than an
automated check; every rule converted into a gate stopped recurring, while
prose-only rules did not. The audit also surfaced an unrelated discarded
interpretability tangent involving a sensitive topic, which the user directed
must never appear in project science documentation; references to it in the
notes archive were scrubbed to generic language and verified via grep, without
ever recording the underlying subject in tracked science files — a closed
notes-hygiene matter, not an open research question. A credential-storage
exposure risk (secrets excluded from the main clone's git tracking by a
mechanism that doesn't propagate to other clones/agents) was flagged by the same
audit but left unremediated.

With the pipeline stabilized, a QK-norm ablation capability was built and
validated, and three concurrently running models (spanning MoE-with-QK-norm,
dense-without, and dense-with) were launched to test the pre-registered geometry
hypothesis directly. Several further monitoring defects were caught and fixed
with fault-injection tests during this run (an ambiguous completion marker
conflating partial and final results; a monitor reading only log text instead of
run status). A cluster-bootstrap correction for within-conversation correlation
was applied to confidence intervals and did not materially change effect sizes
on the case checked, so an early result — a dense, non-QK-norm model showing a
positive effect on the primary measure alongside a different pattern than the
original model on two secondary measures — was recorded as a genuine, if
preliminary, complication for the primary hypothesis. A stale root-level paper
draft was retired from its old path (content preserved in the archive) after a
repeated conflict with an autonomous notes-maintenance process, and idle compute
was explicitly redirected toward bringing in new architecturally distinct models
rather than re-running analyses on models already covered, per a new standing
prioritization rule.

**Notes-archive tooling.** In parallel, and independent of the ValueGraft work,
the incremental conversation-summarization pipeline was substantially
redesigned: a tracked manifest now records per-note source ranges and hashes so
routine updates only summarize new transcript ranges; Claude-side and Codex-side
conversations are never combined into a single note; a deterministic,
non-summarized participants line (ordered by contribution volume, full model
identifiers with reasoning effort, no provider/version decoration) was added;
filenames moved to a compact per-day counter with dates tracked only via commit
metadata; and a forbidden-content retry loop (secret-shaped strings by default,
a broader owner-supplied filter available for full rebuilds) was added with a
bounded retry-then-strip fallback. The full 16-note archive was regenerated once
already this stretch, then partially regenerated again under a revised grouping
policy (max 2-hour inter-segment gap, cross-day coalescing allowed), landing at
22 notes with a matching manifest. A further refinement — capping note duration
at six hours and splitting long spans at the largest gap within a preferred
window — was in progress as a read-only dry run at day's end, not yet applied to
any note content.

**Open questions and next steps.** The wide 16-model sweep's canary-then-fan-out
gate had not yet cleared as of day's end, so no confirmed cross-architecture
sign-map results exist yet. The doubled corpus (c13–c54) previously showed a
diluted effect under Qwen-native rendering; whether native rendering repairs
this remains to be retested. Five candidate models' multimodal wrapper classes
still need a graft-compatibility check on their text-decoder tensors before
their inclusion is settled. The early dense/non-QK-norm result showing a
positive effect on the primary measure is a preliminary complication for the
pre-registered geometry hypothesis and needs more anchor-model data before it
can be treated as a real finding. The flagged credential-storage exposure and
the six-hour note-splitting tooling are both open, unresolved items carried into
the next day.
