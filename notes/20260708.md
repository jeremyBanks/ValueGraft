**Research state**

The core effect is validated on the exact `Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507`
checkpoint. Two independent harnesses reproduce positive referent recovery of
roughly +0.12, with the expected referent > sense > stance pattern;
identity-graft and alpha-zero controls pass. The effect is therefore not
explained by the earlier debugging failures, which came primarily from using the
thinking Qwen3 checkpoint rather than the known-good Instruct checkpoint.

The robust reporting standard is raw continuation logprob lift (`lp_E − lp_B`),
percentage helped, and conversation-cluster bootstrap intervals. The mean
gap-closure ratio `(E−B)/(A−B)` is deprecated because small denominators make it
unstable. Current ranges are approximately:

- Referent: raw lift +0.125 to +0.156; 71–81% helped.
- Sense: raw lift +0.047 to +0.062; 59–64% helped, suggestive but underpowered.
- Stance: approximately null overall, with 38–54% helped.

Keys are approximately neutral rather than actively harmful; value grafting
remains the positive result. The judged metric is separate and still requires an
independent audit.

The native per-model design is now preferred: each model receives the same
scenarios, planted facts, compaction structure, and shared gold continuations,
but generates its own assistant replies and summary. This removes the
foreign-reply confound and better matches the deployment estimand. Reply length,
headroom, task competence, and meaningful-eviction checks remain necessary
covariates or exclusions. The full 54-scenario corpus is eligible for this
design, but the newly rendered conversations were initially weak under foreign
replies and should not be labeled intrinsically defective until native rendering
and competence gates are evaluated.

The planned architecture study is preregistered around QK-normalization as a
possible predictor of graft sign, with dense/MoE within-vendor pairs and broader
vendor diversity. QK-norm detection now inspects loaded modules rather than
unreliable configuration flags. Mistral produced a preliminary positive-referent
but negative-sense/stance signature; its 12-conversation sample is insufficient
for broad conclusions. OLMo’s apparent failure was likely an absolute logprob
competence floor rather than alignment failure. Model comparisons remain
confounded unless interpreted jointly with controls and competence measures.

Batch decoding was about 2.3× faster but changed greedy bf16 outputs and
weakened the referent effect while shifting `evicted_fact`; per-token decoding
remains the validated method. Any fp32-argmax batching alternative requires
reproducing the native result and restoring the affected category before
adoption.

Topic-sensitivity analysis supports a restrained interpretation: internal states
retain topic/event associations while generation routes toward reform or
development language. Raw logit lens recovers much of the signal; J-lens
provides cleaner category alignment and transported-space comparisons but is not
uniquely necessary. English/Chinese similarity is stronger for matched topics
than controls, especially in J-lens space, but does not prove identical
representations or a causal mechanism. The appropriate claim is language- and
topic-dependent narrative routing, not ignorance, refusal, censorship, or panic.

**Prior art and terminology**

The prior-art searches found strong precedents for individual ingredients—KV
eviction and compression, cross-context cache reuse, learned latent summaries,
cache editing, and asymmetric positional treatment—but no mature method
combining preservation of write-time values, refreshed or relocated keys, an
in-context generated summary, and direct tests of retained-text semantic
continuity. “Models Take Notes at Prefill” is the closest mechanistic precedent,
while Kamera and related reuse work provide partial analogies. The novelty claim
should remain explicitly composite and provisional, with recent preprints and
publication status verified before strong attribution.

The durable conceptual distinction is between preserving cache utility and
preserving the interpretation of surviving text. Existing benchmarks mostly
measure efficiency, perplexity, retrieval, or aggregate task accuracy; they do
not directly test referent rebinding, sense changes, or stance drift across
compaction. Claims should therefore distinguish mechanism evidence from
evaluation novelty.

**Workflow and reliability decisions**

Positive controls are mandatory before trusting a new pipeline. Agreement on a
negative case is not validation; the pipeline must reproduce a known positive
result through the actual production path. Exact model identifiers and
configurations must be checked before debugging non-reproduction. Cheap
tokenizer and template smokes precede tiny-model tests, which precede
large-model runs. The alignment path must test representative long
self-generated summaries and fail loudly on structural mismatch; skipped grafts
or unverified fallbacks are unacceptable.

The infrastructure moved toward fail-closed operation: preflight checks validate
model IDs, loadability, deployed files, dependencies, credentials, syntax, and
repository state; a fresh verification token is required before costly fan-out;
a three-conversation probe gates expansion; monitors classify missing,
malformed, stale, dead-process, and result-error states; and completion requires
a unique terminal marker plus sample size. `transformers==4.57.1` is pinned
because a later unconstrained version broke model conversion. Community pods
were terminated after successive infrastructure and loading failures; a secure
single-model canary is the current gate, with limited concurrency only after
health and probe checks pass.

Independent multi-item generation should be sharded across diverse subagents for
both speed and output quality. Progress must be verified from real activity and
artifacts before killing or deleting work. Uncommitted inputs must not be
removed, all work remains on `trunk`, and completed units should be committed
and pushed. Slow local generation is inappropriate for filler content when
faster parallel model/API generation is available.

**Repository and archive state**

Research notes were consolidated under `notes/`, obsolete experiment directories
were removed after preserving applicable material, and archive guidance was
added. Transcript tooling now extracts mainline Claude/Codex conversations while
excluding tools, systems, notifications, and sidecars; preserves model/provider
provenance; creates dated shards; and supports combining and splitting
summaries. Archive normalization is UTC-sortable, kebab-cased, idempotent,
handles Markdown-like `.txt` files, and preserves synthetic Git chronology.

The archive was regenerated and renamed with manifest-aware path updates,
individual dated commits, compact participant slugs, and forbidden-output
scanning. Validation passed for naming, manifests, compilation, and focused
tests; final regeneration changes were committed, though the latest note does
not establish that every later commit was pushed. Incremental updates should use
source-message ranges and hashes rather than filenames, with overlap marked
context-only. The six-hour maximum conversation policy is implemented in
planning and dry-run logic, but no six-hour regeneration has yet been
authorized.

Durable scientific and operational state belongs in the designated findings,
incidents, decisions, master-plan, state, provenance, reliability, and
agent-guidance files. The unrelated dirty Qwen result JSON remains intentionally
untouched.
