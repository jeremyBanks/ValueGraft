_The ValueGraft effect was revalidated on the exact Qwen3 Instruct-2507
checkpoint, while cross-architecture expansion was halted by infrastructure and
methodology failures. The day also consolidated archive tooling, clarified the
native per-model experimental design, and found that prior art supports adjacent
mechanisms but not the full semantic-continuity claim._

**Participants/contributors:** User; `claude-opus-4-8`; `claude-sonnet-5`;
`gpt-5.5-xhigh`; `gpt-5.5-medium`; `gpt-5.5-low`; and `gpt-5.5` (low effort).

**Research status.** Two independent harnesses reproduced the original
referent-grafting effect on Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507: approximately +0.12
recovery, with the expected referent > sense > stance pattern; identity and
alpha-zero checks passed. Earlier alignment failures were traced primarily to
testing the wrong thinking checkpoint, not to a failed graft mechanism. The
original c01–c12 corpus remains trusted evidence.

The robust reporting standard is raw logprob lift (`E−B`), percentage helped,
and conversation-cluster bootstrap intervals. Mean gap-closure ratios are
unstable under small denominators and are deprecated as headline measures.
Current evidence remains positive for referent, suggestive and underpowered for
sense, and null for stance; keys are approximately neutral rather than harmful.

The native per-model design is now preferred: hold scenarios, plants, prompts,
compaction structure, and gold continuations fixed, while every tested model
generates its own replies and summary. This removes foreign-reply provenance as
a major confound and better matches the deployment estimand. Reply length,
headroom, task competence, and meaningful-eviction criteria must still be
measured and used for gating or normalization. The 54-scenario corpus is
eligible, but the newly rendered conversations are not yet scientifically
validated; their weak recovery may reflect rendering or native-generation
properties rather than intrinsically bad scenarios.

A planned cross-architecture sweep produced no usable results after sequential
failures involving missing scaffolds, incompatible or multimodal checkpoints,
launcher races, monitoring gaps, pod instability, and an unconstrained
`transformers` upgrade. The project therefore adopted a secure-tier, fail-closed
single-model canary with a three-conversation probe before any fan-out.
Per-token decoding remains the validated method: faster batching changed outputs
and weakened the referent result, so it is not acceptable without a new
positive-control validation. The preregistered QK-norm hypothesis is frozen, and
detection now inspects loaded modules rather than unreliable configuration
flags.

Prior-art searches converged on a cautious novelty claim. Existing work covers
KV eviction, compression, cross-context reuse, cache editing, attention-based
transplantation, and learned compact states; recent work especially supports the
idea that write-time values can carry semantic commitments while keys require
positional treatment. No located work directly combines preservation of retained
write-time values, key refresh or relocation across a text-compaction boundary,
and explicit evaluation of referent, sense, or stance stability. This remains a
provisional literature assessment because several cited works are recent
preprints and the source reviews were excerpted.

**Durable methodological rules.**

- Validate every new pipeline through a known positive control; agreement on a
  negative case is insufficient.
- Verify the exact checkpoint and configuration before debugging
  non-reproduction.
- Mine existing data before spending compute, and read actual numerical outputs
  rather than trusting status labels.
- Push checks to the cheapest layer: tokenizer/data smoke, tiny-model full path,
  then the expensive positive control.
- Shard independent generation from the start for both speed and author
  diversity; verify real progress before killing or deleting work.
- Work only on trunk, preserve uncommitted inputs, and commit/push each
  completed unit.
- Consult Fable early when debugging stalls, a subagent loops, or results remain
  confusing.

**Repository and archive state.** Research notes were consolidated under
`notes/`, obsolete experiment folders were removed after preserving applicable
material, and transcript tooling now supports reproducible mainline extraction,
filtering, sharding, combining, splitting, provenance retention,
forbidden-output handling, and manifest-aware incremental updates. The archive
was regenerated and normalized with chronological synthetic Git dates, validated
manifests, and passing focused tests. A six-hour maximum conversation-note
policy is implemented with a preferred split window four to five hours after
note start, but its dry run and any regeneration remain pending. The unrelated
dirty Qwen result JSON is intentionally untouched.
