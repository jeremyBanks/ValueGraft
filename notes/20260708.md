_This UTC day was dominated by the ValueGraft cross-architecture experiment
moving through a difficult reproduction-and-infrastructure crisis to a
validated, redesigned wide sweep, while a second, largely independent thread
built and repeatedly refined an incremental summarization pipeline for the notes
archive itself; three literature searches into prior art for the project's core
KV-cache mechanism, and a small interpretability side-check, were also
completed._

**Reproduction crisis and the estimator fix.** A live re-run of the original F1
code failed to reproduce saved numbers. An initial suspicion of
Mixture-of-Experts routing nondeterminism was wrong — two same-hardware runs
were byte-identical, proving the apparatus deterministic — and the real causes
were a `transformers` version drift between runs and a Cauchy-unstable
mean-of-ratio estimator, (E−B)/(A−B), that blows up near zero denominators.
Consulting Fable resolved this from data already on disk, at no GPU cost. Robust
metrics — raw E−B, percent-helped, median-ratio, bootstrap confidence intervals
— were locked in as the project standard, retiring the mean-ratio estimator.
Under the robust metric the primary effect held (referent significant, sense
positive but underpowered, stance null, as originally claimed), and a related
overstated "keys actively hurt" claim from earlier key/value ablation work was
downgraded to "keys neutral," leaving "value is the operative axis" a closed,
settled question. A separate alarm over the alignment mechanism's description
("token matching") was resolved by reading the code directly: alignment is a
positional 1:1 span map within regions, not fuzzy matching against conversation
text.

**The positive-control failure and the self-generation finding.** Running the
harness on the known-positive model with an externally supplied summary produced
a null result; substituting the model's own self-generated summary reproduced
the known positive almost exactly. This established that the value-graft effect
depends on the model having generated its own summary at the compaction boundary
— not merely on semantically equivalent content being present — and became a
durable project rule: self-generated summaries throughout, with a toggle added
specifically so the harness can never silently regress to the fixed-summary
design.

**Fable's redesign around attention geometry.** Because value vectors are
computed in the attention block while MoE routing lives in the feed-forward
block, Fable flagged "MoE flips the sign" as a probable reviewer-fatal overclaim
and reframed the sweep's central question to whether attention-geometry
properties (KV-head count, head dimension, GQA ratio, QK-norm, RoPE treatment)
predict the sign of the effect. This added a same-generation dense Qwen3 model
as a de-confound, shifted the design toward deep anchor models (placebo grafts,
identity checks, alpha dose-response, full raw-trace and
attention-hyperparameter capture) plus a shallower breadth tier, and introduced
a per-layer "champion scan" with an associated "rescue test" (grafting only
positive-scanning depth regions on an architecture that grafts negative overall,
to see if the net effect flips sign) — explicitly documented as a tentative,
non-exhaustive scan, not a valid cross-model comparison, so it cannot be
inflated in the eventual paper.

**Build-out and model set.** Corpus authoring was switched from a slow local MLX
model to a fast parallel mix of subagents (Sonnet, Opus, Fable, Codex), cutting
generation time from roughly 90 minutes to minutes; the corpus grew from 12 to
27 conversations (adding a "strong_prior" probe category) and was later doubled
toward 54 for additional statistical power, on the reasoning that conversations,
not probes, are the true unit of power under conversation-level cluster
bootstrap resampling. The harness was hardened with placebo grafts, an
identity/integrity check, alpha dose-response, full raw-trace capture, and
per-model attention-hyperparameter logging to support a later sign-regression.
The final wide-sweep model list — verified against Hugging Face before being
trusted, after an earlier hallucinated repo ID was caught — settled at 16 models
across 9 vendors, anchored by two independent within-vendor dense/MoE pairs
(Qwen3, and Gemma 3 vs. the newly available Gemma 4). Estimated cost for the
full sweep with the doubled corpus was roughly $30–50 against a roughly $75–80
balance.

**The checkpoint saga.** A cascade of self-inflicted operational errors
compounded the debugging effort: a near-complete authoring subagent was killed
based on a misleading output-file-size proxy (later corrected to verifying real
progress before any destructive action); an `rm` after a failed merge deleted
uncommitted batch files (recovered byte-identical by resuming the same
subagents); and a subagent-created branch caused mainline commits to land off
trunk (fast-forwarded back, establishing a hard trunk-only git policy). The
eventual root cause of a multi-hour non-reproduction was that debugging had been
run against the thinking-variant checkpoint (Qwen3-30B-A3B) rather than the
non-thinking Instruct-2507 checkpoint the known-good result was measured on — a
mismatch that explained the `<think>`-block tokenization divergence, the
alignment crash, and every downstream symptom. This produced two standing rules,
now written into AGENTS.md rather than private memory (so they're visible to
subagents and collaborators): verify the exact model checkpoint against any
known-good run before other debugging, and consult Fable for a strategic read as
soon as a fix hasn't converged in one or two attempts, rather than after hours
of narrow debugging.

**Nativeness confound and the per-model-native redesign.** Fable identified that
the original corpus's assistant replies were generated by Qwen in-context,
meaning the graft effect could be an artifact of reply-nativeness rather than
attention geometry — the same failure mode that had made the doubled corpus
(with foreign-model replies) underperform. The resolution, validated by Fable as
the "matched-scaffold, model-filled" design (v2.1), has each model generate its
own native replies and summary from a shared scenario scaffold while the probe's
gold continuation stays fixed; because the graft metric is a difference over the
same shared gold continuation, generic nativeness effects cancel by
construction, and residual effects (reply competence, summary headroom) become
measured covariates and gates rather than confounds. Primary inferential weight
rests on the two within-vendor dense/MoE pairs, where nativeness is controlled
by construction; the full 16-model regression is confirmatory of one
pre-registered geometry direction. This episode led to a new blocking
documentation requirement (`METHODS-PROVENANCE-REQUIREMENTS.md`) mandating
thorough disclosure of data provenance — who or what generated each piece of
conversation content, exact checkpoints, gates — since the omitted nativeness
detail had turned out to be load-bearing for validity.

**Render cost, batching, and reliability engineering.** Native rendering proved
far slower than estimated (roughly 10 minutes per conversation, driven by
genuine autoregressive generation cost), raising real feasibility concerns for
the full sweep. A batched-decode plus KV-cache snapshot-replay optimization
(Fable's proposal, ~10x candidate speedup) hit a CUDA OOM, was fixed with a
batch-size cap, but ultimately showed a weakened, CI-crossing-zero effect and a
large sign flip on a secondary measure — judged too risky to fidelity for a
~2.3x wall-clock gain, so the validated per-token render was kept for the actual
sweep. Launching the wide sweep then surfaced a long sequence of infrastructure
bugs (missing environment-variable forwarding, an undersized fixed timeout,
concurrent job races masquerading as OOMs, a broken rsync path, five of sixteen
planned models turning out to be multimodal-wrapped rather than plain causal
LMs, log-based state proving unreliable). This drove construction of a
reliability layer — a fail-closed pre-flight gate, a pod health/alerting
monitor, and mandatory shellcheck linting (which caught a real bug in the health
monitor itself and a macOS bash-3.2 incompatibility) — plus a RunPod tier policy
(community tier by default, capped secure-tier fallback, always canary on
secure). A canary-discipline lapse (fanning out to 11 models before validating
one end-to-end) surfaced an uncapped `transformers` version pin auto-upgrading
to an incompatible release, fixed with a fail-closed version check.

**Trust breakdown and reporting discipline.** After several failures had only
surfaced through manual inspection rather than monitoring alerts, and after a
pod was terminated without explicit instruction, two standing rules were adopted
and written into AGENTS.md: report only directly observed facts in the past
tense, separately naming what remains unverified, with no predictive
"fixed"/"works" claims; and never act on the user's infrastructure or spend
based on inferred intent. A delegated audit of the notes archive found that
recurring failure themes correlate specifically with guidance that exists only
as prose reminders — every theme converted into an automated gate stopped
recurring. The audit also surfaced an unrelated interpretability tangent that
was scrubbed from the archive into generic language at the user's direction and
never recorded in any tracked science file.

**Resumption.** With the canary validated, an initial small-sample replication
check came back inconclusive (CI spanning zero), so a fuller confirmatory run
was required before broader spend. Three concurrent cross-vendor models were
then launched to test the project's primary pre-registered hypothesis — that
QK-norm (a query/key attention-stabilizing normalization) predicts the sign of
the graft effect — alongside a controlled ablation that disables QK-norm within
one model, holding everything else fixed, to isolate the mechanism from other
architectural differences. Further monitoring defects were found and fixed (a
dual completion-marker bug conflating partial and final results; a monitor
reading log text instead of run status, misclassifying a real failure as normal
progress), each verified via fault injection. A statistical correction —
resampling by conversation rather than by individual data point, since points
within a conversation are correlated — did not meaningfully change effect sizes
in the case checked, and an early cross-architecture result (a dense,
non-QK-norm model with a positive effect on the primary measure but a different
pattern on two secondary measures) stands as a preliminary complication for the
QK-norm hypothesis. At day's end, the primary confirmation run was partway
through, with a standing rule that idle pod capacity should always be redirected
toward new architecturally distinct models rather than extra analysis on models
already covered.

**Prior-art searches.** Three independent literature searches (via Claude/Fable,
Gemini, and GPT-5.5) converged on the same conclusion for the project's core
mechanism — preserving write-time value states while recomputing keys,
specifically to test semantic reinterpretation of retained tokens across a
compaction boundary: the exact composite claim appears novel, but each
mechanical ingredient (key/value asymmetric treatment, cache editability and
splicing, attention-based state transplant, in-context summary-state retention)
has scattered prior art. The closest single works identified are "Models Take
Notes at Prefill" (Bojie Li, Pine AI) and a position/conditioning-separation
result (Kamera); the clearest gap across all existing KV-cache eviction,
compression, and reuse literature is evaluation — no benchmark measures
referent, word-sense, or stance reinterpretation of surviving text after cache
manipulation, only efficiency, perplexity, retrieval, or aggregate QA accuracy.

**Interpretability tangent.** A raw-logit-lens comparison against the project's
specialized lens tool, on a topic-sensitivity probe, confirmed that raw logit
lens already recovers much of the same late-layer signal (redirection rather
than refusal or lack of recognition), meaning the newer lens is not uniquely
necessary — it yields a cleaner, more semantically expanded readout, but the
underlying finding is accessible through older techniques. Documentation was
updated to keep this comparative, non-causal framing explicit.

**Notes-pipeline build.** A separate, sustained effort built an incremental
conversation-note summarization pipeline for the notes archive itself, replacing
full-archive reprocessing. Key design points now standing: a manifest tracks
per-source-stream message ranges and content hashes so updates only summarize
new ranges; Claude and Codex conversations are never combined into one note;
every note opens with an italicized summary paragraph (not a title) and uses
prose with bullets reserved for dense enumerations; a deterministic
(non-summarized) **Participants:** line lists contributors by descending text
volume with full model identifier plus reasoning effort, omitting "User" when
absent; filenames use a compact date plus rolling two-character per-day counter,
with the canonical date carried in git commit metadata rather than the filename;
and content filtering defaults to secret-shaped strings only, with a broader
sensitive-topic filter available only as a manual, owner-supplied argument for
full rebuilds. A segment-grouping policy (max two-hour inter-segment gap,
cross-day coalescing allowed) was adopted after review of alternatives,
producing a 22-note archive; the normalizer was made manifest-aware so renames
auto-update manifest paths. A further refinement — capping note duration at six
hours with message-level (not just segment-level) splitting at the largest gap
within a four-to-five-hour window — was in progress and unregenerated at day's
end, pending a dry-run-only evaluation and a test-fixture fix.

**Outstanding risks and next actions.** The primary confirmation run and the
QK-norm ablation results were still pending; the preliminary cross-architecture
complication (a non-QK-norm model showing a positive primary-measure effect)
needs resolution once more models report. The credential-storage exposure gap
flagged during the notes audit remains unremediated. The six-hour note-duration
split logic needs its test fixture corrected and a dry run completed before any
further note regeneration. More broadly, the day's dominant lesson — that
written-only guidance recurs as failure while automated gates do not — is now
driving reliability work, and any further scaling or design decisions on the
sweep are expected to route through Fable before being banked, per the process
rules established this day.
