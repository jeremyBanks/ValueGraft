# Fable independent methods review — v13 powered-successor preregistration freeze-readiness

**Date:** 2026-07-12
**Reviewer:** fresh Fable instance, no prior involvement in v13 authoring.
**Review boundary:** freeze-readiness of `COHERENT-STATE-POWERED-SUCCESSOR-V13-PREREGISTRATION.md` only, read against `notes/powered-successor-design-review-disposition.md`. Settled project history and later exploration details are not relitigated.
**Anchors supplied with the charge:** independent semantic N is one outcome-seen e01; no population CI exists; all old exact-bf16 placebos were unavailable; the protocol seeks a recipe-scoped simultaneous UCB in absolute correct-target nats with real carrier renders; provider balance ≈ $57.
**Standing of this note:** this is the Section 18 condition-1 review. Advice, not authority. No result is assumed.

## Verdict

**Not freeze-ready as written.** The statistical core — unit definition, treatment-blind selection architecture, stratified estimator with alpha spending, honest conclusion labels, and control logic — is sound and correctly scoped. Freeze is blocked by four items, all fixable without changing the design's substance: (B1) Phase-A screening cost is unbudgeted and un-gated and plausibly rivals the entire treatment core on a $57 balance; (B2) the primary estimand's probe/scoring location is undefined in the binding text; (B3) the VP placebo acceptance window has a predictable failure mode, no pre-registered fallback, and its feasibility demonstration is not a freeze condition despite the anchor history that every prior placebo died at exactly this kind of gate; (B4) the Section 1 claim wording ("state written while a model **produces** a handoff") does not match the W-origin-render forced-encoding half of the primary cells.

## 1. What is sound (affirmed)

1. **Independent-unit honesty (§3.2).** One paired fixture is N; renders, arms, loci, schedules, layers, executions, hosts are nested. Correct, and consistently enforced through the estimator, the bootstrap clustering, and the reporting language.
2. **Treatment-blind selection architecture (§§3.2, 7, 9, 15).** Phase A's exposure list (content evidence, carriers, A_C/A_W/FF, oracle, damage, cost) excludes every treatment cell; candidate order is frozen and seed-derived; stratum extension under shortfall stays machine-blocked; the release manifest commits eligibility and selection before any outcome; VP selection may inspect only geometry and C/W/F row values. Selecting on FF and the ≥5-nat damage gate conditions the population, but the estimand is explicitly `R | eligible`, scoring is deterministic (temperature 0), and the floor-selection interaction with recovery is a named simulation stress (§12.3). This is clean.
3. **Text-leakage differencing (§§5, 6, 8).** All arms share identical visible tokens within a render, so stylistic or steganographic leakage in an accepted carrier moves FF as well and cannot masquerade as state recovery; the primary contrasts difference it out, and the damage gate bounds gross cases. This is a strong property and worth stating explicitly in the paper later.
4. **Position-preserving compaction and K handling (§§6, 7).** Keeping evicted-history position gaps makes post-RoPE K transplant well-defined; VP never permutes K. Consistent with the disposition's RoPE correction.
5. **Sequential machinery (§10).** Alpha spend 0.005/0.010/0.035 split over two cells sums to exactly 0.05 over six look/cell events; the union bound gives simultaneous coverage over all looks jointly, so the selected look's bound is valid regardless of the data-dependent stop; cell correlation is genuinely harmless to it. The stratified mean with Welch–Satterthwaite df matches the equal-weight mixture estimand. Fail-closed rules for missing/nonfinite/unbalanced/duplicate inputs are right. The document honestly refuses to call the t result distribution-free and mandates dual implementation, 200k simulated trials with correlation/skew/heavy-tail/rare-responder/contamination stress, golden edge cases, and a machine alpha-sum check (§12). This is well above the field's usual standard.
6. **Apparatus-sensitivity guarantee for a null (§13.7).** The signed bidirectional ULP path control is exactly what a bound-style conclusion needs to rebut "the splice was inert/ignored."
7. **Labels and degradation (§17).** BOUND_RESOLVED vs CONTROL_LIMITED vs POSITIVE_LEAD vs INCONCLUSIVE_AT_CAP vs INVALID_TECHNICAL vs PORTABILITY_UNRESOLVED force honest reporting; a null at R2 is never phrased as "no hidden cache information"; per-cell numbers are always reported.
8. **Legacy quarantine (§3.3)** and **reserve-only surplus menu (§17)** — e02–e06 never pooled; discovery/confirmation split on positions 7/8 committed blind; nothing in the surplus can touch the primary selector or UCB.
9. **Render-origin balance (§5).** One C-origin and one W-origin render per fixture makes treatment and wrong-history control symmetric in on/off-policy authorship, with origin interaction co-reported.
10. **Implied power is plausible.** At N=48, look-3 per-cell γ=0.0175 with df≈40 gives t≈2.2, so resolution at a true mean near zero needs fixture SD ≲0.79 nat (SE ≤ ~0.11). Against the observed 0.06–0.13-nat schedule-sensitivity scale that is a comfortable margin — but it must be confirmed by the §12.4 power grids before freeze, and look 1 (n_h=3) should be expected to resolve essentially never when variance concentrates in one stratum (W-S df→2, t→~20); that is an acceptable consequence of the chosen spend, not a defect.

## 2. Blocking findings

### B1. Phase-A screening is unfunded and un-gated; it plausibly costs as much as the treatment core (§§9, 16)

No primary treatment begins until **eight eligible fixtures per stratum (64 candidates minimum)** clear content review and Phase A (§9). Phase A per candidate requires building the C and W source foundations under the role-native q=1 schedule — one model call per historical assistant token, because the generated-to-forced bit-exactness requirement (§5) forbids substituting cheap chunked prefill — plus up to six render attempts, A_C/A_W/FF scoring, and the nonfocal oracle. Those foundations are the dominant cost of a full treatment case; a Phase-A visit is plausibly 50–80% of a case. The audited $28.47–33.68 envelope is described as the six-arm/two-render treatment core; the §16 buckets ($1.50 pilots, $18 through N=24, $16.70 continuation, $4.50 audit, $12 reserve = $52.70 of $57.13) nowhere name screening. Even at 100% eligibility yield, screening 64 candidates is roughly $20–30 of unbudgeted spend; the yield will not be 100% — the ≥5-nat damage gate, the dual greedy oracle gates (A_C and A_W must both begin with exact targets with correct margin signs), the nonfocal oracle under fresh compaction, and the render acceptance/review gates are each untested on this recipe. Because all screening spend lands **before** any treatment value exists, the worst case is spending most of the balance and obtaining no population statement at all — precisely the failure the owner handoff exists to prevent. My per-candidate estimates are inferences from the protocol's own timing gates, not measurements; that is the point — nobody has the measurement, and the freeze conditions do not require one.

**Required before freeze:**
1. Add an explicit screening bucket and a measured per-candidate Phase-A cost from the pilot stage.
2. Add a preregistered yield/cost gate: after a first balanced screening batch (e.g., two candidates per stratum), project total screening cost from the observed acceptance rate; if projected screening + treatment core + host audit + locked reserve exceeds the then-current balance, halt and redesign rather than continue burning.
3. Decide and specify the foundation economics. The clean option: make persistence of the selected R2 K/V row bundles (≤256 rows × 48 layers — tens of MB) **mandatory** at Phase A, and let treatment splice from durable bundles into cheaply rebuilt fresh boundaries instead of re-running the heavy replays; §14's retained-state key (design, release, runtime fingerprint, case, render, schedule, history) already anticipates exactly this reuse. Note the constraint: cross-session reuse requires runtime-fingerprint equality, so a host change forces rebuild — budget whichever branch is chosen. Persisting raw source rows does not violate §9 (rows are treatment inputs, not probe outcomes), but say so explicitly.
4. Add a preregistered pre-treatment terminal label (e.g., `RECIPE_INFEASIBLE_PRETREATMENT`) so a screening-stage abort has a name in the record; §17's labels all presuppose a scheduled look occurred.

### B2. The probe and target-scoring position are undefined in the binding text (§§3.1, 6, 8)

§6 enumerates the visible context as items 1–5 ending with the byte-identical retained tail; §3.1 says the tail "end[s] with an assistant turn"; §8 and §9 then score L_C/L_W and require greedy answers to "begin with the complete exact C target." Nowhere does the preregistration state **where** the focal probe lives or at what position target tokens are scored. If the tail's final assistant turn is content-complete, there is no elicitation point; if it is header-only (the probe being the tail's final user turn), the phrase "ending with an assistant turn" is misleading and the tail-neutrality review needs to know the question is inside the tail. The templates and schema that would pin this do not exist yet (§18.5 pending), so today the preregistration is the only binding statement of the primary estimand — and it is silent on the estimand's measurement location. **Fix (one paragraph):** state the probe turn's literal placement and text policy, that it is byte-identical across C/W/F, that L_C/L_W are computed by teacher-forcing the target/countertarget tokens at the declared answer position, and that greedy generation at the same position is recorded separately.

### B3. VP availability has a predictable failure mode, no pre-registered fallback, and no pre-freeze demonstration (§§7, 13.8, 18)

The acceptance window requires `||VP−F||/||C−F||` in [0.80, 1.25] aggregate **and** per-layer median. Two structural facts push against it: (a) at decoder layer 0, V rows depend only on token embeddings (V carries no RoPE and layer-0 hidden states are pre-attention), so C−F is exactly zero there while the permutation displacement is full inter-token scale — layer 0 contributes only to the numerator; early layers behave similarly in attenuated form; (b) within-class inter-token V-row differences are generically larger than same-token cross-history deltas, so the full fixed-point-free derangement — the only permutation the rule allows, since no row may stay fixed in a class of size >1 — may systematically overshoot 1.25 even in mid layers. Whether depth-weighted norms rescue the aggregate is empirical; the window was chosen without geometry evidence, and the anchor history is that **every** previous exact-bf16 placebo died at its gate. As written, §13.8's availability demonstration happens after freeze, so the protocol can be frozen into a state where the strongest conclusion label (BOUND_RESOLVED requires ≥90% placebo completeness) is unreachable by construction, discovered only after paid spend. The CONTROL_LIMITED degradation is honest but should be a contingency, not the likely path.

**Required before freeze:** (1) move the exact-subject realistic-geometry VP availability demonstration into §18 as a freeze condition — it needs only pilot-class spend from the $1.50 bucket; (2) pre-register **now**, additively and before any pilot geometry is seen, a fallback family that remains state-only and outcome-blind — e.g., computing the norm-ratio over the layer subset where `||C_ℓ−F_ℓ||_F` exceeds a frozen floor, and/or permitting partial-cycle derangements over a frozen-rule-chosen row subset to make displacement tunable while every moved row remains a real bf16 row; (3) retain CONTROL_LIMITED as the final backstop. Deciding the fallback after observing pilot geometry is defensible (geometry is not a treatment outcome) but strictly weaker than committing it now.

### B4. Claim wording does not match the render-origin design (§§1, 5)

§1 claims the state "written while a model **produces** a compact handoff under a full conversation." For the W-origin render, the CC/FC donor state is produced by **force-encoding** a W-authored text under C — the same mechanistic object (causal K/V of the handoff tokens under the full history; state depends only on history, token IDs, positions), but not production state, and half of every primary cell mean is of this kind. If on-policy authorship matters, the averaging dilutes the very effect being bounded, and a skeptic can call the stated claim mislabeled. **Fix (wording only):** define the estimand as state written while the subject **encodes** the handoff under the full history, with production being the origin-history special case proven bit-identical by the generated-to-forced gate; note the deliberate 50/50 origin balance in §1's claim boundary; and require the per-origin cell means (already implied by §8's render-origin interaction) as a mandatory labeled table in the report.

## 3. Improvements (non-blocking)

1. **Nonfocal evidence placement (§3.1).** Phase-A gate 3 requires the nonfocal oracle to pass **under fresh compaction**, which is only possible if the nonfocal evidence is visible post-compaction (retained tail or system). §3.1 never says so. State it as an authoring constraint now, or the generator can produce 64 candidates that all fail gate 3 — this compounds B1's yield risk.
2. **Spelled-out numbers (§5).** Render acceptance bans digits and declared forbidden phrases; require template forbidden-phrase lists to include number-word forms of numeric targets/changed values ("fourteen"), or the digit filter has an obvious hole.
3. **POSITIVE_LEAD criterion (§17).** "Compelling assumption-robust evidence" is judgment language in an otherwise mechanical document. Give it a mechanical floor (e.g., stratified-bootstrap one-sided 95% lower bound > 0 in a primary cell **and** positive matched specificity mean), keeping the no-confirmatory-claim sentence.
4. **Post-treatment fixture voiding (§10).** "Unbalanced looks fail closed" plus "no replacement after outcomes" means one scientifically voided fixture (not merely a resumable technical failure) could strand the campaign with no computable look. Preregister the degraded path now: report at the last balanced look, or drop the affected stratum with an explicit label.
5. **Flip-endpoint alpha ledger (§11).** The tail endpoint H explicitly has its own marginal ledger; the flip endpoint B says only "the scheduled-look alpha." State explicitly that B's ledger is its own labeled marginal ledger and never draws from the primary 0.05 (which §12.7 verifies is fully spent by the six primary events).
6. **Per-cell partial resolution at N=48 (§17).** Coverage is simultaneous over all six events, so if U_V ≤ 0.25 but U_R > 0.25 at cap, a value-only-cell bound is statistically licensed; the current labels only permit INCONCLUSIVE_AT_CAP. Name the partial outcome so it doesn't have to be argued post hoc.
7. **Idle cost through release review (§16).** Keeping the core host warm through independent release review prices review wall-clock at ~$1.42/h against no named bucket. Cap it (e.g., terminate if review exceeds a frozen hour bound; the harvest/restart criterion already exists).
8. **VP event classes (§7).** "Frozen event classes" are defined only in sealed code. Name them in the preregistration text (carrier content / canonical close / ack user / ack header, or whatever the truth is) for reviewability.
9. **Sim coverage of look-1 df (§12).** Add a named golden/stress case: n_h=3 with variance concentrated in one stratum (W-S df ≈ 2, t ≈ 20) to confirm the implementation, not just the theory, behaves at the first look.
10. **Words vs tokens (§5).** The prompt requests 40–60 words while acceptance requires 40–80 tokens; add one clause confirming the mismatch is deliberate so it isn't "fixed" later into an inconsistency.

## 4. Charge questions, answered directly

- **Sampling-frame validity:** Valid as declared. The population is the equal-weight mixture of eight literal template strata conditional on eligibility (`R | eligible`), inference targets exactly that, acceptance rates are reported, and the document explicitly refuses generalization to natural conversations or to templates as samples. One template per stratum means stratum ≈ template; the claim boundary owns this. No frame/estimand mismatch found.
- **Treatment-blind selection:** Sound (see affirmation 2). The only gaps are explicitness gaps: say that persisting Phase-A source rows is permitted (B1 fix 3) and keep the §9 exposure list as the single source of truth.
- **Render/origin logic:** Internally correct and symmetric; identical visible tokens within render difference out text leakage; generated-to-forced bit-exactness is required only on origin history, which is the only place it is possible. The one defect is the §1 wording mismatch (B4).
- **Sequential alpha and stratified df:** Correct construction (affirmation 5). Union bound over six events at total 0.05 gives valid simultaneous coverage for the data-dependent stop; W-S df with zero-variance handling and fail-closed edges is right; normality dependence is honestly stated and stress-tested. No error found in §10's formulas.
- **Control feasibility:** The design answers the placebo-unavailability anchor in principle (permuted real bf16 rows are dtype-valid by construction), but the acceptance window is at genuine risk (B3), and identity/specificity controls are otherwise well-founded. The ULP path control covers apparatus sensitivity independently of VP.
- **Conclusion labels:** Coherent, honest, and appropriately degrading; add the pre-treatment infeasibility label (B1 fix 4), a mechanical POSITIVE_LEAD floor, and the per-cell partial-resolution label (improvements 3, 6).
- **Implementation feasibility:** The warm-loop, checkpoint, resume, warm-order, corruption, and release-verifier requirements are demanding but coherent and testable; the binding risk is not code, it is money — B1. Secondary: dual-implementation of the selector plus 200k-trial simulation is local-only and cheap; the review pipeline (≥128 blind singleton reviews plus paired and cross-template reviews with third-family adjudication) is time-costly but budget-light.
- **Does it answer the owner handoff?** Yes, conditionally. The handoff asks for a statistically robust conclusion from the authorized remaining budget, closing the gap from one outcome-seen fixture and no population CI. A recipe-scoped simultaneous 95% UCB at N up to 48, with honest scope, legacy sensitivity, and a mechanism-informed reserve menu, is the right achievable deliverable — **provided** the screening economics (B1) are made real and the strongest label remains reachable (B3). As drafted, the protocol could exhaust the balance before its first treatment outcome, which would answer the handoff with nothing.

## 5. What this review did not verify

The simulation artifact, golden tests, templates, candidate files, review manifests, selector code, and release verifier do not exist yet; §18 conditions 3–7 remain to be earned and nothing here pre-approves them. My screening-cost figures are order-of-magnitude inferences from the protocol's own timing gates, not measurements. The layer-0 V context-independence argument is architectural fact; whether the VP window actually fails at depth-weighted aggregate is an empirical question the pilot must answer.
