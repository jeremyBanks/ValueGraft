## Accepted recommendations

1. The independent unit is one paired conversation fixture. Renders, arms,
   schedules, loci, layers, executions, and hosts are nested measurements.
2. `e01` is outcome-seen discovery/technical material only. `e02`--`e06` are
   valid fixed cases but not draws from the new recipe; all five form a frozen
   legacy sensitivity set.
3. The primary claim is scoped to a frozen engineered recipe, never natural
   conversations generally.
4. Treatment-blind content, oracle, target-neutrality, and damage gates define
   the conditional recipe population. Gate prevalence is reported.
5. Two genuine subject-generated carrier realizations are required per fixture;
   they are averaged within fixture and never increase N.
6. Exact fresh self-replacement is a bit-exact technical identity, not a
   tolerance-based placebo.
7. Whole-bf16 V-row permutation is the feasible nonsemantic disruption
   placebo. Post-RoPE K-row shuffling is rejected as positionally confounded.
8. Wrong-history state at identical logical positions is the primary semantic
   specificity control.
9. The six executed core states per render are `FF`, full-KV correct/wrong,
   value-only correct/wrong, and V-permutation placebo. The old 34-row grid is
   unnecessary for the primary decision.
10. One model load/attestation serves a bounded warm case loop with synchronous
    immutable checkpoints, one live case at a time, and explicit eviction.
11. A second host is a fail-closed implementation/portability audit, never more
    semantic N.
12. The old v12 runners and seals cannot authorize the successor. Low-level
    replay/cache/scoring primitives may be reused after exact production-path
    tests; orchestration, schema, selector, controls, analysis, and release are
    new.
13. One exact e01 short pilot and one long-geometry pilot must measure runtime
    before unseen primary treatment. A projected N=48 core above `$35` fails the
    scaling gate.

## Corrections to Fable's proposed design

### Primary scale

Fable proposed mean fixture-level recovery ratios. Those ratios are unstable,
overweight low-damage fixtures, and change meaning under the damage gate. The
successor instead makes **absolute correct-target mean-log-probability recovery
in nats** primary. Raw margin movement, correct/countertarget components,
damage, and ratio-of-means are co-reported. Any relative interval uses Fieller
inversion and returns unbounded when the denominator is not established.

### Sampling frame

Purpose-authored fixtures do not become iid natural conversations by assertion.
The successor defines eight fixed template/rule strata and random parameter
draws within those strata. Inference is to that literal conditional recipe.
Each sequential look is balanced across strata, and the primary standard error
uses the stratified estimator with Welch--Satterthwaite degrees of freedom.
The named finite panel is also reported without population rhetoric.

### Sample supply and looks

Fable's e02--e26 arithmetic supplied only 25 total fixtures and could not reach
24 after ordinary attrition. The successor creates eight candidates per each of
eight recipe strata and requires six eligible primary fixtures per stratum,
with outcome-blind replacement before treatment if needed. Balanced looks are
`N=24, 32, 48` (three, four, and six eligible fixtures per stratum), not pod
boundaries.

### Sequential confidence

The proposed t bound is exact only under independent normal stratum errors. The
successor says this explicitly, simulates the complete selector/stopping rule
under correlated, skewed, heavy-tailed, rare-responder, and contaminated
distributions, and reports stratified bootstrap plus finite-sample bounded/tail
sensitivity analyses. Alpha spending is `0.005, 0.010, 0.035`, divided across
the two primary cells. Fixture order and look masks are committed before any
treatment result.

### SESOI

Five percent was not justified by the observed schedule discrepancy. The
successor uses an absolute operational-resolution target of `0.25` nat: about
two to four times the observed 0.06--0.13-nat schedule sensitivity and a
1.284-fold odds change for a one-token target. This is a stopping target, not a
claim of universal practical irrelevance. The observed UCB is always reported.

### Heterogeneity

Two deterministic executions cannot identify a random-effects responder
population. The successor separately reports the recipe prevalence of fixtures
whose best primary-cell recovery exceeds a frozen `0.5`-nat threshold, using an
exact binomial interval on the fixture-level any-cell indicator. Mean and tail
results never cancel each other.

### Behavioral flips

The old 0/24 11.7% bound ignored sequential peeking and cell selection. The
successor defines one fixture-level endpoint in advance: whether either named
primary cell changes the damaged fresh answer to the exact correct target. It
uses the alpha ledger at sequential looks or labels a fixed-final bound only.

### Render and host replication

Exact reruns estimate deterministic reproducibility, not render variance. Each
fixture therefore has two seeded stochastic carrier renders, one originating
under C and one under W, with exact tokens forced under C/W/F. Origin is balanced
and reported. A second host replays saved artifacts under a fixed absolute
tolerance; it does not estimate a host population variance from a handful of
machines.

### Controls

Fable's full-KV token shuffle is invalid because cached Qwen keys are post-RoPE;
moving them changes positional phase. The successor uses fresh K plus a
fixed-point-free whole-row V permutation selected from a frozen geometry/state-
only list, and retains C/W wrong-history full-KV at identical positions.

## Chosen primary design

- **Design ID:** `coherent-state-powered-successor-v13`.
- **Exact subject:** pinned Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 bf16/eager stack.
- **Population:** equal-weight mixture of eight fixed engineered rule/template
  strata, conditional on content, target-neutral-render, oracle, and at least
  5-nat correct-target/margin damage gates.
- **Primary locus/schedule:** v12-style maximal carrier boundary R2 under the
  role-native q=1 schedule N.
- **Primary cells:** correct-source full-KV and correct-source value-only,
  each versus the shared fresh baseline.
- **Controls:** wrong-history full-KV/value-only, exact identity, applied
  whole-bf16 V permutation, nonfocal outcome, and a frozen P-schedule audit
  subset.
- **Renders:** two stochastic target-neutral subject handoffs per fixture,
  one C-origin and one W-origin; averaged within fixture.
- **Looks:** balanced `N=24, 32, 48`; stop when the maximum simultaneous
  one-sided 95% UCB across the two primary cells is at most `0.25` nat, otherwise
  continue to the next look. At 48, report the result whether resolved or not.
- **Legacy sensitivity:** all of e02--e06, rebound to the successor protocol.
- **First surplus:** downstream/retained-tail locus discovery on treatment-
  untouched reserve fixtures followed by confirmation on a disjoint reserve
  set; maximal visible-state restart is next.

## Compute disposition

At the observed effective provider rate near `$1.42/hour`, the audited mixed-
length N=48 six-arm/two-render core envelope is `$28.47--33.68`. Freeze:

- `$1.50` admission/gate/pilots;
- `$18.00` through N=24;
- `$16.70` conditional continuation through N=48;
- `$4.50` independent-host audit;
- `$12.00` locked failure/CI reserve;
- the remainder initially unallocated and later released by observed costs.

The full primary may proceed only if the pilot's live projection plus host audit
and locked reserve fits the then-current provider balance and the core projection
is at most `$35`. This is a spending control, not a reason to stop at a
statistically inadequate N.
