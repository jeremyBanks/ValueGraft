## Main developments

- The prior paper was judged unready for external sharing. Its synthetic corpus
  provenance was misstated, the held-out result was a source-conditional sign
  reversal rather than a homogeneous null, three compression conditions
  reconstructed the wrong request, grafts covered summary plus retained-tail
  rows, provenance artifacts were incomplete, and placebo results did not
  establish semantic specificity.
- The strongest prior empirical claims were narrowed:
  - synthetic held-out effects were approximately `+0.120` nats/token on
    Qwen-rendered bodies and `−0.050` on Claude-authored bodies, with pooled
    `−0.003`;
  - the old SWE scalar effect was null when unique trajectories and the
    independent pool were combined (`+0.0053`, CI spanning zero);
  - a tuned SWE layer map produced a small out-of-fitting teacher-forced
    likelihood signal (`+0.0135` over 102 trajectories), but not task success,
    action correctness, or superiority to the scalar graft.
- A prior-art review confirmed that generation-time KV state can carry
  information beyond re-encoded text, while downstream-note work suggests that
  useful state may be written onto boundary or aggregator tokens rather than the
  summary content rows alone. These results constrain, but do not settle, the
  repository’s narrower training-free intervention question.
- The coherent-state apparatus repeatedly caught its own defects before semantic
  scoring: position/key re-rotation was retired, the cyclic wrong-history
  control was invalidated, pseudoreplicated periodic schedule fixtures were
  rejected, and multiple independent harvest and provenance counterexamples led
  to successive validator amendments.
- The exact local c10 schedule-origin diagnostic completed as
  `QUERY_SHAPE_ROUNDING`. Identical protected prefixes in equal-shaped branches
  remained bit-identical despite changed future tokens, while short-query versus
  long-query execution diverged after attention. This rules out the tested
  future-token leak but does not generalize to 30B/A100 or make the numerical
  differences negligible.
- The v10 experiment was permanently invalidated as an authorizing instrument.
  Natural c10 and c02 cases showed large schedule-dependent bf16 divergence at
  decision-relevant scale; no paid semantic run launched and no semantic outcome
  exists. The old seven-row synthetic gate was exposed as one repeated
  five-token stream, and the wrong-history arm as repetitive donor corruption
  rather than a coherent counterfactual.
- Strategy pivoted away from completing the twelve-case paired-v11 corpus. The
  next proposed study is an additive v12 carrier-state canary: four reviewed
  engineered cases, a primary event-aligned R2 carrier region, full-KV and
  value-only contrasts, focal-selectivity controls, a norm-matched placebo, and
  explicit P/N schedule comparison. Canary cases are permanently excluded from
  later confirmation.
- The canary design was progressively corrected: turn-addition call shapes,
  carrier-before-tail ordering, model-call-aligned region boundaries,
  pre-carrier length gates, bf16-representable path controls, frozen P
  schedules, exact fresh-destination maps, and separate technical, eligibility,
  and treatment release phases. No subject-model forward has yet occurred.

## Decisions and durable clarifications

- A green local ladder is construction evidence, not scientific evidence and not
  certification of the 30B numerical regime. Paid-stack gates must rerun on the
  exact checkpoint, tokenizer, dtype, backend, and commit before semantic
  scoring.
- Schedule equivalence is no longer assumed. P and O are distinct replay
  protocols; any claim must be schedule-specific or survive a preregistered
  estimand-level robustness comparison. Neither represents native live-agent
  cache state.
- The primary semantic question is correct-history versus coherent minimally
  counterfactual history under identical visible carrier text and matched
  geometry. Fresh-versus-correct is a utility contrast contaminated by
  source/destination execution differences, not the cleanest semantic causal
  contrast.
- Forced-summary studies are controlled-mediator experiments. They can measure
  state dependence at fixed visible text, but may contain history-summary
  contradiction; leakage, support, and target-neutrality must be assessed before
  treatment outcomes are unblinded.
- R2 is the sole canary stop/go region and includes carrier content plus the
  complete structural carrier-close/anchor call. R1 and R3 are descriptive
  localization arms. Full-KV success does not validate value-only grafting.
- The repository’s durable methodological principle is: a gate’s fixtures must
  be capable of failing for the failure mode the gate is intended to detect.
  Coverage counts, repeated parameterizations, and self-consistent validators
  must not be treated as independent scientific evidence.

## Empirical observations and caveats

- On Qwen3-0.6B CPU/bf16/eager, c10’s ordinary versus coarse replay schedules
  produced K/V maxima of `16.125/5.125` and a selected-margin shift of about
  `0.06055` nats; c02 independently shifted by about `0.132` nats. These are
  apparatus observations, not semantic effects.
- The schedule-origin result is strong evidence for query-shape-dependent
  finite-precision arithmetic on that configuration, but not for a universal
  mechanism or exact 30B behavior.
- The banked 30B render corpus is duplicate text, not independent replication or
  preserved native KV state; 226 of 264 assistant replies hit the token cap, and
  runtime provenance is incomplete.
- Revision-1 canary stimuli failed source-length and blind content/diversity
  review. Later revisions passed mechanical, paired, and diversity review, but
  the runtime and harvest stack remains non-authorizing. No model weights were
  loaded during these validations.
- Archive maintenance selectively repaired overlong conversation summaries and
  refreshed naming/rollups without changing experiment or gate artifacts;
  historical note references may therefore have shifted.

- Complete the bounded v12 store, lossless tensor evidence, independent
  harvester, phase-separated release receipts, budget enforcement, and exact
  model-facing runner.
- Freeze the final canary specification, including literal stimuli and reserve
  cases, arm matrix, targets, thresholds, schedule-ratio formula, region
  intervals, positive control, and exhaustive stop logic.
- Rerun all mechanical, blind-review, provenance, monitor, and local Qwen3-0.6B
  tests on one clean exact commit. Then perform only the paid technical/gate
  subset before any semantic score, with the initial spend capped at the
  authorized small tranche.
- Treat any schedule instability at the target-effect scale, failed positive
  control, unsupported counterfactual, provenance mismatch, or harvest
  inconsistency as a stopping result rather than a reason to relax thresholds.
- Begin the corrected methodological paper in parallel. Its durable contribution
  is the bounded interpretation of the old graft, source/provenance corrections,
  schedule-dependent bf16 measurement floor, invalid-control postmortem, and
  fail-closed validation lessons—not a claim of general KV-memory absence or
  coding-agent improvement.
