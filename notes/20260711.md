The principal scientific conclusion is now narrow: generation-time KV state may
preserve information unavailable to fresh re-encoding, consistent with recent
prior work, but the repository has not shown that a training-free post-prefill
old-value/fresh-key graft reliably recovers it. The earlier synthetic result is
not a homogeneous native-model null: c07–c12 used Qwen3-4B/4-bit-rendered bodies
and showed a positive effect, while Claude-authored c13–c24 showed a negative
effect. Three compression conditions also reconstructed state under the wrong
summary request, invalidating the intended severity comparison. The intervention
affected both summary and retained-tail regions, and the main synthetic states
were reconstructed prefills rather than preserved generation snapshots.

SWE-Gym evidence remains proxy-level. The fixed scalar effect was heterogeneous
and null when unique trajectories were pooled; the tuned layer map was positive
on disjoint evaluation subsets, including the latest 45-row confirmation, but
lacks a matched placebo and measures teacher-forced next-action
likelihood/action matching rather than executed coding success. The selected
action metric is additionally coarse, and the original trajectories were
authored by other models. Exact SWE summaries, token IDs, contexts, and complete
code provenance were not preserved, limiting reproducibility.

The paper should therefore retain its negative-result and audit framing while
correcting provenance, intervention-region, compression, placebo,
reproducibility, related-work, and claim-strength statements. It is not ready
for external sharing. Recent primary-source checks established that MEMENTO
directly reports a restart loss on AIME24 (66.1% versus 50.8%, with unequal
repetition counts) and that _Models Take Notes at Prefill_ places causal
information on downstream aggregator tokens; these works constrain
interpretation and motivate future downstream-row assays, but do not establish
the same effect for this checkpoint or intervention.

Methodological decisions now governing the experiment are:

- v10 uses eager attention, position-preserving logical gaps, contiguous
  physical cache storage, and bit-copied coherent summary K/V; lossy key
  rerotation and delta-mismatched `G_delta` placebos are retired.
- Co-primary contrasts are `G_correct−G_fresh` and `G_correct−G_wrong`,
  evaluated at the conversation level. N=6 supports competence/headroom and
  futility decisions only; efficacy requires both final N=12 intervals to clear
  zero.
- A null is bounded to the fixed checkpoint, benchmark, eager backend, and
  position-preserving layout. It does not address packed serving, other
  backends/models, training, or live-agent performance.
- Direct retained-tail transplantation is rejected for v10 because source and
  destination tail positions and causal ordering differ;
  downstream/request/header or matched tail factorials belong in a separately
  designed follow-up.
