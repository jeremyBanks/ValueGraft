_The UTC day completed a clean Luna-configurable transcript-archive rebuild and
a broad scientific audit. The repository now has stronger provenance and
notes-hygiene guarantees, while the research conclusion has narrowed: coherent
generation-time state remains plausible, but naive post-prefill value-only
grafting has not shown a robust general benefit._

**Participants/contributors:** User; gpt-5.6-sol-xhigh; claude-opus-4-8.

The archive workflow is now consolidated behind one provider-configurable
command supporting extraction, summarization, filename normalization, and
recursive daily/monthly/yearly/archive rollups. It preserves exact
source-supported contributor identities, filters subagent material to final
responses, prevents summarizer self-ingestion, places finals chronologically,
and stages generated replacements atomically. The Luna rebuild produced 55/55
valid conversation notes, 10/10 valid rollups, preserved 269 opaque source IDs,
and passed 56 tests. Filename and referent-filtering rules were tightened;
exclusion filtering now targets subjects and contextual clues rather than
literal strings alone.

The scientific record requires substantial qualification:

- The held-out synthetic set is source-heterogeneous, not uniformly native to
  the test model. Qwen-rendered conversations showed a positive per-layer effect
  of about +0.120 nats/token, while Claude-authored conversations showed about
  −0.050; the pooled value near −0.003 is therefore an arithmetic mixture, not a
  homogeneous universal null.
- Three of four compression-sweep conditions appear methodologically invalid
  because summary generation and write-time-state reconstruction used different
  requests. Only the realistic condition is currently request-consistent.
- The graft affects both retained-tail and summary-token regions, so existing
  results cannot be attributed to summary-state retention alone.
- Shuffled and Gaussian placebos are not treatment-delta-matched; correct values
  being less harmful than mismatched values does not by itself establish
  semantic recovery.
- The historical SWE-Gym scalar result did not independently replicate: pooled
  unique-trajectory scoring was approximately +0.0053 nats/token with an
  interval spanning zero. A tuned champion produced a small positive result,
  reported as roughly +0.0126 pooled across 143 trajectories and +0.0135 on
  out-of-sample-only data, but its advantage over the fixed scalar graft was not
  decisive.
- SWE-Gym measures teacher-forced next-action likelihood or coarse action
  matching on imported GPT-4o/Claude trajectories. It does not measure executed
  coding-agent success, patch resolution, or test outcomes.
- SWE artifacts lack exact generated summaries, complete token/context render
  state, and reliable code-commit provenance, limiting reproducibility.

The persistent methodological distinction is between information surviving in
coherent original K+V state and the much narrower intervention of replacing
fresh values while retaining fresh keys and upper-layer inconsistencies. The
evidence supports a qualified negative result for the latter, not a claim that
latent continuity is absent or that values are useless. The speculative
mechanism note identifies re-derivability, useful-subspace redundancy,
layer-specific interference, procedural-state density, and attention/key
mismatch as hypotheses; gradient-projection analysis is the most decisive
near-term test.

The paper remains held for one holistic correction pass. That pass should
correct body provenance, source-stratified synthetic results, compression
validity, graft-region attribution, placebo interpretation, SWE metric scope,
missing render artifacts, and commit-recording claims. The recommended next
experiment is a controlled same-text comparison of coherent original K+V,
identical-text fresh restart, wrong-history context, old-V/fresh-K graft, and a
treatment-delta-matched placebo. Only after local apparatus and capability gates
pass should the project proceed to a modest model-native agent study with
common-prefix workspace forks and executed tests.
