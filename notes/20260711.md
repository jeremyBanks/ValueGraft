The paper and prior empirical record require substantial narrowing. The held-out
synthetic set is mixed-source rather than model-native and shows a
source-conditional reversal: approximately +0.120 nats/token on
Qwen3-4B-rendered bodies versus −0.050 on Claude-authored bodies, with a pooled

reconstruct write-time state under a different request. The graft also affects

task success, and only 45 of the planned 75 confirmation trajectories were
completed.

The scientific interpretation now distinguishes several claims that had been
conflated:

- generation-time KV state may contain useful information beyond summary text,
  supported by recent MEMENTO and downstream-note prior art;
- a coherent full-state channel, if present, is distinct from the utility of an
  old-V/fresh-K transplant;
- a summary-region null does not exclude information stored on boundary,
  aggregator, or downstream rows;
- correct-versus-fresh is a utility contrast, while correct-versus-coherent
  minimally counterfactual history is the cleaner history-specificity contrast;
- all results are checkpoint-, backend-, schedule-, layout-, and
  benchmark-specific unless separately demonstrated otherwise.

The original cyclic wrong-history control is retired. It preserved lengths and
structural slots but repeatedly recycled short donor phrases—up to 51 cycles in
one slot—so it compared coherent history with repetitive corruption rather than
a coherent counterfactual. Future controls must be plant-specific, minimally
counterfactual, token-length and schedule matched, naturally coherent, and
independently reviewed. Likewise, the schedule called “message-aligned” was
found to be a coarse system/whole-history/request partition, not true
turn-by-turn replay.

A major apparatus result is the failure of the v10 schedule-invariance premise.
On Qwen3-0.6B CPU bf16 eager attention, natural c10 and c02 prefixes showed
large deterministic differences between schedules, including selected-margin
shifts of about 0.0605 and 0.1318 nats. The preregistered c10 origin diagnostic
found identical protected rows when query shape and future content were matched,
but divergence between a 23-token query and the same prefix as the beginning of
a 4096-token query; this supports query-shape-dependent bf16 arithmetic rather
than tested future-token leakage. The earlier 7/7 exact-zero synthetic gate was
pseudoreplicated over one periodic five-token stream and cannot validate natural
inputs. These failures are apparatus evidence, not semantic results, and v10 is
permanently non-authorizing.
