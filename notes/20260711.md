_The day narrowed ValueGraft’s claims substantially: prior positive and null
results were re-audited, the v10 coherent-state experiment was permanently
invalidated as an authorizing instrument by realistic schedule-dependent bf16
divergence, and the original wrong-history control was shown to be
distributionally invalid. The project now favors a small exploratory decision
canary and a methodological paper over completing the large confirmatory
corpus._

**Participants/contributors:** User; `gpt-5.6-sol-xhigh` (extra-high reasoning);
`claude-opus-4-8`; and `claude-fable-5`.

The earlier paper is not ready for external release. Its held-out synthetic
corpus was mixed-source rather than native to the test model, with opposite
subgroup effects: approximately +0.120 nats/token for Qwen-rendered bodies and
−0.050 for Claude-authored bodies, producing a misleading pooled near-null.
Three compression conditions reconstructed write-time states under the wrong
request; the intervention covered summary and retained-tail regions rather than
summary alone; and the primary harness reconstructed rather than preserved the
actual generation-time state. Placebos were not perturbation-matched, so aligned
values being less harmful than shuffled or Gaussian substitutions does not
establish semantic specificity. The most credible coding result remains a small
selected-layer improvement in teacher-forced next-action likelihood on
out-of-fitting trajectories, not task success; the fixed scalar result was null
on the independent pool, and only 45 of the planned 75 confirmation trajectories
were completed.

The central methodological result is now stronger. The v10 local ladder passed
seven synthetic schedule rows made from one repeating five-token phrase, which
was pseudoreplication and could not validate realistic content. The first
realistic c10 case failed dramatically under ordinary versus coarse
history/request chunking: K/V maxima were 16.125/5.125 and the selected margin
shifted by about 0.0605 nats. The preregistered origin diagnostic showed
identical protected rows when future content changed at the same query shape,
but divergence when the same prefix was processed as a short query versus the
leading rows of a long query. This supports query-shape-dependent bf16
arithmetic, not a tested future-token leak, on the pinned 0.6B CPU/eager
configuration. c02 independently showed a comparable schedule-sensitive margin
shift. These results do not establish the exact 30B/A100 mechanism, but they
show that schedule variation can occur at the scale of the hypothesized effect;
v10 therefore cannot authorize semantic interpretation or paid execution.

A separate raw-input audit found that the frozen `G_wrong` arm repeatedly cycled
very short donor phrases to fill long message slots, in one case repeating a
seven-token acknowledgement 51 times. Although token counts, structural
positions, and spans were mechanically exact, the control compared coherent
history with repetitive corrupted text rather than with a coherent wrong
history. Its co-primary specificity contrast is invalid for the intended claim.
The replacement direction is a plant-specific minimally counterfactual history:
alter only the focal fact and all necessary downstream references, preserve
message-level token widths and schedules, and require blind decoded-coherence
review. Existing feasibility witnesses are not yet frozen or execution-ready.

The project’s terminology and estimands should remain separated:

- `Correct − Fresh` is a utility contrast between source-conditioned state and a
  fresh compacted destination; it includes execution-protocol differences and is
  not by itself a clean semantic contrast.
- Correct versus a coherent plant-specific counterfactual under the same
  schedule is the cleaner history-specificity contrast.
- Full coherent K+V retention, value-only grafting, and any key/value split
  answer different questions and must not be conflated.
- A margin increase is insufficient evidence of improved competence;
  correct-target likelihood must also be examined, alongside the counterfactual
  target and focal selectivity.
- Turn-aligned replay and ordinary chunking are distinct deterministic
  protocols, not interchangeable replicas or live-agent cache states. Any result
  must name its schedule and report schedule interaction.
- A null summary-row assay would only constrain downstream-usable state in the
  tested summary rows. Prior work indicates that information may be written onto
  downstream boundary, delimiter, tail, or aggregator tokens; it does not
  justify a claim that generation-time state carries no useful information.

The large paired-v11 corpus is paused. Its drafts and checkpoints are preserved
as engineering fixtures, but they share an engineered planning-dialogue scaffold
and have not passed final diversity or coherence review. The banked 30B render
corpus is not a substitute: most assistant replies hit the 320-token cap, and
its revision, backend, dtype, raw IDs, and native cache provenance are
incomplete. No semantic v11 forward pass has occurred.

The binding next step is a separate exploratory decision canary, permanently
excluded from any future confirmatory sample. First complete a $0 end-to-end
local apparatus test. Then, only if it passes, run a tightly capped exact-30B
canary with an engineered carrier stratum before harder conversation cases. The
paid stack must rerun its own 30B technical gates before semantic scoring, use a
positive-control-first abort rule, keep any anchor text identical across region
arms, and freeze the decision-carrying region and stopping rules in advance.
Required evidence includes full-history competence, measurable compaction
damage, a history-directional and focal-selective coherent-state effect,
correct-target improvement, separately reported value-only behavior, acceptable
summary leakage, and schedule noise materially below the observed signal. A
failed or ambiguous canary should terminate the rescue search under the current
budget.

The corrected paper should proceed in parallel. Its durable contribution is a
bounded account of fragile source provenance, non-replicating proxy
improvements, invalid controls, pseudoreplicated gates, and schedule-dependent
bf16 cache state discovered before paid semantic execution. The notes-archive
repair for overlong conversation summaries is complete and preserves the
affected material with corrected splitting and regenerated rollups.
