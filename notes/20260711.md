The principal research conclusions are now:

- The earlier value-only graft supports only a narrow, apparatus-specific
  negative result. It does not test whether coherent generation-time state
  preserves information beyond summary text, and it cannot establish that
  write-time values are useless.
- The held-out synthetic corpus was misrepresented as model-native. Its bodies
  were rendered by Qwen3-4B 4-bit for one subgroup and authored by Claude-family
  contributors for another. Effects reversed by source: approximately +0.120
  nats/token for the Qwen-rendered subset and −0.050 for the Claude-authored
  subset, yielding a misleading pooled null near −0.003.
- The compression sweep is not interpretable as reported because three levels
  reconstructed write-time states under a request different from the one used to
  generate the summary. The graft also covered summary and retained-tail
  regions, while the paper often described summary-only intervention.
- The strongest coding result is a small held-out teacher-forced next-action
  likelihood signal from a layer map selected on separate data: about +0.0117
  nats/token on 57 evaluation trajectories and +0.0158 on 45 additional
  confirmation trajectories, pooled around +0.0135. This remains a proxy over
  foreign GPT-4o/Claude trajectories, not agent task success; the selected map
  lacks a matched placebo, and no patch or test was executed.
- The project’s prior “content-specificity” interpretation was downgraded.
  Correct values are less destructive than shuffled or Gaussian substitutions,
  but those controls were not treatment-delta matched and therefore do not
  isolate semantic information.

The paper is held for one holistic correction pass. Required wording must
distinguish: existence of a history-conditioned channel; correct-history
specificity; coherent K/V retention; old-V/fresh-K utility; and live-agent
benefit. Log-probability differences are nats/token, not mathematically bounded
quantities. Snapshot hashes are provenance witnesses, not archived reusable
tensors.

The archive workflow was substantially repaired. A fresh Luna rebuild produced
55 conversation notes, rollups, exact model-based participant records, opaque
source lists, strengthened referent filtering, and 56 passing tests. A
transcript-boundary bug in incremental regeneration was also fixed: overlong
conversation notes are now split using the established six-hour policy, with
selective regeneration from a frozen input snapshot.

The experimental design pivoted from packed post-RoPE transplantation to a
same-position gapped-cache assay. The frozen amendment uses contiguous physical
cache storage with natural logical/RoPE positions, bit-for-bit copied
correct-history K/V, identical summary tokens across fresh and wrong-history
arms, exact-length wrong-history substitutions, and per-arm downstream
recomputation. Primary contrasts are correct coherent state versus fresh restart
and versus matched wrong history; optional V-only, K-only, and delta-matched
placebo arms remain secondary and must not be pooled with the primary assay.

A first paid A100 attempt correctly failed closed before semantic scoring. Its
whole-model bf16 position-shift diagnostic showed large K/V and logit drift,
approximately $0.066 in cost, and the pod was terminated safely. The evidence
measures numerical divergence from independently shifted 48-layer bf16
trajectories—not the scientific hypothesis—and must remain preserved as
apparatus evidence.

Subsequent validation found further issues in v7/v8/v9 release contracts,
including fabricated semantic evidence, incomplete source binding, invalid

authorization pending a fresh ladder and exact-commit reviews.

concurrently only if semantic authorization mechanically requires independently
validated local and production gates. The retained-tail proposal was rejected
for v10: source tail tokens occur before the request and summary, while
destination tail tokens occur afterward, so rotation-free bit-copying is
impossible and would violate the frozen geometry. Tail or downstream-aggregator
hypotheses belong in a separately preregistered follow-up.

Current status is pre-launch. The local 0.6B ladder reached production-token
schedules but encountered a strict equivalence failure; no paid pod or semantic

suite and monitor tests, obtain independent code/science/cross-family reviews on

of all authorization artifacts.

Likely next scientific work is a narrowly controlled model-native comparison of
coherent correct-history, fresh identical-text, wrong-history, V-only, K-only,
and matched-placebo states, followed—only if a channel and usable treatment are
