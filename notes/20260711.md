_The day narrowed ValueGraft’s claims substantially while strengthening its
methodological record: prior results were re-audited, the coherent-state
apparatus repeatedly failed closed before semantic scoring, and
schedule-dependent bf16 computation was isolated as a major resolution limit.
The project is now paused pending a smaller, independently reviewed paired assay
rather than further expansion of the invalidated design._

**Participants/contributors:** User; `gpt-5.6-sol-xhigh` (extra-high reasoning);
`claude-opus-4-8`; `claude-fable-5`.

The earlier paper cannot yet serve as a final scientific record. Its synthetic
held-out corpus is mixed-source, showing an exploratory source-conditional
reversal rather than a homogeneous null; its compression sweep partly
reconstructs the wrong request; its graft affects summary and retained-tail
regions; and its SWE results are offline teacher-imitation proxies with
incomplete render provenance. The strongest retained evidence is a small,
provisional coding-likelihood signal, not demonstrated agent success. Recent
prior work supports the broader possibility of useful generation-time KV
information, but does not validate ValueGraft’s particular intervention.

The coherent-state program then exposed several independent design problems:

- The original bf16 paid attempt correctly halted before semantic scoring when a
  position-shift diagnostic failed. A redesigned gapped assay later failed its
  30B zero-gap technical gate because contiguous and split bf16/MoE execution
  diverged dramatically; tolerances were not loosened.
- The v10 local ladder passed seven synthetic schedule rows built from one
  repeating five-token stream, but failed on realistic c10 and c02 prefixes. c10
  produced a selected-margin shift of about 0.0605 nats, with K/V maxima of
  16.125/5.125; c02 independently produced a margin shift of about 0.1318 nats.
  The preregistered origin test found identical-shape branches bit-identical on
  protected earlier rows, classifying the c10 failure as query-shape-dependent
  bf16 rounding rather than a tested future-token leak. This applies to the
  pinned 0.6B CPU/eager configuration and does not automatically generalize to
  30B/A100.
- The frozen wrong-history arm was mechanically position- and length-matched but
  scientifically invalid: short donor phrases were cyclically repeated up to 51
  times, creating repetitive corrupted histories rather than coherent
  counterfactuals. Its co-primary specificity contrast is retired.
- The banked 30B conversation corpus is duplicate, heavily capped, and
  incompletely runtime-provenanced. It may be a text candidate after review, but
  not native cache evidence or an independent replication.
- Independent audits clarified that “message-aligned” schedules were actually
  coarse system/whole-history/request partitions, and that technical coverage
  counts, repeated fixtures, calibration variants, and donor constructions must
  not be treated as independent scientific evidence.

Terminology and methodological boundaries are now explicit. Schedule equivalence
is not assumed; canonical and alternative schedules must be compared at the
estimand level, with difference-in-differences. Small-model ladders certify
implementation paths, not production numerical behavior. A summary-only null can
only bound a channel in the tested summary rows and cannot exclude information
written onto downstream delimiters, tail, or aggregator tokens. Correct-history
versus fresh encoding (`GF`) and correct-history versus a plant-specific
minimally counterfactual history (`GMC`) are distinct estimands. Any future
counterfactual must preserve message widths and structure while changing only
the focal fact and necessary downstream references, and must pass blind
decoded-coherence review.

The immediate research direction is a new additive paired-v11 corpus and assay:

- Freeze twelve concise, diverse, fully authored conversations before model
  outcomes, each with referent and overloaded-sense plants plus coherent
  plant-specific counterfactual histories.
- Compare turn-aligned replay (`P`), ordinary 4096-token chunking (`O`), and the
  fixed gapped destination (`D`), treating schedule interaction as an observed
  quantity rather than noise to be averaged away.
- Score full coherent state, fresh encoding, and focal counterfactual arms, with
  V-only diagnostics clearly separated from full K+V retention.
- Require deterministic replay, full-history competence and compaction headroom,
  correct-target and counterfactual components, focal selectivity, durable
  provenance, and independent harvest reconstruction.
- Begin with a $0 two-case local decomposition, then consider only a small
  exact-30B technical canary if the apparatus and corpus pass fresh reviews. No
  semantic run, N=12 expansion, or live-agent study is currently authorized.

Outstanding risks include whether schedule effects cancel in the intended
contrasts, whether a useful channel resides outside the summary rows, whether
natural counterfactuals can be made exactly length-matched without semantic
artifacts, and whether long-context bf16 execution has sufficient precision for
the proposed effect sizes. The likely next actions are to preserve v10 as a
terminal non-authorizing methodological record, complete and review the
paired-v11 corpus contract, run the local decomposition, and begin the paper’s
holistic correction/postmortem in parallel.
