The archive workflow was consolidated behind a provider-configurable command
supporting extraction, summarization, filename normalization, and hierarchical
rollups. Luna and Claude execution paths now use nonpersistent workers, explicit
summary-worker exclusion, narrow inclusion of final subagent assessments,
chronological insertion, contributor provenance, referent-level filtering, and

The research interpretation changed materially. The held-out synthetic bodies
were not native generations from the 30B test model: c07–c12 used Qwen3-4B 4-bit
outputs, while c13–c24 were Claude/Fable-authored. Recomputed effects reverse by
source block: approximately +0.120 nats/token for c07–c12 and −0.050 for
c13–c24, with a pooled value near −0.003. The pooled result is therefore an
arithmetic mixture of opposing source-conditional effects, not a clean universal
null. The subgroup estimates are exploratory and confounded with other source or
corpus properties.

The broader evidence still does not establish that naive post-prefill, aligned
old-value/fresh-key grafting reliably improves semantic continuity or agent
performance. Correct values are less damaging than shuffled or Gaussian
controls, but those placebos are not matched to treatment-delta norm or
covariance, so the contrast demonstrates compatibility and harm avoidance rather
than hidden-history specificity. The intervention affects summary and
retained-tail positions together, not summary tokens alone. The compression
sweep is also methodologically suspect: level-specific summary requests may not
match the request used to reconstruct write-time states; only the realistic
condition is currently considered secure.

SWE-Gym remains a small, domain-specific lead rather than a task-success result.
The fixed graft is heterogeneous across the original 75 and independent 98
trajectories, with pooled unique-trajectory effect about +0.0053 nats/token and
an interval spanning zero. A per-layer-tuned champion is positive on its
internal held-out split, approximately +0.0117 to +0.0126 nats/token, but does
not clearly outperform the fixed scalar graft and has not demonstrated executed
patches, tests, or agent resolution. The trajectories and target actions are
foreign successful demonstrations, and the expensive SWE render artifacts omit
exact summaries, token IDs, alignments, and complete code provenance.

Terminology and methodological constraints that should persist:

- Distinguish information being present in coherent generation-time KV state
  from successful transplantation of selected value vectors.
- Describe the synthetic result as source-conditional and exploratory, not as a
  homogeneous clean null.
- Describe `E-B` as a mean log-probability difference in nats/token; it is not
  mathematically bounded.
- Call the intervention “summary + retained tail” unless a
  summary-only/tail-only factorial is run.
- Treat action-match metrics as coarse tool/path agreement unless edit contents
  are checked.
- Preserve exact model, data, request, token, cache, and code provenance for
  every future render.
- Do not promote the README externally until provenance, compression
  methodology, placebo limitations, and conclusions are corrected together.

Outstanding work is intentionally narrow. First complete the final-paper audit
and one holistic correction pass, verifying the compression request trace and
action-match implementation. Then perform artifact rescoring and a small local
causal canary comparing coherent original state, identical-text fresh restart,
matched wrong history, old-value/fresh-key graft, and a treatment-delta-matched
placebo. Only if those gates succeed should a capability-matched model-native
experiment proceed, followed by a real agent pilot with environment forks and
tests. Broad architecture, alpha, layer, or imported single-action sweeps should
not receive further budget. Compute spending is complete, pods are terminated,
notes regeneration and renaming remain out of scope, and external publication
stays paused.
