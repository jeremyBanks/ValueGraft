# Local coherent-state N48 v2 — frozen design

**Design ID:** `coherent-state-local-mlx-n48-v2`  
**Status:** `DESIGN_AND_FIXED_CARRIERS_FROZEN_NO_V2_FORWARD_PASS`  
**Subject:** `mlx-community/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507-4bit`, revision
`e9675aa3ca5f900ccef55267914466d55ab325fa`  
**Backend:** Apple MLX, mixed 4-bit/8-bit weight conversion, bf16 runtime
tensors, value-only intervention.

V1 is closed before treatment and archived under the slug
`local-coherent-state-n48-v1-failed-stochastic-carrier`: its prompt required
shared background while its filter forbade the nouns that constituted that
background, yielding 0/48 accepted attempts on all three frozen seeds across

computed. V2 reuses only carrier-invariant, outcome-blind objects: the recipe
frame, random permutation, literal base fixtures, and base-history reviews.

## Question and claim boundary

For a sensitivity-enriched paired conversation corpus, do value states written
for a fixed, externally authored, contextually neutral token sequence while the
subject is forced through that sequence under full history C versus W contain
history-specific information that improves the exact hidden answer after the
identical sequence is freshly encoded without that history?

This is an off-policy forced-carrier experiment. It does not establish behavior
of the model's naturally generated summaries or production compaction. Its
scope is exactly this model, revision, backend, precision, context construction,
and two fixed carrier texts.

## Frozen frame, selection, and independent unit

The base frame remains the deterministic 32,768-member powered-v13 recipe:
4,096 candidates in each of eight rule strata. The already-pushed OS-random
seed record and complete PCG64 literal permutation were frozen before any
candidate text. Ranks 1–10 in each stratum are the only candidate pool.

One paired C/W fixture is one unit. The first six eligible candidates in frozen
rank order in each stratum form fixed N=48. Complete C/W histories undergo
outcome-blind review for coherence, opposite target entailment, minimal change,
retained visible-context neutrality, and absence of focal leakage. Invalid
candidates advance only to the next frozen rank. If any stratum has fewer than
six eligible ranks among 1–10, v2 terminates rather than revising the pool.

## Two fixed carrier conditions

The exact carrier bank is
`data/coherent_state_local_n48_v2/fixed-carriers-v1.json`. It contains two
externally authored generic texts (`fixed_a`, `fixed_b`), their UTF-8 hashes,
word counts, and provenance. Neither was generated by the subject or selected
using a v2 score. Before the first v2 forward pass, both texts must pass against
all 80 frozen fixtures:

- 40–60 whitespace words and 40–80 exact content tokens;
- no special token, digit, target/countertarget, changed value, focal/nonfocal
  fact, rule branch, contextual name, or forbidden surface;
- independent target-aware compatibility with both C and W;
- exact token IDs at the corresponding C, W, and packed-fresh carrier spans.

These are two fixed levels of a carrier-text factor, not random render draws.
For every conversation, primary effects are averaged across the two levels
before inference. They never increase N. Per-carrier effects are reported as
fixed-condition sensitivity results; opposite signs limit any claim to the
specific carrier exhibiting it.

## Source, destination, and forcing schedule

For each C/W history, insert the fixed carrier request and one fixed carrier
before a neutral acknowledgment and the byte-identical retained tail. The exact
carrier IDs are forced one token per model call under C and W. Remaining source
tokens use the same fixed chunked prefill schedule. Persist per-token forced
carrier log probabilities under C and W; they are manipulation diagnostics and
never eligibility gates.

The packed fresh destination contains the original system message, carrier
request, identical fixed carrier, acknowledgment, and retained tail. Earlier
focal history is absent. Persist fresh carrier token log probabilities as the
third surprise diagnostic. Values are position-unrotated; source value rows for
exact visible token twins are mapped to their packed fresh destinations.
Post-RoPE keys are never transplanted.

An exact forcing identity gate compares B built by the production forcing
schedule with an independent fresh encoding at the same packed positions.
Carrier IDs, complete cache, next logits, and target scores must be bit-exact.
Failure voids v2; thresholds/text are not patched in place.

## Arms and applied controls

Required states per fixture/carrier condition:

- `A_C`, `A_W`: untouched full-history oracle contexts;
- `B`: packed fresh visible context;
- `B_sham`: B's own values routed through the exact assignment path, bit-exact;
- `E_C`: fresh keys plus correct-C write-time values at exact visible twins;
- `E_W`: fresh keys plus wrong-W write-time values at the same destinations;
- `VP`: fresh keys plus a deterministic displacement-matched whole-V-row
  placebo selected from C/W/B state geometry without targets or outcomes.

The placebo reuses the audited state-only v13 constructor. `content` is the
carrier-content span; `structural` is the other aligned carrier-request,
acknowledgment, and retained-tail rows. Classes never mix. Its frozen SHA order,
moved-row counts, displacement-ratio interval, active-layer minimum, and cosine
threshold are unchanged. Unavailable remains unavailable. Positive history-
specific language requires E_C to exceed both E_W and VP; the numerical E_C−B
bound remains reportable as control-limited if VP is unavailable.

## Scores and treatment-blind eligibility

From the identical answer position, append the fixture focal probe and
teacher-force C target and W countertarget separately. Score only declared
answer-content IDs. Persist token log probabilities and `L_C`, `L_W`, margin
`M=L_C-L_W`, correct-target damage `Dplus=L_C(A_C)-L_C(B)`, and margin damage
`Dmargin=M(A_C)-M(B)`.

Before treatment, expose only base/carrier review, geometry/identity, A_C/A_W/B
scores, nonfocal visible-fact controls, and forcing diagnostics. Both carrier
conditions must have finite scores, oracle C/W target competence, fresh failure
on C, `Dplus >= 5.0` and `Dmargin >= 5.0` nat/token, normal uncapped oracle
generation, and passing nonfocal/identity checks. E_C, E_W, and VP target
outcomes remain uncomputed until the fixed 48 candidate/carrier pairs and all
eligibility evidence are committed.

## Estimand and confidence rule

For carrier condition `q` of fixture `i`,
`x_iq = L_C(E_C_iq) - L_C(B_iq)`. The fixture effect is
`X_i=(x_i,fixed_a+x_i,fixed_b)/2`; `Z_i=clip(X_i,-0.5,0.5)`. The headline is the
equal-weight mean across the balanced eight strata.

The bounded mean endpoint uses alpha `.04`, giving the N=48 one-sided
distribution-free Hoeffding radius
`sqrt(log(1/.04)/(2*48)) = 0.18311186883717767`. Report both lower and upper
endpoints; the upper endpoint is the requested negative bound. Alpha `.01`
separately bounds prevalence of raw `X_i>0.5`, for at least 95% simultaneous
coverage. Raw mean/t interval, stratified bootstrap, per-carrier fixed effects,
damage, specificity, placebo movement, margin, greedy answers, and nonfocal
outcomes are mandatory companions.

No treatment peeking changes N or selection. Checkpoints every four completed
fixtures report only valid N, elapsed wall time, resource state, and failures.

## Surplus order

After N48 is immutable: separate carrier-only versus retained-tail loci, then
the owner's verbatim doubled-carrier variation with both-copy and second-copy-
only value grafts, followed only then by read-demand or layer/depth variants.
