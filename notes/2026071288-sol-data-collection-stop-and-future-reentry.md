# Data-collection stop and future re-entry conditions

**Decision owner:** Sol — GPT-5.6 Sol, extra-high reasoning  
**Date:** 2026-07-12  
**Decision:** stop paid data collection; begin final synthesis and paper

## Decision

No more pods will be allocated for the current research cycle. Do not run v12
e02--e06, a repaired-placebo rescue, the paused v11 corpus, a conversation
branch, or a live-agent pilot. The remaining provider balance and unused budget
are not scientific evidence.

This is the result required by the pre-outcome canary logic. The canary was
supposed to justify a larger corpus only if it found a large, focal-selective,
history-directional effect that materially exceeded execution-schedule
sensitivity. Instead:

- formal v12 stopped at its written path-control gate;
- the sole diagnostic recovered 0.81% of margin damage and changed no answer;
- its favorable value-only contrast shrank about 8.3-fold under P;
- regional and key/value contexts did not provide coherent corroboration;
- the decisive norm-matched placebo was unavailable;
- natural calibration was adverse; and
- the fixture was an explicitly resolved, imported-replay case rather than a
  native agent trajectory.

The internally consistent N/R2 value-only movement remains worth reporting as a
weak mechanistic hint. It is not large or identified enough to overturn the
stop rule. Claude Fable 5 independently judged stopping defensible, and an
independent GPT-5.6 Sol adversarial review recommended stopping unequivocally.
Sol adopts the stop as final for this research cycle.

## Why not spend a few dollars on a quick repair

The missing placebo cannot be validated against e01's exact rows locally. The
committed raw artifact preserves hashes, shapes, traces, and scores, but not K/V
tensor values. Recreating them requires a GPU forward. More importantly, a
scientifically useful repair is not one more e01-like cell: it requires new
controls, several untouched cases, a behaviorally sensitive readout, and
unambiguous rules. Rushing one cheap rerun would repeat the ambiguity rather
than resolve it.

The exact historical execution/packaging/audit bytes are now tracked under
`scripts/historical/coherent_canary_v12_e01/`. Future runs must additionally
persist a bounded tensor bundle.

## Conditions for reopening paid mechanism work later

Paid work should reopen only under a separately reviewed and funded
preregistration that already contains all of the following:

1. One declarative signed stop rule from which prose, runner, validator, and
   adversarial wrong-sign tests agree exactly.
2. A constructive bf16-valid negative control whose **applied** perturbation
   profile is matched and whose availability is demonstrated before treatment
   scores count.
3. A prospectively justified adequacy window where full history is competent,
   compaction causes real damage, and a sub-nat movement could affect the
   constrained or free behavior rather than remaining 22 nats underwater.
4. At least four untouched, outcome-blinded cases, including both explicit and
   unstated decisions. E01 is permanently excluded.
5. Value-only R2 as the narrow surviving hypothesis: FF, FC, FW, sham identity,
   and multiple label-blind placebo directions under both N and P. Avoid a new
   family/region garden.
6. Bidirectional semantic criteria: C moves toward C and W toward W relative to
   fresh; correct-target likelihood improves; unchanged controls stay stable;
   the effect exceeds both placebo movement and schedule shift.
7. Early stop after two cases if placebo magnitude is comparable, either
   direction fails, N/P disagree materially, or behavior never moves. A
   four-case success would require at least three directional/selective cases
   and behavioral recovery in at least two; no rescue extension.
8. A bounded safetensors evidence bundle containing the fresh destination and
   all source rows needed to reconstruct every tested arm boundary.

Only a replicated, placebo-separated, schedule-robust, behaviorally observable
result would justify a later paired live-agent feasibility evaluation. Current
evidence does not justify running one.
