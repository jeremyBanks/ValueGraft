## Main developments

- The Transformers 5.0.0 loader correction now recognizes the Qwen3 MoE
  conversion from 18,867 checkpoint tensors to 531 packed runtime tensors while
  requiring the frozen 48-layer × 128-expert × 3-projection layout, parameter
  conservation, exact shapes/dtypes, conversion order, and three bit-exact
  packing sentinels. The first 30B attempt had loaded weights successfully but
  stopped before any forward pass because the original attestation assumed
  one-to-one topology.

- The runtime RoPE fingerprint was corrected to read
  `config.rope_parameters["rope_theta"]`, matching the pinned library’s
  deserialization behavior. The frozen geometry, model identity, thresholds,
  inputs, interventions, and outcome rules were unchanged.

- The third local technical run reached Qwen3-0.6B inference but hit the frozen
  64-token cap without normal EOS. The original code discarded generated/forced
  branch evidence before comparison; the amendment now persists both branches,
  independently evaluates generated/forced equivalence and strict identity,
  records an ordinary `FAIL`, and continues later diagnostics. Regression
  coverage passed 31/31 and the focused v12 suite passed 162/162.

- The local cap result was judged a noninferential proxy limitation, not
  evidence of cache inequality or a reason to retune the fixture. Because
  equivalence, repeat identity, and all other plumbing conditions passed or were
  valid-and-adverse under preregistration, the result permits only the bounded
  exact technical gate. It does not authorize Phase A, treatment, semantic
  release, or claims.

- Two identical RunPod Secure Cloud requests exposed materially different host
  drivers: 580.159.03 allowed CUDA initialization and model loading, while
  550.90.12 made pinned CUDA 13 unavailable before download. This confirms that
  container and GPU labels alone do not define a reproducible environment. The
  second attempt also exposed a separate commit-interlock defect involving an
  incorrectly expanded SHA.

- The exact launch review added nonzero failure enforcement, fresh-raw-artifact
  checks, explicit receipt pointers, hash-verified pull and
  commit-before-shutdown behavior, actual API cost monitoring, and a new
  immediate authorization binding the final launch commit. No paid pod was
  provisioned for this launch review; paid v12 compute remains $0 in the
  disposition note.

## Durable decisions and clarifications

- The exact gate remains uniformly strict: normal EOS, bit-exact repeated runs,
  generated/forced equivalence, complete identity evidence, and all other
  preregistered technical controls. The local exception is governance-only and
  cannot weaken validator code or exact-subject eligibility.

- The authoritative local advancement evidence is the validator pair
  distinguishing generated/forced equivalence from full identity; fragile runner
  labels or advisory flags are insufficient.

- The effective runtime must include weights, tokenizer, source code, Python
  packages, compiled CUDA libraries, physical GPU, host driver, and execution
  backend. Future GPU admission must require A100-80GB PCIe identity, at least
  80,000 MiB, driver ≥580.65.06, and `allowedCudaVersions=["13.0"]`, with
  immediate termination on mismatch and no unfiltered fallback.

- Provider selection is a methodological qualification task. An AI
  recommendation may nominate a provider but cannot substitute for independent
  checks of reproducibility, host filtering, provenance, artifact egress,
  billing, observability, and failure handling.

- Evidence durability applies before strict predicates and before downstream
  branches. Remaining exceptional paths that can discard partially built branch
  records should be reviewed.

## Empirical status and caveats

The local proxy showed no normal-EOS path on the real model; normal-stop
bookkeeping is covered only by deterministic fake-model tests so far. The exact
30B topology, sentinel checks, forward pass, generated/forced identity, semantic
comparison, and treatment outcome remain unobserved. The loader and runtime
suites passed their focused regressions (including 19/19, 158/158, 162/162, and
165/165 reports), with only recurring SWIG deprecation warnings; these tests do
not substitute for exact-model execution.

The confirmed driver drift explains at least one paid infrastructure failure and
may have amplified diagnostic churn, but it does not explain previously isolated
checkpoint, fixture, cache-position, or counterfactual-history defects.

## Risks and next actions

- Verify that the admission interlock, CUDA-build choice, fresh authorization,
  and final launch commit are all present in the repository before provisioning.
- Perform at most the bounded exact technical attempt under the qualified host

  strict cost monitoring, and no automatic fourth attempt after another

- Pull and hash all artifacts before termination; preserve failures as durable

- If the exact attempt fails, stop for a fresh design audit before further paid

  semantic or treatment action follows automatically.
