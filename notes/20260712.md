- Formal v12 stayed terminally stopped under the literal preregistered
  path-control rule: the first unsigned-measurable ULP-2 edit moved in the wrong

  remains one engineered case, diagnostic-only, placebo-uncontrolled, and
  nonauthorizing. Its best value-only N/R2 movement was small relative to

  exactly. This demonstrates deterministic reproduction of one fixed computation
  across the two observed hosts, not an independent sample, causal quantization
  effect, or efficacy result.

## Persistent decisions and terminology

- P01/P02 are not additional semantic cases: the distinct-case count remains
  N=1, e01. Repeats test execution stability, not statistical replication.

- “NF4 versus bfloat16” means bundled eligible-linear weight representation and
  kernel/runtime differences. Both regimes used bfloat16 KV storage; no four-bit
  KV-cache claim is licensed.

- The safe scientific distinction is between compaction damage, forced
  log-probability movement, and behavioral/task recovery. A positive margin can
  arise from suppressing a countertarget; it is not recovery unless the correct
  target improves under the preregistered interpretation.

- The paper must preserve four evidence strata separately and must not pool
  their sample sizes, procedures, controls, or inferential status. The SWE-Gym
  selected-map movement remains a small, descriptive likelihood result with task
  overlap, no matched placebo, no executed action, and no task-success endpoint.

- Repeated exact outputs across hosts or runs establish fixed-fixture
  reproducibility only. They do not establish general hardware invariance,
  population validity, semantic transfer, quantization causality, or agent
  benefit.

- The correct description of the e01 placebo outcome is “missing control
  evidence,” not a null placebo result. The construction failed systematically
  at the target dtype/geometry.

## Empirical observations and caveats

- e01 compaction damage was approximately 22–24 nats, while graft movements were
  much smaller. The bf16 and NF4 regimes both left the intended focal answer
  unrecovered; correct- and wrong-source grafts were nonselective, and all
  primary generations remained wrong.

- The favorable-looking e01 value-only movement was schedule-sensitive and
  region-incoherent. Token-level analysis showed that the alternate schedule’s
  small phrase-average included a wrong-direction first target token offset by a
  conditional second-token movement.

- The NF4 fresh-answer anomaly and its graft-induced flip reproduced exactly
  across P01/P02, but all grafts produced the same wrong output and the matched
  placebo was unavailable. This is a reproducible runtime-specific behavioral
  pattern, not semantic recovery.

- Legacy source-stratification showed materially different signs between
  4B-rendered and assistant-authored conversation blocks; this is corpus/source
  heterogeneity, not a causal nativeness result. The legacy per-head map’s
  evaluated intervention is preserved, but its claimed selection lineage is not
  reproducible from committed profiles.

- SWE-Gym cluster recovery showed that task/repository keys were recoverable
  from the ignored parquet and that task overlap crossed the fitting boundary.
  Cluster-sensitive intervals remained above zero, but the result stays
  descriptive and control-incomplete. Fixed-scalar estimates also varied across
  post-hoc hash halves at a scale comparable to the selected-map movement.

## Outstanding risks and next actions

- No further paid run, P03, formal-v12 reentry, e02–e06 expansion, live-agent
  pilot, or repaired-placebo rescue is authorized for this cycle. A future study
  requires a new preregistration, independent cases, constructive
  production-dtype controls, preserved tensor bundles, schedule-matched arms,
  and a powered confidence or equivalence analysis.

- The project still lacks a statistically confident upper bound on graft
  magnitude. The handoff explicitly identifies the goal-versus-trajectory gap:
  one scored semantic case and substantial unused current-phase budget despite
  the original request to maximize robustness and confidence. This is a
  documented process failure, not evidence that the scientific question is
  settled.

- Remaining repository work includes final Codex/Luna accounting qualification,
  review of two stale historical test assertions, and completion of deferred
  transcript-duration repairs. Any publication or handoff should preserve the
  distinction between committed exact totals, lower bounds, counterfactual
  valuations, unknowns, and attribution gaps.
