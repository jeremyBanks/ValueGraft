## 2026-07-12 — retire the failed route

- The powered-v13 remote release/lifecycle system is retired. Its failed cloud
  setup is evidence, not authority. No additional pod or lifecycle repair is
  part of the active experiment.
- Reuse is limited to scientifically useful CPU components: the deterministic
  eight-stratum recipe/frame, target proofs, carrier prompt, and the state-only
  V-row placebo constructor.
- The subject is the locally cached 30B-A3B MLX 4-bit conversion at its exact
  resolved revision. Claims name that precision/backend and do not borrow bf16
  or full-KV scope.

## 2026-07-12 — primary design

- Governing design ID: `coherent-state-local-mlx-n48-v3`. V1 closed before
  treatment on its carrier prompt/filter. V2's only Phase-A candidate was
  treatment-blind and is excluded unconditionally after revealing that
  inherited Dmargin and nonfocal-prefix gates mismatched the absolute correct-
  target endpoint. V3 was frozen before any further forward pass.
- Primary unit: one paired C/W conversation fixture. Fixed N=48, six from each
  of eight logic strata, selected in a fresh outcome-blind order from the
  32,768-member recipe frame.
- Each fixture has two fixed externally authored carrier-text conditions. Their
  measurements are averaged inside the fixture; they are not random renders or
  N. Per-condition effects are reported as fixed-effect sensitivity evidence.
- Primary outcome is correct-target mean-token-logprob movement from fresh
  compaction B to correct-history value graft E. Correct and wrong targets,
  margin, oracle damage, render difference, wrong-history specificity, sham,
  and placebo movement are mandatory companions.
- Primary bounded unit value is the mean of the two render movements clipped
  to `[-0.5, 0.5]`. A one-sided upper bound answers the requested negative
  question; the symmetric lower bound is used only for a positive claim.
- Primary treatment is value-only because MLX cached keys are post-RoPE while
  values are position-unrotated. No local full-KV claim is attempted.

## 2026-07-12 — controls and validity

- A fresh-value sham through the exact assignment path must be bit-exact. The
  real long-canary gate passed before treatment.
- Wrong-history values forced under the same visible carrier are the semantic-
  specificity control.
- The deterministic state-only v13 V-row constructor supplies the applied
  nonsemantic placebo after mapping its two classes to this design's two
  aligned visible regions. It may inspect state geometry but never a target,
  logit, continuation, or treatment outcome.
- Candidates are selected by frozen rank after blind text review and
  treatment-blind oracle/fresh validity. Failures advance to the next frozen
  rank; no text or threshold is repaired after selection starts.
